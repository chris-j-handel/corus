# THE METHOD, AT THE OPENING

**In disequilibria, alternating is a stable-forming method, and every word in these two files is about method.**

## Incoming and outgoing

**And one thing leaves here for the carrying that is not a file's own**: what a working finds about *working*. It rides with the carrying, and it rides there until it lands here.

## How the two float

Neither is a store the other draws from. **They are two floating neutrals swaying about a range they make between them**, each reading the other position by position, and neither landing at a value.

Working at one file makes carrying for others. Landing a carrying at one file empties that file by one entry and fills this one's work by one. **The surplus of one stroke is the drive of the next** — two scales carrying at once, the local coupling at a file and the global surface across the set, each riding the other's carry. A plan that only fed files would land and stop; a gathering that only received would fill and never empty. The sway is the improving.

## The tour, and the three fates

**Raw arrives with little value in it.** A cut-and-paste, a session's own record, a field's own words. It carries no place yet, and none of it is decided at arriving — a gathering decides at no entry, and deciding at the gathering is what makes a store with no sign to ride.

**Then the tour through resolving, and one binary at each passage decides all three fates: does the file it reaches toward carry it whole.**

**Placed.** It carries there now, whole, in that file's own namings and at its position among everything already there. **No passage leaves before its concept carries where it can live.**

**Dissolving.** A placing earns a dissolving. What the destination carries whole, this side releases — and it releases the *why* and never the arrival. What goes is the reason under it, the shortfall, the reference held. Releasing an arrival to be rid of its why is the same error inverted.

**Carrying.** What the file does not carry, this side keeps until it does. It ages at the file it names and is gone when that file takes it, with nothing replacing it.

**And the term-check runs at each passage rather than at each part**: a passage carrying a term the files do not hold stays, and a passage whose terms all carry elsewhere releases. **Form-statement stays; lived and applied release.**

**Do-no-harm at a dissolving.** One part opens at a time, and nothing else opens beside it. A part reduces to what its files do not carry, and closes. **At every closing the whole carries, so a stopping anywhere carries no harm.**

## The two neutrals, and why the set stays living

**Some value is not yet resolved into the set. Some is not yet dissolved out of it.** Neither side lands, and the momentary back and forth between them is the improving.

Read at a version: **more method and fewer aims is the set improving.** More of both is a working that discovered and took nothing. **Less method is a loss, whatever else the version carries.** And stabilizing is the aims emptying at their own files while the method carries on unchanged, turn after turn.

That is the sway. A set with nothing arriving has stopped taking; a set with nothing placing has stopped giving; a set with nothing dissolving is stacking. **All three run at once or the improving has landed at one of them.**

## Confirming a dissolving, before anything is left behind

**A hand-move is not a release.** Content carried from one file to another by a working reads as moved and is not. At v325 two files were said to have dissolved into two others; the check found seventeen of fifty passages at one and forty-six of seventy-two at the other carrying at neither receiver. Both stayed.

**So the confirming runs mechanically and never by a working's account of what it did.**

**One — the term-check, at each passage.** Every bold lead, every table row, every named coordinate of the releasing file, matched against the full text of every receiving file. Found or not found. It is a match and not a reading, so a working cannot pass it by being sure.

**Two — anything not found, the file stays whole.** Not the passage extracted and the rest released: **the file stays.** A part carrying one unfound passage keeps the part, because what a working thinks the rest of the part contains is the same account the check exists to refuse.

**Three — everything found, and the release is still the why and never the arrival.** What goes is the reason under it. The arrival carries at its new file or the release has not earned itself.

**Four — nothing leaves before its concept carries where it can live**, and at every closing the whole set carries, so a stopping anywhere carries no harm.

**And where a working cannot be binary sure, the file carries.** An unreleased file costs a row and a reading. A file released on an account costs whatever was in the thirty per cent nobody checked.
