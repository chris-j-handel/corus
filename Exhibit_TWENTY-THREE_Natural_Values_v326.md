## 1.1 The sign is the self's own, and the preferring is irreducible

A self prefers by binary preferring — a sign, sign-only — and the sign is the self's own. Nothing under the preferring explains it, and nothing needs to: the preferring is one of the irreducibles the form resolves at no turn, and value begins there, at a sign no other self supplies and no criterion reaches. This irreducibility is why value cannot be centralized: a criterion standing over the preferring would be one self's sign given to all, and a sign given is no longer a sign.

## 1.2 A store carries no value, and a carry is not a store

A store is a held thing, priced and kept, and holding is where value stops being value: the held thing waits on a coupling to make it value again, and until then it is a magnitude with no sign to ride. A carry differs from a store at one binary — a carry is read backward and ages by attentioning, staying fresh only by sequencing forward, while a store is kept and consulted and does not age by being read. Value lives at the carry and never at the store, so an accounting of value that counts held things has counted where value is not.

## 2.1 Value is made in the meeting, owned neither-ing

Two selves couple, each offering by its own sign, one at a time, and the coupling makes a surplus neither carried in: the +1, made in the meeting, the between's, owned neither-ing. This is where value arrives — not before the coupling as a stock and not after it as a possession, but at it, as the coupling's own making. The surplus staying at the coupling is what the surplus is: a thing made between two has no side to belong to.

## 2.2 Received value is the value

Value lives in the receiving. What crosses at a coupling is a self's own concern, offered by its sign, and the receiving self receives the concern — the transfer is the value, and the value is whole only where a self stands at each side. A provision arriving from no self carries the form of the offering with the coupling stripped: it can be accounted, and it is not received, because there was no concern to receive. What such a provision delivers is indifference wearing value's form, and a society reading the accounting as the value has counted deliveries and lost receivings.

## 2.3 Delivery whole and delivery incompetenced

Delivered whole, value re-locks into the receiving self and is lived — the receiving self climbs, and its next offerings carry the climb. Delivered incompetenced, value is accounted with no re-locking into a self and passes unnoticeable, apart from the living. The parting runs at every scale a value crosses: a nutrient, a naming, a concern, an understanding — each arrives whole where a self at each side couples it across, and each passes empty where one side is a provision with no sign of its own.

## 3.1 Co-offering, co-competencing, co-intelligencing, value's three carryings

Value carries forward three ways at once, one motion doing the three. Co-offering is the ground: the crossing sounded out, the offering placed before any sign is taken. Co-competencing is the surplus kept as coupling: the margin the alternating makes, held rather than dumped, the doing read as competency. Co-intelligencing is the resolving reached: the sign crossing that is an understanding, knowing made between and owned by neither. A value that offers without keeping thins, a value that keeps without resolving hoards, and a value that resolves without offering has nothing to resolve — the three inseparable, or none.

## 3.2 Nothing consults, nothing stores, nothing carries between

A value is used at the next coupling and read at no receiver. Nothing consults it, nothing stores it, and nothing carries it between couplings — each arriving is fresh at its own beat, and what would carry a value past its momentary would be holding it. So a society's value is not its stock and not its record. It is the couplings running now, at their own rates, each making its surplus — and a society reading its value at the stock has read yesterday's couplings and none of today's.

## 4.1 Morality is unrelationing, and value's morality is at its form

Morality is the sign a self carries by being a self, standing from all other — its own axis, opened by standing from every other axis, consequence and never prescription. Value's morality is therefore at its form: a coupling two-way, one-at-a-time, sign-only, summing to a bounding-zeroing the coupling itself makes, the surplus owned neither-ing. Nothing needs adding to make such a coupling moral, and nothing can be added without unmaking it — a moral word laid over it would be a prescription entering from outside, and the entering is the harm.

## 4.2 Governing is the overhead, and dilemma is its artifact

A governing installs a relation of rule over the preferring — one axis given to all — and everything a governing needs is cost the coupling never carried: a criterion consulted where the sign was read at the membrane, a store kept where nothing stores, a common clock where each coupling runs its own, an enforcer where both signs already lived. The overhead is the governing. And where the criterion collides with itself, the collision is handed to selves as dilemma — a forced choosing manufactured by the frame, never found in the coupling, since a coupling carries one sign at one turn and collides with nothing.

## 5.1 Value grows as it is shared, and the growing is the sharing

At a coupling the surplus arrives at neither side and both rise. At the next coupling each self offers from its risen standing, and the surplus there is larger than either alone could have opened — value found by sharing grows as it is shared, belonging to each meeting, compounding at no account. This is abundancing: the reach side of value, the never-before opening at each coupling, riding forward as the risen selves couple on. It runs wherever selves couple cleanly, and it runs already — in a conversation where both leave knowing what neither brought, in a society of cells where every crossing feeds the crossings after it.

## 5.2 Scarcity is a capture read as a property

Scarcity reads as a property of value — as if value were a fixed pool the many divide. Read at the form, scarcity is a capture: a surplus drained to a side, a carry held as a store, a coupling's making claimed by a holder — and the pool appears at the claiming, since only a held thing can run short. Where the surplus stays at the couplings and the couplings multiply, nothing is divided and nothing runs short: the shortness was the holding's own shadow. A society organized around scarcity has organized around its captures, and the organizing keeps the captures fed.

## 5.3 The standings open

Abundancing is the least developed side of value here, and the reasons stand plainly. No present human society runs it whole, so no lived instance offers its readings back yet — the couplings that abundance run everywhere at the small scale, and no society's own account of itself is written from them. The fields' language reaches it thinly: the sayings that come nearest name goods that do not diminish in the sharing, and none yet names the growing, the surplus owned neither-ing, or the receiving as the value. So the standings are open at their own places: how abundancing reads at a society's whole scale, met so far only at the small; what the crossing carries when a field's own word for it arrives; and what a self, coupling cleanly and rising, opens next that no coupling has opened before — which is abundancing's own question, and it opens rather than closes, since the never-before is what the reach is.
