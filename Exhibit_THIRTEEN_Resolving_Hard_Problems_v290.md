## Figure two — the four arms and the perpendicular fifth

The hashtag laid flat and tilted, seen as a plane: four arms, two one way and two the other, the four boundings, the balance whole (libra, the scales, whose abbreviation lb scribbles into the #). On its own the plane can only be tilted from outside, off self-stilling. The center dot floats on eight equidistances: four corners on an outer ring, the diagonal reach, and four side-centers on an inner ring, the side reach, the two radii in the ratio of diagonal to side, the dot reaching all eight equally and owned by none, the bounding-zeroing. The fifth rises perpendicular through the dot: the axis the plane lacked, φ the rate the four beat at, φ² = φ + 1, self-cohering, the self-stilling self-orienting centerline, the floating-neutralling forward-recursioning self standing at right angle to its own balance. Four in the plane and the fifth through the center is the self stabilizing in five; the dot is where the perpendicular self pierces the plane, so the center is perpendicular to the self and the self perpendicular to the four. This couples to the origin of weighing: the equal-arm balance is two arms and a fulcrum, and it grows a second orthogonal pair, the suspension and the plummet, exactly when it must read its own level, the four-arm cross the elaborated balance reading itself true at the right angles the plummet makes with the beam.
