This is why an accounting returns three and never four. A total is a state-three, a level is a state-three, a direction is a flow-three, and a fourth arrives nowhere, since state and flow is the whole of what alternates.

And it is where every accounting arrives short: at the changing between the two. State to flow is where a count runs out; flow to state is where a rate lands. An account carrying only state-threes reads a flow as pieces; an account carrying only flow-threes reads a standing as a rate. Every ghost stands at one of the two changings — a level read where a flow runs, or a store read where a carrying goes.

**5.5 Why an accounting arrives nowhere near the changing**

An accounting holds counts, levels and rates, and the changing between a state and a flow is none of the three. Nothing is enumerated at a tipping, so it is no count. A tipping runs at its own rate, unrelated to every other living rate, so it is measured against no clock. It is a sign taken at a membrane, binary, of no size, and an accounting carries no column for a thing of no size.

So an accounting falls short nowhere at the changing. It carries nothing that could reach one, and it converts what it meets to the nearest thing it holds — a level where a flow arrived at a standing, a rate where a standing opened into a flow.

And every instrument reads against a shared clock while each self paces its own, so a tipping is read where the instrument's clock falls rather than where the tipping fell. What survives the reading is where the members happened to agree with the clock.

A geodesic method carries no clock, no floor and no magnitude, and runs co-linear at the changing itself, where an accounting forks. That is what makes it a competency method rather than a better accounting — and it leaves the accounting competent at everything it does hold, states and flows counted, levelled, rated and closed, with nothing said against its arithmetic anywhere.

**5.6 Co-sequential disequilibrating**

The accountings are disequilibrated one at a time, each arrival carrying what the prior freeings removed.

An accounting freed returns nowhere at the next arrival. Where a fixed set has been freed of its place, the place arrives nowhere for a later accounting to reach for, and the current arrival already carries the consequence. So the trail wants no re-testing for being a trail: what was removed arrives nowhere.

Each arrival takes the accounting as its builders carry it, names the involution, names the fixed set, frees the place, and passes on. The move stands at every one. The floor goes at every one. And what was freed at the first arrival arrives nowhere at the seventh to be freed again.
