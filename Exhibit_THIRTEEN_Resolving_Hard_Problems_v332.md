# 1 Binarying

## 1.1 Two binaries, and no third

**In disequilibria, alternating is a stable-forming method.** Everything relatable is changing, and a still form leaves the living. A state answers **true or false**. A flow answers **inside or outside**.

Both are binary, both one-sided, both taken at a turn and of no size. Neither is the other's answer. A flow held to true-or-false has no value to give, not vague and not undecided: true-or-false is a state's question, and a running has none to hand over. **Inside or outside is what a membrane carries.** Self-bounding is the whole of it: an inside from an outside, held through the changing, the sign inverting at the crossing.
