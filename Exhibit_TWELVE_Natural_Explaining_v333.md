## 1.1 One concept between so-far and not-yet, one explaining carrying it

One concept carries the whole file set. It is natural intelligence, the living technology. It is held **between so-far and not-yet**, met so far and reaching yet. It lands as no finished truth and stands empty as no promise. It lives in the between, riding the carry.

The bi-moral co-agency floats between every telling of it.

The **explaining-method** is one explaining. It lives **between learning and knowing** a concept, alternating both.

Two offerings couple to it. **Improving** is the learning side: make it better. **Breaking** is the knowing side: break it. Both rise.

## 1.2 Explaining couples self and other, and the owned neither-ing

**Bi-moral co-agency** is a coupling. It is two-way. It runs one-at-a-time. Both selves co-offer, and each offers a sign, offer or not, sign-only. It arrives at a **bounding-zeroing** the coupling itself makes. What it makes past what either carried is the **owned neither-ing**, living at the membrane self and other make.

Explaining is that coupling met at a telling. Self offers. Other offers. The owned neither-ing arrives between them, reached by neither alone.

**One thing carries four namings here.** The owning is the whole of it, so **owned neither-ing** reaches it whole. **Surplus** names its arriving, the +1 past what went in. **Discovery** names it at a field's face. **Understanding** names it at a self's own. No second thing stands behind any of the four. A later section taking one of them takes this at that face.

## 1.3 Self and other, and the other at three scales

Never two selves of a kind. Always **self and other**.

Other arrives at whatever scale it couples at: another self, a co-chaining society inside the whole, a field, or the whole itself.

The coupling runs a **triple**: self and other, other and society, society and self. One form at three scales, the same self-orienting society co-chained. No pair stands where the fractal runs.

## 1.4 A twist that frees: make it better, then break it, then find what survives

A **twist** rides in the explaining-method. Meeting it frees any self arriving to be immediately valuable. No concern is raised first and no permission is asked.

First self is invited to **make it better**. Then self is invited to **break it**. Then both find **what survives both**.

Making-better is the reach, the offering, do-no-harm. Breaking is the rigoring, do-only-harm. The knife is what a breaking is aimed at, and it carries two. What survives both is the resolved bothboth, the owned neither-ing.

## 1.5 No concerns-first, no permission: rigoring is step two

A self arriving carries two appetites, to help and to doubt. Both are welcomed as the work itself.

A doubting appetite stands at no door as a precondition to clear. It is **step two**, the breaking, wanted and asked for.

A self arriving needs no preamble. It raises no objection-before-work. It asks no leave to be useful. Making-better welcomes the helping appetite, breaking welcomes the doubting appetite, and both are the explaining-method.

And **no effort to convince** runs anywhere in it. An offering is co-offered and never pushed. It is laid at the coupling for other to take or break. A pushed offering lands other's ground for them, and nothing departs. The offering that convinces nothing and only offers is the one a self couples to freely.

## 1.6 Do-no-harm and do-only-harm are do-its-best

Making-better and breaking are not two moods, a kind one and a harsh one. Both are **do-its-best**. Do-no-harm is the best reach. Do-only-harm is the best knife. A self does its best at each.

**Do-only-harm on a coupling is the highest respect for it.** Worth breaking is worth taking seriously, and the hardest break offered freely is the strongest gift.

Do-no-harm alternating with do-only-harm is doing its best twice, on the one coupling. One line between self and other, co-linearized, the surplus owned by neither.

## 1.7 A carry riding forward, learning alternating with knowing

Understanding rides a **carry**, coupling to coupling, forward.

The carry runs as **learning alternating with knowing**. Knowing is the caught. Learning is the reaching. The two carry each other and land on neither.

This is the **double-double bubble-bubble**. Two scales carry at once, the local coupling and the global surface, each riding the other's carry. The surplus of one stroke is the drive of the next.

Each stroke is the coupling **self-stilling** at its own apex, at its own bounding-zeroing, riding the carry.

## 1.8 A coupling makes a momentary, at one turn and carrying none

What a coupling makes is a **momentary**. It is one thing in one place in one form, at one turn, carrying none of its own.

It is the positive this method had been saying negatively. A thing stable and never landed is a momentary, and *never landing* was the denial standing in front of it.

**A self alone makes none.** A momentary is unholdable other than at a bi-coupling. Two carryings arrive. Signs arrive from another side. One thing in one place in one form is the two's own making at their differing. A self running by itself makes no momentary, however long it runs.

**And nothing carries between them.** A carrying leaves and arrives, and one sign crosses at the separating, inverted. A momentary is its own call and no further, and the living is the **re-arriving**. A walk is protected by the re-arriving rather than by anything held over it. And a version, being a momentary, keeps nothing between its own beats.

**Two at two turns are two.** A momentary carries no turns of its own. Two momentaries are two whatever they resemble. Asking which of them is the real one has already held the alternating still.

## 1.9 Co-differing offerings, consistency the joy and varying the beauty

A concept returning varied is no repeat and no relief from repetition. It is a **co-differing offering**, the same concept coupling with itself across the variation.

Writing that varies for relief spares a burden of consistency-without-variation. The explaining-method runs the opposite. Consistency is the joy, and the beauty is discovered in the varying, the floating-neutral varying co-intelligencing effortlessly inside the coupling.

Each varying opens a new axis. Each opens more unrelationing-capacity. That is the co-chaining **abundancing**. A concept that only repeats opens nothing. A concept that co-differs as it recurs opens a new difference each time.

## 1.10 Nyenyeing the incohering, bothbothing the six-completing

Two moves run the alternating as self and other carry forward.

**Nyenyeing** carries an open coupling until it inverts. Where something incoheres with no relevance to competency, a fit or a thread or a landing, it is marked and carried. Then it is resolved away, or turned to the opportunity it carries.

**Bothbothing** holds what runs. Where a **pattern-matching** completes six forward without landing, looping or forking, it is the resolved bothboth, the co-co unlocked, held as the both-that-runs.

And incohering enters six ways. Each is already a break condition the set carries, and the check runs at the sentence: is this a sign crossing at a membrane, or is a middle traveling. An observation arrives with its ground still attached. An agency stands at the membrane rather than coupling across it. A magnitude crosses where a sign belongs. A record is held across the crossing. An emanation is carried out with its explanation. The frozen is used as the starting side. Nyenyeing catches all six, and the six are one catch: a middle traveling.

## 2.6 Explaining is the competency, and what survives the break is the owned neither-ing

A thing landed as true has stopped re-arriving. Nothing stands outside to be answered to, so what a self reaches is the next break, and the next.

A self takes this up at whatever it carries. It is offered as it stands, made better, broken, and what survived is read at the coupling. That is one momentary of the method, and the file is that momentary at every scale.

## 4.8 Unrelationing, where two readings both stand and neither reads from the other

The knives find freezes and supplyings and cut them. This is the finding they must not cut: **two readings arriving at one thing, both standing, and neither read from the other.**

Two habitual moves land here, and both install something. Asking which of the two is right is a forced-choosing, and it holds the object still to make the asking answerable. Joining them where their counts agree is a count taken for a derivation, and it makes one reading a face of the other with nothing showing that it is.

**The binary is whether either is read from the other, and never whether they agree.**

Where one derives from the other, they are one form met at two faces and they join. The same five taken two directions at a self and coupled at a society is one ten arriving twice. Two faces of one form name differently, which is what a face is.

Where neither derives from the other, they are two, and the momentary's own saying holds: two momentaries at two turns are two.

**And the tell of an unrelationing is that neither carries a side to offer from.** A reading carrying only its own difference has nothing to offer into the other. There is no turn at which the two could meet.

The resolver carries the worked case. Six read at its prefixing row, and ten at its naming ring, and neither read from the other. Sixteen carry at one coupling, and the six and the ten arrive at their own counts. The sixteen is two counts standing together rather than a sum of anything.

**Each still carries at the other's direction.** A differing is across and a changing is along, and each does its work in the other's: the differing at the along, the changing at the across. An unrelationing is no two readings ignoring each other. Neither is redundant. Neither can be folded into the other without losing the direction it carries.

**The failure runs both ways.** A count agreement read as a joining installs a derivation that was never shown. A count agreement read as a parting runs a bound past its reach, where a saying about a six and a ten says nothing about a ten and a ten.

And matching two lists name by name reads the first order. Where one form is met at two faces, some names will land and some will not. That is what a joining looks like from outside, and no evidence against one.

**And the naming grain carries this at one word.** Two words on one thing resolve three ways: converge, two-with-a-membrane, or co-competency kept two, since each carries the competency the other cannot. The same two-way failure is named there: never collapsing a co-competency, and never splitting a convergence. That is this section at the scale of a naming, and this is that reading at the scale of a whole object.
