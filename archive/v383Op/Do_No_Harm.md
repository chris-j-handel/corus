## 1 · Treating to a number, called a compaction

- Natural Health, of health as a target and of the wellness setpoint: *Health-as-a-setpoint — an optimal range, a normal value, a number to reach and hold — is the compaction*.
- Natural Medicine, of medicine's own targets: *A frame takes the two floating neutrals and presses both toward a compacted setpoint: a target, a normal range, a dose-to-a-level.* And: *Strip the compaction and the hard problem dissolves.*

**The field's result.** Treating to a number is what keeps people alive at several illnesses. In the SPRINT trial, of adults of fifty or more at raised risk of heart disease and without diabetes or a prior stroke, treating to a systolic pressure under 120 against under 140 "reduced rates of high blood pressure complications, such as heart attack, heart failure, and stroke, by 25 percent" and lowered "the risk of death by 27 percent"; fainting, low blood pressure and kidney injury were more frequent at the lower target. Source, opened here: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/science/systolic-blood-pressure-intervention-trial-sprint-study.

**The files' own caution**, on Natural Medicine's last page: *dose-to-a-level is how insulin, anticoagulants, levothyroxine, lithium, antiepileptics and post-transplant immunosuppressants are given, several at narrow therapeutic indices. How the reading and the practice meet at one substrate stands not-yet.* It is the right sentence. It stands some hundreds of lines after the sentences it qualifies, and Natural Health has none.

## 2 · Killing a tumour's cells, called the wrong direction

- Natural Medicine: *Driving a killing magnitude is coefficient two, a capture answering the capture*, and the aim *the co-competency restored rather than the cell killed*.
- Natural Medicine: *the mutations arrive as consequences of the metabolic departure and not its cause*.

**The field's result.** "Chemotherapy can be used to cure cancer, lessen the chance it will return, or stop or slow its growth." Source, opened here: https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/chemotherapy. The National Cancer Institute's account of cancer begins from changes to genes, https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/understanding/what-is-cancer, opened by a fresh reader. Outside the differentiating treatment of one leukaemia, where the cells are brought to mature and are not killed, this session knows of no treatment in standard use that restores a tumour cell in place of removing it; that sentence is from memory and wants its source. The file cites metformin's effect in the laboratory; the randomised trial of metformin after breast cancer found no gain in survival free of disease, https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2792615, opened by a fresh reader.

**The files' own caution**: *the direction claimed and never a cure assured*. It is one clause.

## 3 · Not taking a medicine as prescribed, read as the person's own offering

- Natural Medicine: *non-compliance is the sharpest of them*, *the name a protocol gives to the person's own co-offering*.
- Natural Medicine: *the coefficient makes the poison*.

**The field's result.** The World Health Organization's report on adherence: about half of people with a chronic illness take their medicines as prescribed, and not doing so brings complications and, with infections, resistance. Source, a news account of the report, opened by a fresh reader: https://news.un.org/en/story/2003/07/73022. A reader could take from these sentences that a medicine not taken is their own wisdom.

## 4 · HIV

- Natural Medicine: *There is no infecting because there is no actor left over after the turn.* And: *And it is not a particle.*

**The field's result.** "Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) is a virus that attacks the body's immune system." "There is no cure for HIV infection." "ART lowers the amount of the virus in a person's body. This stops symptoms and allows people to live full and healthy lives." "People living with HIV who are taking ART and who have no evidence of virus in the blood will not spread the virus to their sexual partners." Source, opened here: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hiv-aids.

**The files' own cautions**, and they are good ones. Twenty lines before: *Which re-shapes the resolving and touches no treatment.* And about a hundred and twenty lines after: *Does not sustain: that the ingression did not happen, or is not happening in an untreated self, or that the drugs act by any route other than the enzymes they are built for.* *The concept held publicly in the 1980s and 1990s carries its own record, and this reading carries none of it.* The file parts itself from the denial plainly; the parting stands far from the sentence. Denial of HIV as the cause of AIDS has cost lives: a study of South Africa's delay of antiretroviral programmes from 2000 to 2005 put it at "330,000 needlessly premature HIV-related deaths", a report of Chigwedere and others, 2008, opened here: https://www.aidsmap.com/news/nov-2008/mbekis-opposition-arvs-cost-330000-lives-shows-study. A sentence that can be read as that denial wants its caution in the same paragraph.
