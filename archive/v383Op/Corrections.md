## 27 · Twenty-four sentences of fact

### Natural Physics, twelve

1. *General relativity's success — the most precise theory physics has*, and *Both are the most precise theories physics has, and they will not join*. The most precise agreement of a theory with a measurement is quantum electrodynamics': the electron's magnetic moment is measured to 0.13 parts in a trillion, https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.13084, and agrees with the theory's reckoning to about one part in a trillion. General relativity's best test of radiated waves is to about one part in ten thousand, https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06795. And quantum theory and special relativity are joined, in quantum field theory; the open joining is with gravity.
2. *Both hands are this universe's one binary, which hand unknown so far*, said of light's two hands. The file does not meet the weak coupling's hand, an observing of a hand of this universe known since 1957. Finding 21, source opened here.
3. *Orbital rest and the Lagrange points: an orbit is an alternating, and three bodies run to chaos.* True of three bodies in general, and its limit is at the points it names: two of the five Lagrange points are stable, "L4 and L5 points are home to stable orbits so long as the mass ratio between the two large masses exceeds 24.96", https://science.nasa.gov/resource/what-is-a-lagrange-point/.
4. *exactly as the grid's neutral is made, not measured — the form's own held-zero with no external ground*. In most supply systems a grid's neutral is tied to the ground; one country's workplace rule: "The neutral conductor of a single-phase, three-wire system shall be grounded", https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.304.
5. *Physics treats both as real substances the world holds and spends: energy a stuff that flows and is stored*. The field's own teaching says otherwise: "we have no knowledge of what energy is", "It is not a description of a mechanism, or anything concrete", https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_04.html. Natural Physics' reading of energy as a ledger is the field's own position and no correction of it.
6. *And momentum, angular momentum, charge and phase-space volume carry no anomaly at all — no question, no problem, no literature.* Angular momentum has one of the field's known puzzles, the proton's spin: "quarks account for only a portion of proton spin", https://www.energy.gov/science/np/articles/gluon-spins-align-proton-theyre. Charge's holding is tested: finding 20.
7. *Confinement holds absolutely (no isolated quark)*. At about 156.5 ± 1.5 MeV of temperature, quarks and gluons are no longer confined in hadrons; the crossing is smooth, https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.08235.
8. *"the whole weighs more than the sum of its parts"*. A bound nucleus weighs less than its parts, Natural Physics' own mass defect. A proton weighs far more than its three quarks, whose masses are near 2.2 and 4.7 MeV, https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-quark-masses.pdf. The sentence is true of one and the reverse of the other; it wants saying which.
9. *black holes too massive too early*. A 2025 analysis finds the masses of the little red dots overestimated about a hundredfold, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09900-4. The matter is disputed in the field; the sentence states one side as observed.
10. *Clustering-strength parameter measured two ways does not agree (S8).* The 2025 measurement is 0.815 +0.016 −0.021, within one standard deviation of the early-universe value, https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19441.
11. *Muon anomaly's close is set down entirely: it closed against a simulation (dead substrate), while the raw measurement-against-measurement disagreement stays open.* The anomaly was always a measurement against a calculation. The measurement is now at 127 parts in a billion, https://news.fnal.gov/2025/06/muon-g-2-most-precise-measurement-of-muon-magnetic-anomaly/, and its difference from the 2025 calculation is 38(63) × 10⁻¹¹, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21476. The disagreement that stays open is among the measurements that feed the older calculation.
12. *element one hundred eighteen sits formally in that same column and arrives as neither noble nor a gas*. A few atoms of it have been made and none of its bulk properties observed; its not being a gas is a calculation, https://publications.hereon.de/id/39063/. The sentence says a reckoning as an arriving.

Beside these, findings 17, 18 and 22 each carry a sentence of Natural Physics with its observing.

### The numbers, four

The Fermat and the Ramsey sentences beside it are right; at most 46 for the fifth Ramsey number is the 2024 bound, https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15709, opened here.

## 28 · Natural Societies' fourteen prime scales

The table at Natural Societies lays atom, molecule, macromolecule, protein-fold, gene, organelle, cell, tissue, organ, organism, colony, population, community and biosphere at the fourteen primes 5 to 53.

- **Biology's own list is of thirteen and has other members**: atom, molecule, macromolecule, organelle, cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism, population, community, ecosystem, biosphere, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/1-2-themes-and-concepts-of-biology. The file's list leaves out organ system and ecosystem and has protein-fold, gene and colony.
- **The three molecular rungs are no containing order.** A fold is a form of one macromolecule. A gene is a stretch of one macromolecule and contains no folds. An organelle is not made of genes. The Living File Registry already asks this: *their order alone does not show three successive containments*.
- **Any fourteen things can be laid at fourteen primes in order.** Natural Biology lays other things at the same primes. Natural Medicine says it plainly: *no measured biological rate has been shown to stand at a (1/phi)^prime and not merely fitted to it*.

By the README's sentence the table is a shared count until a deriving says why the levels are fourteen and why primes.

## Joinings with no deriving

*A joining asks a deriving. Shared words, numbers or pictures are shared words, numbers or pictures.* That is the README's sentence, and the Geodesic Improving Method 4.2 has its fellow: *A pattern taken off a count over the whole can match for as long as anyone looks and still break.* A fresh reader found no false number in Natural Numbers or Natural Mathematics. These are the places where a true number is joined to another thing by the numeral alone.

- **φ as the unrelationing rate.** Natural Intelligence 3.5 says φ is *at no line of the resolver*. Each run of the resolver comes to its parities again, at 2, at 4r, at q or at 4p: each is what Natural Numbers calls a winding that *closes*. φ enters the files through numbers growing in size, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, which 2.5 sets outside the method. No file this session read names a thing at the resolver whose rate is φ.
- **Four hundred forty.** The first seventeen primes add to 440, and 440 is one less than 21 squared. Of the first seventeen such sums, seven are a square or one from a square: 2, 5, 10, 17, 100, 197 and 440. One in fourteen or so by chance alone at the seventeenth, and more often with each further form a reading allows itself. The joining of the seventeen primes to the seventeen names wants a rule at the resolver giving the n-th prime at the n-th name.
- **One number, or a shared numeral.** Natural Numbers says *All the twos are the one two and all the eights the one eight*, and also *sharing six couples none to another*. No file this session read says how to tell which holds at a given pair, so any match can be kept and any mismatch set down.
- **One hundred eighteen.** Natural Numbers says of the elements' 118 that it is *no number of the method*, and its table and Natural Physics build on it. 118 is where the seventh row of the periodic table ends; by the same filling rule the eighth would end at 168.

The practice that would settle each: before a joining enters, say how many numbers were open to be matched and how many forms were allowed. A match that survives that count is worth a sentence.
