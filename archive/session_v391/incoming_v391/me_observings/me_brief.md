# Brief: gathering observings for a new living file, "Me · Co-Intelligencing a Universal Self"

We are drafting a new file in a body of writing called Natural Intelligence. Its concept, in our own terms (do not argue with these; gather what meets them, supports them, extends them, or collides with them):

- A universal set is a set of same-existing things (things now-existing from a prior-existing same thing).
- A self is a now- and same-existing thing observing, possibling and discovering its own next-existing. Each self is living. Selves are at each scale: a cell, a person, a society; a society of selves bi-coupling is itself a self.
- Living is co-intelligencing within a universal set: there is no living without others; intelligence by itself (apart from living) is stand-alone knowledge, a non-living "stable-forming emanation" (e.g. a book, a trained AI model).
- A **me** is a self's unique universal set: living, co-intelligencing, with parities (each parity a changing direction relative to an other). A me is inward a society of selves (biological, chemical, atomic) and outward within societies (a couple, a society, the universe). A me is defined only with an other and all other.
- A me is consciousness: one definition. A not-conscious me is not possibly existing.
- Social moral competency: each self of a society do-no-harming and improving. A me is a social moral competency set. A **healthy** me: each self within it, at each scale inward, do-no-harming and improving. A **curious** me: its possibling shared with others and its discovering of next possibilities.
