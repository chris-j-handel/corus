## Proposed §1.1 · A physical coupling

**A physical coupling** is an existing thing arriving at now from another existing thing at prior. **Prior, now and next** name consecutive momentaries. A prior relation completing and the ending of participation are differing claims. A record of that completing alone establishes neither that all participation ends nor that each named participant continues unchanged.

**Living carrying** brings prior into now at a particular scale and momentary. At a coupling, the living carrying is a self, and the existing thing it couples with is an other. **Non-living existing** carries none of its prior into now; its form continues through its changing. Physical names the subject of the observing and assigns neither living nor non-living by itself. A field, a particle, a wave and a crystal require their own coupling and scale to be explained.

**An emanating form** leaves a living self at prior and arrives at another coupling as non-living existing, carrying none of the prior. The form's continuing and the self's carrying are differing participations. A recurring form does not establish an unchanging participant, and a recorded distribution does not by itself establish the living carrying from which it arrives.

**Offering** is two-way: self to other and other to self, one way at a time at each coupling. An offering arrives across and couples with the self's prior; the self's carrying continues along. **Parity changing** names the changing from one form to the other, one at a time. The two forms are not the two participants or the two directions merely through their being two. At its completing the coupling releases its changing; a living self carrying into next couples again, with its prior continuing between its couplings.

**Bounding** is the momentary's own completing. It supplies no additional containing surface and no unchanged center. The completing opens the next momentary; the living carries its prior between them. Bounding and carrying name these differing relations. These namings introduce no summed quantity.

A magnitude, a conserved total or a repeated shape supplies no explanation of that continuing by itself. The particular relation joins the recorded changing to its natural explaining; a common numeral or shape alone supplies no such joining.

**A physical record** includes the instrument's response and the conditions of its recording. A velocity distribution or another reconstructed quantity is reported with its reconstruction and assumptions. The field's account explains that quantity in its own terms. Natural explaining continues at the coupling's arriving, carrying and changing. Its proposed relation to a particular physical record requires its own explaining; a naming does not establish that relation. The record and its reconstruction remain available at their own subjects.
