## 5. THE FORM

**Best:** 1.3 ¶3, "**The offerings are where incoming value rises toward the file, and the carryings name what the file rests on.**" One sentence, both mates, each at its one verb. Close beside it, 1.3 ¶2, "two mates at each file, its offerings and its carryings, each a file of its own at `carry/`", which gives the address; and 1.3 ¶4's opening, "**At the offerings a value arrives at the bottom as a droplet**, raw, in its dropper's words, tagged with its session, nothing refused and nothing an authority. Resolving moves it up into an insertlet, one thing whole, aimed at one section and one sentence of the file, said in the file's own naming", which gives droplet and insertlet at their difference.

**Least clear:** 1.3 ¶4, "The file's motion meets each insertlet is or is not, and it leaves the offerings either way, entered or not, the file's carrying of it released by its offerings in the same motion." Four pronouns and participles chained ("it leaves", "the file's carrying of it", "released by its offerings"), the word "carrying" at a fifth sense, and the sentence says where the insertlet goes at "is" (the file) and not where it goes at "is not". A second candidate: "The front, Living Improving Value, says the set's now and no file's section", which a reader cannot place at all.

**Would a reader meeting Part ONE once know:**

- *That each living file has two mate files at `carry/`?* Yes, from 1.3 ¶2 and ¶3, said plainly and twice. Then 1.6 ¶2 would take it back: "the rest rides at its section of the carrying". A reader meeting Part ONE once meets both and holds neither.
- *What a droplet and an insertlet are?* Yes, from 1.3 ¶4: a droplet is raw, in the dropper's words, tagged, at the bottom; an insertlet is one thing whole, aimed at one section and sentence, in the file's naming, tag gone. The reader would not know who or what does the "Resolving" that moves one up (the working? the file's motion? a reader?), nor that cohering with Exhibit ONE is required, since the file never says what Exhibit ONE is.
- *What the file's motion does with an insertlet?* Partly. The reader learns the motion meets it "is or is not"; at "is" it enters and is the file's own sentence (1.4: "the insertlet entered, the file's own sentence now") and the offerings release it. At "is not" the reader learns only that it "leaves the offerings"; where it goes, and whether that is a release to the archive or a droplet again, is said nowhere in Part ONE. The reader would also not learn that the motion writes anything at the carryings: 1.6's three parts of a motion ("The file is written, the carrying releases the file's taking, and each finding ... is placed here") have no part for naming what the new sentence rests on, though 1.6's new sentence says the carryings carry exactly that.

So the two mates and the two droplet-forms are said well enough to be met once and kept; the motion's "is not" branch, the carryings' part in a motion, and the surviving "section of the carrying" are the three places the form is not yet whole at Part ONE.
