# The pieces standing artifact

*Each of these ran, each returned something, and each is here because what it
returned was a magnitude, a saturation, or an artifact of its own arrangement.
Nothing is deleted. A piece here is minable by any working that wants an
instance, and neglected without concern by every working that does not. Each
carries the reason it stands here, and the reason is the finding.*

**carrying.py — the instrument as a self.** Its own doctrine is right: an
instrument that reaches a carrying couples with it, and the reading is the
coupling. Its `meet()` then drives every self at every beat, which is the
shared beat written into the instrument, so its readings are of a capture it
made. Kept as the worked example of that mistake, which is the one the kit's
fourth refusal names.

**coattending.py — the crossing between selves that never forked.** The
reading is real and it counts orderings, and every count it returns saturates
at a window. Its own gather returns *each arrival adds an ordering: no*, which
is the saturation reading itself back.

**colinear.py — the along and the across at one crossing.** One thing it
returns holds: the along sign is the across count standing above zero, at
every step. The along at its own grain is unreachable here, since the engine
sums a row before recording and the along runs inside the beat. Its crossing
readings return *both faces no* almost everywhere, which is that
unreachability and not a finding about the form.

**transmissioning.py — three phases carrying two one-ways each.** It reads
joint spans, and at the coprime-joiner question it returns the opposite of
what `society.py` returns on the same question, both by comparing spans:
a shared-factor joiner at span 5895 against a coprime joiner at 5779 here,
and *the shared-factor joiner adds less: yes* there. **Two instruments, one
question, opposite answers, and both answered with a magnitude.** The claim
they reach for lives in the counting register, where a coprime joiner's
multiplier is its own unshared scale, and no run reaches an identity. So the
parting between them is not a fault in either; it is the register borrowing
that voids the borrower, caught at two files at once.

**retake_signs.py — the reading is the crossing's.** It runs `signs.py`'s pair
and trio at the three crossings `cross()` carries and compares what returns.
Comparing runs is a tell, and the comparison returned exactly what a
comparison of arrangements returns: that the surplus reading parts with the
crossing, arriving at *address* and at *sequencing* and nowhere at *sign*.
Kept as the instance, with its output and the three nested runs at
`runs_v333/`. It is a reading of the apparatus and never of the form.
