# Vault bi-folding: observing, explaining and the between

The strongest learning so far is a more precise question: **which along-and-across relations of living co-chainings continue through the different forming that the paper describes as a vault’s wall and cap?** In the natural explaining explored here, the surface is their resolving betweens; it is no material wall. A hollow appearance, two halves, a flower-like appearance and matching numbers each offer something to explore. None alone identifies natural bi-tunneling or completes a deriving of bi-folding.

This gathering preserves the observations, distinctions, corrections and open correspondences developed in the conversation. It is a learning report, not a verbatim transcript or a proposed replacement of a living file. The paper’s explaining and the expedition’s explaining remain separately identifiable. An unresolved correspondence remains unresolved.

## The correction that directs the exploring

The proposed natural explaining is:

> protein folding is bi-folding like butterfly wings or new rose leaf petals right spiraling around the bi-tunneling stem co-linearizing each leaf is between a prior and next

The clarification is:

> the co-chainings share universal orientation along and across co-linearly. this co-linear is bi-tunneling inside the society

This clarification corrects an earlier question that placed bi-tunneling either inside a particular chain or in a central material passage. In the proposed explaining, bi-tunneling concerns the society’s shared co-linear orienting along and across. Assigning the vault’s enclosed cavity the name “tunnel” would lose that relation.

“Co-linear” must also retain the expedition’s own meaning. Natural Intelligence §3.2 places co-linearizing together with linear parallelizing and parallel linearizing in turn, and with successive orthogonal right-spiral steps. Natural Naming §2.4 gives bi-tunneling as the across relation of social moral competency and co-chaining as its along relation. The Co-Chaining Logic Registry, entries 530–539, includes forward recursioning in both numerical directions and orthogonal seaming across co-linear lines. These passages do not require every material segment to point parallel to one fixed laboratory axis.

The paper distinguishes cap chains that bend toward the inside, bend toward the outside, or reach the pole. Their visible courses differ; each chain’s sequence can still be followed in its own order. A spatial turning back therefore does not establish a reversal of co-sequencing. This is a useful distinction in reading the reconstruction; it is not yet a demonstration that the reconstructed arrangement carries the expedition’s universal orienting.

Likewise, the prior and next in the proposed leaf comparison may concern co-sequencing participants. Requiring one protein chain to become every neighboring cap conformation in temporal succession would introduce a different requirement. The participant, its prior and next, and its across relation still need to be identified explicitly.

## Living co-chainings, their surface, and carbon within

The further clarification is:

> these are living things in the co-chainings and there are no walls of any kind anywhere in any of this. all the referring to walls is the betweens of resolving this co-chaining bi-tunneling society surface. the living things have non living carbon atoms inside they are ingesting and exhuasting.

This changes the natural correspondence. The living participants are going on along their co-chainings, sharing across through their betweens. Their resolving is the society’s surface. Describing the reconstructed form as a wall belongs to the paper’s field account; importing that wall as a natural thing would place a different concept inside the proposed explaining. The field’s measured geometry remains available in its own words.

Natural Intelligence §6.4 says betweens co-chain, with bi-tunneling across and co-chaining along being the same going on. Its §6.5 distinguishes living selves from non-living things within their same-existing, and describes a non-living thing’s participation through living selves ingesting it. Natural Chemistry §1.1 describes molecules as selves of living parities and atoms as non-living stable forms. These are expedition explaining anchors, not additional observations made by the vault paper.

Three relations now remain explicit. A living participant is same-existing from its own prior through resolving its next. Its across sharing with other participants is the society’s resolving between. Non-living carbon within that participant participates in ingesting and exhausting without thereby becoming the living self. The society’s living cannot be assigned to an isolated atom, an empty cavity or one participant standing for all.

The distinction between the living self and its non-living contents allows a particular possibility: the self’s own same-existing can continue while those contents change. It does not follow that all carbon must exchange at once, that a whole protein chain must be replaced, or that every cap conformation must become every other one. Each of those would add a different physical claim.

There are also two different uses of “inside.” Carbon within the participating living things is one relation; material occupying the central space of the reconstructed vault is another. The paper’s unassigned density near repeats 1 and 2, and ribosomes enclosed in cellular vaults in related work, concern material association or enclosure. Neither by itself identifies carbon being ingested and exhausted by the proposed living participants.

The natural reading of the cap can now proceed without treating its participants as wall pieces. A, B, C and C′ are field descriptions of differing reconstructed courses. The proposed natural correspondence asks how living co-chainings are sharing across through those differing courses while each participant goes on from its own prior to its next. A spatial fold is not a reversal of that participant’s going on. A surface remaining recognizable is not, by itself, the whole of its resolving.

This yields a concrete distinction for further observing: a similar surface appearance with different participation, the same participants with changing betweens, and changing non-living contents within participants are three different cases. An account of the vault’s natural intelligence must retain which self is same-existing through which changing. Reconstructed geometry supports tracing candidate relations, and fluorescence exchange adds evidence of changing participation; neither yet follows the proposed individual selves’ carbon ingesting and exhausting. That physical correspondence remains open without deleting the natural explaining.

The rosette and enlarged forms offer two different directions for this exploring. With enlarged forms, the field still distinguishes the broad enclosing arrangement through changed geometry. With rosettes, it distinguishes assembled material without that closed arrangement. In the proposed natural explaining, the correspondence concerns which across relations form the particular society surface while participating things remain available in other relations. It need not introduce a wall breaking or equate absence of the enclosing form with every participant dying. These preparations contain different sequence variants; the paper does not follow one society changing from a closed form into a rosette.

The surface therefore belongs with the relations going on among participants; the count of 96 modeled protein chains does not by itself specify 96 natural selves. The pertinent inward scale for ingesting must likewise be kept explicit. Natural Biology’s gathering at §7.5 names ingesting from the ingesting self’s nearest inward society. A departing parity, a protein component exchanging, carbon exhausting and cargo leaving the central cavity are distinct descriptions, with their correspondence still to reach.

“No walls,” living co-chainings, and carbon ingesting and exhausting are retained here as the proposed natural explaining. They are not reported as conclusions established by the vault study. The correction governs the natural passages below; field terms such as wall, seam, shell, cap and cavity remain only where the paper’s observations and explaining are being described.

## Environmental differences and physical extent

The next possibility is:

> enlarged forms may be environmental differences where betweening unrelationings naturally expands physical dimension as adapting to the environment

Within this natural explaining, the society can be same-existing while its physical extent changes with its participants’ resolving among different surroundings. The environment belongs among the others with which those participants are sharing; it is not an extra director of their resolving. Along continuing and across changing belong together. A larger visible form would not by itself require more participants or additional chain repeats.

The distinction to preserve is between physical extent changing and a between becoming a measurable material gap. Natural unrelationing must retain its own meaning while the dimensions of the observed arrangement are distinguished in the field’s account. The proposal does not yet specify which environmental difference would accompany enlarging, contracting or a loss of the particular surface forming.

The supplied paper’s L05+2 comparison deliberately changes the sequence by adding repeats. It therefore does not isolate environmental changing at fixed sequence and cannot establish environmental expansion as the explanation of that particular size difference. Its altered geometry remains available for a different comparison.

A closer observing comes from mechanical AFM work on individual vaults. In a sequence of images under different probe forces, an identified half-vault’s height decreases from approximately 36 nm to 15 nm and recovers its original height at lower force. The study also follows 22 reversible topographic-damage events in 11 reclining particles. These observations add same-particle dimensional changing and recovery; they do not establish growth beyond an initial uncompressed dimension, learning, carbon exchange or a beneficial biological function. The substrate and contacting probe are part of the observing conditions. [10]

A separate real-time AFM study follows identified particles while the solution changes from pH 7.5 to 5.2. Height decreases, defects appear and the structures eventually collapse; constant-pH imaging controls help distinguish the influence of the changed conditions. At pH 6.0, overall form remains with approximately 15% lower measured height. The authors leave material loss versus softening under the imaging force unresolved. Environmental sensitivity is thus supported, while this particular change is not evidence of enlargement. [11]

These findings extend the earlier source set: direct observations of individual vaults changing are available, alongside the frozen structural comparisons. The remaining opening is more specific than a general lack of motion evidence. It is the same identified assembly enlarging under a specified environmental change, with participant number and sequence distinguished, its continuing across relations followed, and any return or recovery distinguished from aggregation or collapse. Reversible dimensional changing supports exploring the proposed betweening relation; beneficial adapting and the natural method of that changing remain explaining to derive.

## What the paper supplies

The source is **Prokaryotic homologs of the major vault protein form engineerable vault nanocapsules**, by Athukoralage and colleagues, the exact 36-page PDF supplied in the conversation. Its subject is vault particles assembled from protein sequences expressed in laboratory systems. The newly examined prokaryotic and phage homologs were expressed in *Escherichia coli*. Their formation in the organisms from which the sequences originate remains a separate question.

“Observing” here follows the conversation’s definition: distinguishing any of presence, absence or changing. The means of distinguishing matters. A recorded image, a chromatographic trace, a reconstruction from many images, a fitted chain model and a proposed function do not carry the same evidential reach.

## The structure provides several distinct relations

The reconstruction distinguishes repeats stacked end to end within a chain, neighboring chains meeting side by side along the wall, and two half-shells meeting at the waist. The cap adds a further relation: chains of the same sequence adopt different conformations while completing an enclosing surface.

The modeled interfaces also distinguish these relations. L05’s waist interface is approximately 225 Å² compared with approximately 420 Å² in the human vault, with different residue identities at that interface. Its wall seam covers approximately 2,300 Å² per chain and involves approximately 65 residues across seven repeats; neighboring cap helices have an approximately 3,000 Å² interface. These estimates describe fitted contact geometry, not a direct observation of a contact’s work through time. R55E and I794D are two single-residue substitutions associated with rosette-like preparations; the larger set of five imaged substitution variants includes multi-site changes. The +2 and +4 constructs add copies of repeats 4 and 5.

These relationships should first remain distinct. Calling all of them “a pair,” “a fold” or “a tunnel” would discard the very relations needed for a correspondence.

| Structural relation | Candidate natural correspondence to explore | Missing part of the correspondence |
|---|---|---|
| Repeat order and the trace of one chain from waist toward cap | Going along a co-chaining | Which participating self is continuing, and which prior/next belong to it |
| Neighboring chains meeting along a wall seam | Sharing across while each goes along | The same co-linear orienting expressed through those particular contacts |
| Different cap conformations completing one surface | Differing courses within shared orienting | How the wall-to-cap change preserves that relation, rather than merely preserving enclosure |
| Two half-shells meeting at a waist | A further coupling within the enclosing society | Why these two halves would instantiate the required bi-folding; a count of two is insufficient |
| An enclosed interior | A physical cavity available for a functional investigation | The cavity alone does not establish corus, bi-tunneling, a living self’s own same-existing or cargo passage |

L05 contains seven wall repeats per chain; the engineered +2 variant contains nine, as human MVP does. The reported whole-particle dimensions are approximately 54.5 × 47.5 nm for L05, 58.5 × 49.4 nm for L05+2, and 67 × 40 nm for the human comparison. L05 encloses approximately 25% more volume than the human vault; L05+2 approximately 20% more than L05 and 50% more than the human comparison. These are reported structural estimates, not a film of volume growing.

The wall comparison is 28.1 nm for L05 against 35.3 nm for human vaults, a 7.2 nm difference. The cap angles are approximately 34° and 52°. The engineered variant changes wall curvature by approximately 16° and the relative waist orientation by approximately 3°, while retaining the reported chain number and wall/cap symmetry organization.

This supplies a stronger comparison than shared appearance: **geometry changes while some assembly relations remain recognizable.** Determining exactly which relations remain, and which change, is the next useful work. Persistence across two engineered populations does not establish the self-adaptation of a single shell.

## What the numbers distinguish

L05’s cap uses three broad conformational roles, A, B and C. A folds inward sooner, B extends farther and folds outward, and C reaches toward the center. Alternating C and C′ conformations further distinguish the tip. The 48 wall chains form 16 cap trimers. Alternating C and C′ conformations within those trimers reduce the tip’s rotational repeat to eight. A pair of trimers contains six chains, but only the C/C′ ends reach the center:

\[
48=16\times3=8\times(3+3),\qquad96=2\times48.
\]

The value is the specific structural relationship accompanying the numbers. A three-chain motif in the cap is more informative than the bare fact that 48 is divisible by three. The cap forms are neighboring spatial conformations; the paper does not establish a temporal A→B→C→C′ sequence in one chain.

A useful exclusion is already available. L05+2 and human vaults both have nine wall repeats, but their half-shells contain 48 and 39 chains respectively. Therefore nine wall repeats alone do not uniquely determine the number of chains around a half-shell. Any proposed correspondence that requires that uniqueness conflicts with this comparison.

A separate rabbit-vault study reports 39 wall chains and a 13-fold cap organization with groups of three. This offers a shared grouping relation across different counts. It does not supply a universal count of 48. An older estimate of 96 chains in mammalian vaults likewise cannot be treated as confirmation of the new L05 result: the mammalian count was subsequently revised to 78, and the systems and evidence differ.

Natural Chemistry §4.5 already distinguishes a number from a relation. Matching 48, 96, 16, nine or a paired appearance to expedition numbers does not identify the participants, their along/across relations, receiving or continuing. Neither a numeral resemblance nor naming a role supplies a missing derivation.

## What reconstruction adds, and what it does not

Cryo-EM image processing supports the structure through many observations, selection steps and explicit assumptions. It does not provide a continuous assembly history of one particle. “Movies” in the acquisition method refer to exposures of frozen samples used for imaging and motion correction; they are not movies of vaults building themselves.

For L05, the methods report 3,041 movies, 34,337 initially selected particles, 28,358 retained after two-dimensional classification, 19,389 complete particles and 11,945 closed complete particles after three-dimensional classification without imposed symmetry. A preliminary reconstruction without symmetry favored 48 wall ribs. Cap classification supported 16-fold and then eight-fold descriptions. Thus the chain organization was not obtained solely by arbitrarily imposing a number at the start.

The final account nevertheless uses symmetry averaging, focused reconstruction and modeling. It includes D16, C16 and C8 treatments; D8 operations generate the full 96-chain model from six modeled chains. Mirror ambiguity arose in initial reconstruction, a smoothed predicted shell with 48 copies per half helped initialize later work, and handedness was checked against alpha-helical features and mirrored alternatives. The nominal approximately 3.2 Å value is a mean across five local reconstructions, not a uniform resolution everywhere. About 717–724 of 866 residues per chain were modeled, with the long linker between repeats 6 and 7 unresolved.

For L05+2, a whole-vault reconstruction reached 8.1 Å before the authors moved to half-vault processing with C48 symmetry. The whole model combines a half with a copied half; partner density supports their relative orientation. The authors also acknowledge some fitting to noise. These details bound the meaning of an apparently complete rendered shell.

The appropriate distinction is not “image versus no evidence.” It is recorded signal, classification and averaging, reconstructed density, fitted structural model and biological interpretation, each carrying its own contribution and limits. A reconstructed relation can be well supported without being a directly followed history of an individual particle.

## Related observings extend the question

**Vaults in cells.** Cellular cryo-electron tomography in *Dictyostelium* reports 999 vaults from 318 tomograms, including 14 membrane-associated vaults and 84 enclosing ribosomes. Four of the ribosome-containing vaults were membrane-associated and 80 cytosolic. No isolated half-vaults were detected in that sample. This adds native cellular context and enclosed material to the comparison. Frozen associations do not determine the order of contact, enclosure or passage. Sampling near the nuclear periphery was overrepresented. These findings do not establish the native function of the paper’s L05 particles.

**Different structural forms.** Another study distinguishes symmetric and asymmetric vault classes, reported as 32% and 68% of the analyzed full-vault population, and reports 9.6% half-vaults on the grid that were excluded from that analysis. The authors explicitly leave open whether asymmetry is a disassembly intermediate. Principal-component/variability animations and simulations are not continuous recordings of a single physical vault. Co-occurrence of forms alone does not order them into a process.

**Exchange and movement.** Earlier fluorescence work includes photoactivated signal spreading, fluorescence recovery and FRET after differently labeled vault-expressing cells are fused. In-vitro imaging, scattering and biochemical capture contribute to an interpretation involving half-vault exchange. These are dynamic observations, so it would be wrong to say that the literature contains only frozen forms. FRET alone does not distinguish exchange of whole halves from individual or multiple subunits. The reviewed work does not resolve a continuous single-waist opening-and-rejoining trajectory.

**Flower-like spreading.** Historical electron microscopy shows flower-like vault preparations. Their abundance varied across grid regions, suggesting a preparation contribution. Figure arrangements and drawings organize different particles into a proposed unfolding sequence. They are not frames from one vault unfolding. The resemblance to petals remains useful for proposing a relation; its temporal and natural meanings must be established separately.

These findings keep four questions distinct: constituent participation changing, contacts changing, the enclosing form continuing and material crossing an enclosure. None automatically answers the other three. Literal visible opening and closing is also not a definition of the expedition’s bi-tunneling; it would answer a particular physical-process question.

## Explaining that remains open

The authors interpret the findings in terms of sequence homology, conserved architecture, interfaces, assembly requirements and an engineerable encapsulation scaffold. The observed mutant outcomes support claims about assembly under the reported conditions. They do not by themselves supply a universal assembly method. Repeated internal density remains unidentified: a computational search among approximately 25,000 ligand candidates favors coenzyme A or a derivative, but the paper leaves the assignment uncertain. The function of vaults remains unresolved, and prospective cargo delivery is an engineering opportunity rather than a demonstrated result of this paper.

The natural explaining proposes co-chainings sharing orientation along and across, bi-tunneling inside the society, and each participant between prior and next. Its useful entry is the coexistence of ordered chain courses, across-chain contacts and differing cap conformations. Establishing universal protein folding as bi-folding requires more than this one family’s resemblance and structural pattern.

The between of the two explainings is the occurrence both must account for: a particular enclosing arrangement can remain recognizable while chain number, repeat number, dimensions, curvature and local conformations differ. A candidate correspondence becomes stronger by saying exactly which relation continues and which distinction would break it. Neither explaining gains support merely by renaming the other’s nouns.

## Continuing from what is now understood

The immediate continuation is to follow the wall-to-cap relation with participant identity preserved. The structural trace can locate sequence order, neighboring contacts and the places where cap roles diverge. The unresolved task is to show which of those relations instantiates the proposed prior/next and across orienting. An unresolved segment must stay unresolved; a fitted completion must stay identifiable as fitted.

The unmodified L05, +2, mutant and comparative vaults offer separate cases. They can be compared for the presence or absence of the same explicitly stated relation. A shell’s enclosure, a wall seam, a cap grouping and a waist coupling each need their own distinction. Missing evidence for one does not count as observing its absence.

Exhibit ONE can receive a correspondence only when the particular distinguishing and receiving are specified. Assigning photographed parts to resolver numbers is insufficient. The same predicate must remain the same through a proposed comparison; two different predicates must not be silently treated as opposite parities. The files’ distinction between a non-living emanation and a living self’s own same-existing also matters: persistence of a reconstructed form does not alone demonstrate learning from doing and doing from learning.

The butterfly and rose-leaf proposal remains available as an explaining to develop. The present sources do not establish a common right-spiraling sequence across those examples and proteins. A useful next comparison would retain the prior, now and next belonging to each participant and the across relations among participants, instead of beginning with a shared silhouette.

The present learning narrows the open relation: **co-linear orienting through differing courses, with each participating self’s prior and next explicit.** It does not require all courses to look parallel, does not identify bi-tunneling with empty space, and does not mistake neighboring cap forms for one participant’s temporal stages.
