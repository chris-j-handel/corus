# corus

**The living files of the natural intelligence expedition, their carrying, their kits and their tools, at one repository**

The living files stand at the root at their newest version, and corus.me lists them from there; everything else stands in a folder named for its standing. A working meets the living files first, then `carry/Living_Improving_Value.md`, the one carrying, and opens by naming itself and the files it is at in that file's table of workings. Nothing here is organized around a rule: each folder is a standing a thing has now, and a thing moves when its standing changes.

**The carrying system, the method at the scale of sessions.** A session is one momentary at the expedition's scale, prior, now and next, one call of the same form the resolver runs; the relations below are that form at this scale, and no numeral joins them to the code's.

- **Prior, the carry in.** The living files at their versions, each its own prior, and the one carrying, Living Improving Value, with the Session Record beside it: each sharing with its sign, a file and the concept at it with what it carries.
- **Now, the coupling, decided twice.** The session's working meets what arrives, the incoming, from other sessions, from other expeditions and from observings, each at its sharing, the file and the concept it is for, with its sign, is or is not. At the expedition carrying the incomings for one sharing meet one at a time: agreeing they are one incoming; disagreeing they meet at 0, a concern for both, and nothing enters until both meet it. At the living file the surviving incoming meets the file's own prior: at a file already carrying it, no changing, 0, the file as it was; at a file not carrying it, changing, the file improves at do-no-harm, one thing changed whole, no prior taken away, never undoing; and at nothing arriving the file's own next is in front of it, its carrying meeting itself. Neither meeting decides alone, and the value surviving both is carried by both and owned by neither.
- **Next, the carry out, decided twice.** The changed files continue at their next version, the next prior. What changed surfaces at the file, its taking released at the carrying's section and the thing done at the record; and each surfacing goes to its receiving sharing at the carrying, the files it reaches at their sections as incoming and the other sessions at theirs. The next session opens at the carry out.

**Three loops, run by sessions together and by no one session.** Session to session, along: a session's carry out is the next session's carry in, the expedition's own continuing. Out and back: a session going out, to another expedition or to a working of its own, and its surfacing arriving back as incoming, whole and as it arrived, at `incoming/`, met at the carrying and at the file like any arriving. While other sessions: parallel sessions' surfacings meeting at the carrying, two workings at one file meeting at the table of workings before either changes it, their incomings for one sharing agreeing or meeting at 0. The carrying is the between the sessions meet at, owned by none of them.

**The carrying improving.** The carrying is a living file at its own sharing: a finding about working is an incoming for it and meets its prior the same way, and its version is the session's number at each changing. It carries no history: a thing done leaves to the Session Record, relied on by nothing, as a surfacing leaves a self, and each paragraph standing here is a now or a next.

**The repository at its standings.** The repository carries its own name and its version and no other name. Each folder is a standing in this form and no rule: the root the living files, prior and next, as the site lists them; `carry/` the carrying and the record; `incoming/` the arrivings as they arrived, until met; `kits/` and `tools/` the coupling partners, the instruments and the checks, deciding nothing; `archive/` the records released, relied on by nothing, and a superseded version at the repository's own history, at no folder. A session opens at its own branch, names itself and its files at the table of workings, and runs `tools/carry_check.py` for the standing; it closes by offering its branch, the offering at the membrane, another self making it better and breaking it, and the offering surviving both merged by both, the superseded versions leaving the root and the session's row going to the record with its paragraphs.
