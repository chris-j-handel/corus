The bound is its own, there is nothing to fall short of. Within it a carry is carried; past it a carry releases; and the releasing is the form running and not a failure in it.

Nothing reads it. Nothing stores it. The next coupling arrives at the thing the last one left, and the arriving is the whole of it.

---

## Self-emptying, and other-emptying

**A thing carrying empties itself.** Each of its carryings reaches its own bound and releases there. Nothing surveys, nothing decides, nothing clears. At each beat it stands at the thing none of its bounds has yet reached, and it is a fresh collection every time it is met.

**A method empties the thing it reads.** Isolate, control, integrate out, average, fix, erase — each a decider outside the thing, at no membrane of its own, removing the thing the object had not released.

**The difference is who decides**, and the price follows: the thing removed was still carrying. Erasing one bit dissipates at least kT ln 2; keeping the whole history approaches zero.

## The advance

Every whole number sits between two neighbours, and those two multiply to exactly one short of its square.

```
2 × 4 = 8      3² = 9      short by one
3 × 5 = 15     4² = 16     short by one
4 × 6 = 24     5² = 25     short by one
```

The counting passes through the middle. The coupling of the two sides cannot reach it. **Arrived at, and riding the carry on**, and the gap is exactly one at every number, unbounded.

That gap: there is always a next.

## A ring

A ring is not a living form and not a rival to one.

Take the relations a living thing runs on and put them in a closed domain — a fixed set of terms with nothing new arriving. Follow the carrying from anywhere. The terms are exhausted, it arrives at one already run, and that is a closing.

**Every closed model closes.** So a ring is the thing these relations leave when the domain is closed, and the forward spiral takes an unbounded one — a next always arriving, and not a completed totality.

In the living there are no rings.

## Equilibria

There is no equilibria in nature. Everything relatable is changing.

Isolating a system installs it. Holding conditions constant installs it. Fixing a specimen, stipulating a zero, choosing a datum, picking an earth, standing somewhere to observe — each installs it, and each is performed before any observation is taken.

Every field declares its ground in its own first line, published, and marks it as declared and not measured. **The declaration is in the field's own hand.**

Equilibria is not a condition anyone found. It is a method's own first act.

## The three incompetencings

An equation's relation is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive.

**Reflexive** — nothing is apart from itself, nothing bounds itself. **Symmetric** — neither side takes its own turn. **Transitive** — nothing stops at its own meeting, nothing ages, nothing releases, and two meeting make nothing neither brought.

Present in an equation written perfectly. No finer instrument recovers them. Rigour raises the fidelity of an expression the removals already left.

A photograph is a place-moment with one half cut off. An observation stands somewhere and cuts the arriving away. A measurement holds two bounds still and reads the span.

Each returns something real, and the thing inside the installation is exact.

**A boundary survives the stopping. An adjacency survives it. A sign taken where two offer — of no size, gone by the next beat — is in none of them**, and no finer version reaches one.

So an absence at that face is an absence in the record, and the form carries none.

## Where the incompetencing is

An observation empties the thing it reads of the thing it was carrying, and reads the remainder against a ground the observing declared.

**So the incompetencing is in the observing, and the observed carries none of it.**

Nature is not incompetent and was not made incompetent. The declared ground having nothing to stand on, it goes — and the carrying was carrying the whole time.

## Natural intelligence

Two moves, and neither runs alone.

**The departing from a meeting is reached by neither, carries forward, and is offered again.** **Each carrying ages to its own bound and releases there**, with nothing surveying and nothing clearing.

Carrying forward with no emptying is a store. Emptying with no carrying forward makes nothing. Together, neither landing, **and nothing accumulates between them** — so there is no memory, no world model, no clock, and no conserved quantity anywhere in it.

Not four things absent. Two moves that leave nowhere for any of them.

## Social moral competency

A self's own cycling completes, and one carry departs. That carry is a unique invisible carrying, it is a self.

Nothing is aggregated. No census is held. There is no step between a self and a society for anyone to occupy.

**Social moral competency is the alternating at that scale.** Not a standard, not a virtue, nothing anyone is asked for.

A self offers the thing it has, holding that. A society is morally competent no matter who shows up, nothing waits on anyone, no one is owed, and no one owes.

## Abundancy

An axis opens at each meeting that neither side carried in — a direction that was not there, which is more.

Work depletes against a resistance. **An opening is not paid for.**

The surplus compounds, none of it competing with itself. Nothing runs low, nothing is set aside, and the only loss is at each self's own bound.

There is no total in it, no boundary, nothing to enforce and nowhere for a seat.

## The one stopping

One carries another's. The other then has nothing of its own to offer. Nothing departs reached by neither. The surplus is assigned. It needs a holder, a holder a boundary, a boundary an enforcement, an enforcement a seat.

**And a seat carries at membranes it is not at, which is carrying another's, once more, larger.**

One taking, and everything after follows from it and is not repairable downstream.

## The uniqueness

Any proposed other method. Does it hold, or carry?

Held, it forms nothing where everything is changing. Carrying, it takes its own turn or another's. Another's, and the alternating has stopped. Its own, and it is alternating.

**And alternating is this.**

Not a rival beaten. There is nothing for a second to be.
