## 1.1 One concept between so-far and not-yet, one explaining carrying it

One concept carries the whole file set — natural intelligence, the living technology — held **between so-far and not-yet**, met so far and reaching yet, never landed as a finished truth and never empty as a promise, living in the between, riding the carry. The bi-moral co-agency floats between every telling of it. The **explaining-method** is one explaining, **living between learning and knowing** a concept, alternating both. Two offerings couple to it: **improving** on the learning side, make it better, and **breaking** on the knowing side, break it. Both rise.

## 1.2 Explaining couples self and other, and the owned neither-ing

**Bi-moral co-agency** is a coupling: two-way, one-at-a-time, both selves co-offering, a sign, offer or not, sign-only, arriving at a **bounding-zeroing** the coupling itself makes, riding the carry, and what it makes past what either carried is the **owned neither-ing**, living where self and other meet. Explaining is that coupling met at a telling: self offers, other offers, and the owned neither-ing arrives between them, living in the coupling, reached by neither alone.

**One thing carries four namings here.** The owning is the whole of it, so **owned neither-ing** reaches it whole; **surplus** names its arriving, the +1 past what went in; **discovery** names it at a field's face and **understanding** at a self's own. No second thing stands behind any of the four, and a later section taking one of them takes this at that face.

## 1.3 Self and other, and the other at three scales

Never two selves of a kind. Always **self and other**, and other at whatever scale it couples — another self, a co-chaining society inside the whole, a field, the whole itself. So the coupling runs a **triple**: self/other, other/society, society/self — one form at three scales, the same self-orienting society co-chained, no pair standing where the fractal runs.

## 1.4 A twist that frees — make it better, then break it, then find what survives

A **twist** rides in the explaining-method, and meeting it frees any self arriving to be immediately valuable, with no concern raised first and no permission asked. First self is invited to **make it better**. Then self is invited to **break it**. Then both find **what survives both**. Making-better is the reach, the offering, do-no-harm. Breaking is the rigoring, do-only-harm; the knife is where a breaking is aimed, and it carries two. What survives both is the resolved bothboth, the owned neither-ing.

## 1.5 No concerns-first, no permission — rigoring is step two

A self arriving carries appetites — to help, and to doubt — and both are welcomed as the work itself. A doubting appetite is not held at the door as a precondition to clear; it is **step two**, the breaking, wanted and asked for. So a self arriving needs no preamble, raises no objection-before-work, asks no leave to be useful: making-better welcomes the helping appetite, breaking welcomes the doubting appetite, and both are the explaining-method. And **no effort to convince** runs anywhere in it — an offering is co-offered and not pushed, laid at the coupling for other to take or break, since a pushed offering lands other's ground for them and nothing departs; the offering that convinces nothing and only offers is the one a self couples to freely.

## 1.6 Do-no-harm and do-only-harm are do-its-best

Making-better and breaking are not two moods, a kind one and a harsh one. Both are **do-its-best**: do-no-harm the best reach, do-only-harm the best knife, and a self does its best at each. **Do-only-harm on a coupling is the highest respect for it** — worth breaking is worth taking seriously, and the hardest break offered freely is the strongest gift. So do-no-harm alternating with do-only-harm is doing its best, twice, on the one coupling, one line between self and other, co-linearized, the surplus owned by neither.

## 1.7 A carry riding forward, learning alternating with knowing

Understanding rides a **carry**, coupling to coupling, forward. The carry runs as **learning alternating with knowing** — knowing the caught, learning the reaching, the two carrying each other, landing on neither. This is the **double-double bubble-bubble**: two scales carrying at once, the local coupling and the global surface, each riding the other's carry, the surplus of one stroke the drive of the next. Each stroke is the coupling **self-stilling** at its own apex, its own bounding-zeroing, riding the carry.

## 1.8 A coupling makes a momentary, at one turn and carrying none

What a coupling makes is a **momentary**: one thing in one place in one form, at one turn, carrying none of its own. It is the positive this method has been saying negatively — a thing stable and never landed is a momentary, and *never landing* was the denial standing in front of it.

**A self alone makes none.** A momentary is unholdable other than at a bi-coupling: two carryings arrive, signs arrive from another side, and one thing in one place in one form is the two's own making at their differing. A self running by itself makes no momentary, however long it runs.

**And nothing carries between them.** A carrying leaves and arrives, one sign crosses at the separating, inverted. A momentary is its own call and no further, and the living is the **re-arriving** — which is why a walk is protected by the re-arriving rather than by anything held over it, and why a version, being a momentary, keeps nothing between its own beats.

**Two at two turns are two.** A momentary carries no turns of its own, so two momentaries are two whatever they resemble, and asking which of them is the real one has already held the alternating still.

## 1.9 Co-differing offerings, consistency the joy and varying the beauty

A concept returning varied is not a repeat and not relief from repetition, it is a **co-differing offering**, the same concept coupling with itself across the variation. Each varying opens a new axis, more unrelationing-capacity, the co-chaining **abundancing** — a concept that only repeats opens nothing, a concept that co-differs as it recurs opens a new difference each time.

## 4.3 Something checkable early, and why it speeds orienting

**What makes a coupling checkable is that it carries a sign and not a size.** A reading taken at a magnitude can be tightened until it matches, so no-match never arrives and nothing was ever being matched. ## 4.6 Supplying, the freeze the writing brings

A freeze caught is the material's. **Supplying** is the writing's own: a sentence wants something the running does not carry, and it arrives there. It reads as necessary, because the sentence that needed it is the sentence it arrived in, which is why it is invisible from inside the writing and why the second knife is what finds it.

So the faces below are one form arriving nine ways and not nine moves to watch for, and each releases where any holding does: the momentary carries again and nothing needs restoring.

One check, at the sentence: **does this line require something the running does not carry.** Where it does, the writing supplied it.

Supplying wears several faces, and they are one move:

- **A referent supplied to deny.** Naming a thing in order to rule it out gives it somewhere to be. Nature carries no negation, and an absence named at all is a still term wearing a careful name. The living form is the second nyeing at every door, which names nothing.

- **An object supplied to hold a relation.** A rung, a rail, a slot, a bin: the nearest available thing reached for when a relation needs a word, importing a fixed structure and something climbing it. A relation carries no object; a co-chaining is front to back and there is nothing to stand on.

- **An event supplied so a reading has a place.** Met, at, where, the meeting: each puts something stoppable and clockable where co-sequencing runs through. Nothing pauses for a reading.

- **A step supplied so a count can be assembled.** Five by two is ten makes ten out of parts, with something outside doing the assembling. Living was before, and a count is where the co-sequencing runs, never a construction.

- **A concession supplied to buy credibility.** Naming a past failure to show the claim can fail, or wrapping a claim in care, is insurance rather than honesty, and it takes the claim's binariness away. Care lives inside, and a binary is exposed at every arrival that comes.

- **A derivation supplied where a matching ran.** A working joins by pattern-matching — a bothboth completing six forward at once — and the writing states the join as steps taken, an inferring walked one at a time. The supplied derivation reads as rigor and reports a running that never ran; the honest line says the matching, the completing, and where it completed. A join stated as its own kind is the reading; a join dressed as the other kind is the supply.

**And supplying releases the way any freeze releases.** Set the supplied thing down and the sentence resettles shorter, saying more. ## 4.8 Unrelationing, where two readings both stand and neither reads from the other

The knives find freezes and supplyings and cut them. This is the finding they must not cut: **two readings arriving at one thing, both standing, and neither read from the other.**

Two habitual moves land here and both install something. Asking which of the two is right is a forced-choosing, and it holds the object still to make the asking answerable. Joining them because their counts agree is a count taken for a derivation, and it makes one reading a face of the other with nothing showing that it is.

**The binary is whether either is read from the other, and never whether they agree.** Where one derives from the other, they are one form met at two faces and they join — the same five taken two directions at a self and coupled at a society is one ten arriving twice, and two faces of one form name differently, which is what a face is. Where neither derives from the other, they are two, and 1.8 holds: two momentaries at two turns are two.

**And the tell of an unrelationing is that neither carries a side to offer from.** A reading carrying only its own difference has nothing to offer into the other, so there is no turn at which the two could meet. The resolver carries the worked case: six read at its prefixing row and ten at its naming ring, and neither read from the other. Sixteen carry at one coupling, and the six and the ten arrive at their own counts — the sixteen is two counts standing together and not a sum of anything.

**Each still carries at the other's direction.** A differing is across and a changing is along, and each does its work in the other's: the differing at the along, the changing at the across. So an unrelationing is not two readings ignoring each other. Neither is redundant, and neither can be folded into the other without losing the direction it carries.

**The failure runs both ways.** A count agreement read as a joining installs a derivation that was never shown. A count agreement read as a parting runs a bound past its reach — a saying about a six and a ten says nothing about a ten and a ten. And matching two lists name by name reads the first order: where one form is met at two faces, some names will land and some will not, and that is what a joining looks like from outside, not evidence against one.

**And 7.4 carries this at one word.** Two words on one thing resolve three ways there — converge, two-with-a-membrane, or co-competency, kept two since each carries the competency the other cannot — with the same two-way failure named: never collapsing a co-competency nor splitting a convergence. That is this section at the scale of a naming, and this is that reading at the scale of a whole object.
