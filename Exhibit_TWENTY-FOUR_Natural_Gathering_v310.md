# Natural Gathering

**Apexing, the Whole Carry Gathered**

&nbsp;

---

**1.1 Gathering is apexing, the pair with Discovering**

Discovering is omegaing: the reach, the new axis, the forward frontier. Gathering is apexing: coning back over everything folded in, the whole carry gathered, cohering. The pairing passes the seem-alike knife, two-with-a-membrane, the two cones straddling the un-occupied center, neither alone the living: a discovering ungathered scatters, and a gathering undiscovering folds. The living method is their alternating, told at two exhibits.

**1.2 Gather backward, seat forward, keep no log**

Gathering runs backward over the whole standing carry and seats what it finds forward into the living files, and it keeps no log: no history, no journey, no roster of the done. The published record is the incoming and interpretation is the receiving. The field's own unnamed convergences are received as they stand, and cross-illumination reads each instance through the others. Recurrences are held to watch, out of every claim. Seating forward runs verify-first: an aim arriving often finds its seat already standing, and the sweep that returns the already-held is the gather completing at that place. And a reading worked to its edge at one file carries to its dual's turn, the carry the membrane between two files' workings. The two-ledgers diagnostic is a gathering instrument, and the living-archive laws are gathering's condition: the archive stays open by the gatherer's own open frontier.

**2.1 The two standing instances, the improving report and the registry**

The improving report is the form's first standing instance: the work-side of a gathering whose subject-side is the files, dissolving as each improving seats and reaching zero, whatever aims onward routing to its file before the report goes. And the registry is the second: a gathered set defined by two conditions, stated inclusion criteria and uniform per-entry structure, a form the fields already carry under a formal name with a published assessment apparatus of four domains, protocol and design, standardization, validity of the sampling procedure, and validity of data collection, whose named item is unique identifiers. The two conditions travel together for the reason the form itself states: uniform fields without stated inclusion give a table whose coverage is unknown, and stated inclusion without uniform fields gives a list whose entries cannot be compared, and together they give comparability across sites. The four-domain apparatus stands as an external check any gathering can be measured against by anyone.

**2.2 The four opens and the one closed thing**

A gathering stands open four ways: the subject open, the intake open, the properties and checks open, and every line open at an address. Against them one thing is fixed, the properties themselves, uniform elements being what make two entries comparable, so the schema is closed once, at one place, so that nothing else has to be. This is the general form of any gathering: the openness is the living and the one closure is what lets any two gathered things meet.

**2.3 The subject-and-work split**

An entry carries two kinds of line: what stands about the subject, and what stands about the work done on it. Split, the first is what a later reading takes up and the second is what a later working continues, so an incomplete record of work does not block a reading, and a defect in the subject-lines does. The improving reports read as the work-side, the files the subject-side, and the split is a form-condition of gathering.

**3.1 The corus-extraction, a continuing coupling**

The deep gather reaches the corus for readings left only in the origin telling, and run per exhibit it does not empty in one sweep: each sweep finds more before returning what already stood. The extraction runs as a standing coupling, per exhibit, sweeping until a sweep returns only the already-held, and the sweep that returns only the already-held is that exhibit's gather completing, still open.

**3.2 An available incoming, carrying no authority**

A wide gathering of unsolved statements exists, each said in one plain line, drawing from substrates the exhibits gather thinly: ocean, earth, matter, anomalies, animal minds, language, deep history. Its value is as a well of statements to run pairing against, one at a time, keeping what a pair opens, its framing staying outside. One pair run from it returned a reading: each field reports its own transition as moving and reads the moving as a difficulty in locating a line, and the moving is the finding.

**3.3 A recurrence gathered, and the gather at a close**

One recurrence stands gathered out of the files' own claims: at every substrate worked, the field sought stillness and every vein answers with the sway, dynamic equilibrium half-confessed, allostasis the move off the point, the hedonic treadmill the return, the limit reached only at infinity, the still point sought a sway holding at the physical, the chemical, the living, the lived, the social, the human, and the mathematical scales alike. One finding at seven registers, the fields' own confessions carrying it, standing as the one-axis evidence at every scale.

And a working carry gathers at its own close: what a coupling's carry holds ungathered is swept backward before the carry releases, the value seated forward into the living files and the rest released, keeping no log. The sweep that returns only the already-held is that carry's gather complete.

**3.4 So far, and the open incoming**

So far: the pair stated, the two instances standing, the four opens and the one closure, the split, and the continuing coupling. The not-yet: gatherings not yet run, arriving at their own turns, each received as the incoming it is.
