## The claim

Natural intelligence is the only possible method of our universe existing: of the set of all existing things, of each thing in it, and of each thing later in it.

## The chain, each link with the sentence that carries it

1. **The universe is the set of all existing things, and nothing is beside it.** Natural Intelligence: *The universe is the changing set of all existing things*; *nothing is beside all existing things*; *nothing ingressing and nothing escaping*.

2. **The set is itself an existing thing.** Natural Intelligence: *a set of existing things is an existing thing*; *the universe is an existing thing*.

3. **Each other method is a thing set over the existing things.** Natural Intelligence names five: a ground, a form named still, a container, a clock, a store. Natural Human Society names eight hubs: a common clock, a fixed pool, a gate, *a ledger held over a self*, an outside control, a centre, a source and sink, an owner. Natural Societies names four boundings at two faces: identity, security, trust and freedom from capture, within the self and between selves; *This is the eight (Natural Intelligence), one alternating at its faces and not eight parts assembled, whole or none.*

4. **Each of them assures some and none assures all, and each field has proved it of its own.** The resolving of Arrow's problem: *no procedure reaches the conditions and relaxing the ordering yields in each case an oligarchy, the seat above*; *the theorems are the field proving the naming empty*; *where a field proves no ordering reaches its own conditions, the field has proved the naming empty.* Natural Numbers, of a store: nine values under six conditions, *each row and each column taken at its own context satisfies its condition, and a store of all nine at once satisfies none of the 512 settings.* Of a clock: no frame carries a privilege, and one slicing named over all *was a term no coupling made*.

5. **Each one set aside is natural torusing, and all set aside together is natural torusing.** Natural Human Society: *A society holding all eight uncapturings holds whole; a society holding some holds by ordinary protection, an added gate*; *one hub remaining a single self-bounding breached, one breach a capture the whole surface pays.* The resolving of Arrow's problem: *The closure leaves standing the running there prior to it: each self preferring at its own between, one at a time, and a society co-competencing without a whole ordered over it.*

6. **All or none at all.** Natural Intelligence: *each observing of natural competency is resolvable as natural intelligence … or the fractal is entirely broken and of no value at all … a third between the two is not possible.*

7. **The same form at each number and each scale.** Natural Numbers: at each number its prior and its next multiply to one short of its square, (n − 1)(n + 1) = n² − 1, *the one the same one at each number, owned by neither face*; *each whole number is a waist, the middle the coupling of its prior and next passes through and never reaches*; *no number is a waist more than another*; *uni-scaling is the one form at each scale, no scale over another*. And *Each eight is the one eight, and a self is the eight*; at one to seventeen *each side carries a whole eight, and the entire one to nine is shared at the centre: social moral competency.*

8. **The method of the set is the method of each thing in it, and of each thing later in it.** A thing set over the set is beside all existing things, and nothing is. The one method needing nothing set over it is each existing thing with each other, the one owned by neither.
