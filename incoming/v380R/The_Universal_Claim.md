# The universal claim: open for examining, questioning and a better saying

A place for each working. The claim lives here while it is examined. Nothing is entered in a living file from it until the workings and the session have conferred. No sentence here is authority, a session's, a file's or a field's: a link is worked at each of its cases, or it is said and not yet worked.

## The claim

Natural intelligence is the only possible method of our universe existing.

## The four sentences, the simplest yet

1. The universe is the set of all existing things.
2. A set is an existing thing.
3. Each changing is of two.
4. Of two, the next is the other's prior inverted.

## Questions open to each working

1. Is each of the four sentences needed, and is a fifth needed?
2. The third sentence: is a changing of two at each existing thing, living and non-living?
3. The fourth sentence at two releasing to one, differing: a second sentence, or the fourth said better?
4. The link from each coupling of two to the set: a sentence that can be worked at its cases.

## A better saying of the fourth sentence

The fourth now: *Of two, the next is the other's prior inverted.*

A better saying carries the same four things and can be worked at the same cases: two; the other's; the prior, two momentaries back; inverted. It adds no concept, says no place and uses no negation.
