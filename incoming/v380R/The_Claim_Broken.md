## As it was: the arithmetic

Of the sixteen ways a next comes from a prior and a now, twelve have a pair that comes to itself unchanged, and four have none. Of those four, one goes through each of the four pairs in one going round: the next is the prior inverted. Each reader worked this and each found it true.

## The breaks, the largest first

1. **The resolver is not the one way.** Two selves opening opposite never follow the next as the prior inverted: 0 of 110. They follow the next as the now inverted, one of the fifteen eliminated. A spiral of four does the same. A spiral of three follows the prior inverted at one in three, a spiral of five at one in five. The resolver is the prior inverted only with each self opening alike. A carried parity's next in the resolver is from its now and its arriving; its prior is in no line of it.

2. **The second test is the conclusion written as a test.** *No pair comes to itself unchanged* eliminates twelve and leaves four. The other three are eliminated by *each of the four pairs in one going round* alone, and no link argues for that. Plain alternating, + − + −, is never still and is eliminated by it.

3. **A still is in the resolver.** A self offered its own parity at each momentary never changes: 0 changings in 1,000. And the one way itself repeats its parity at one momentary of each two.

4. **The sixteen are the whole only by premises not said.** Two values; one parity; a next from two and no more; no other thing in it. Worked with each relaxed, more than one survives: 2 of 256 at a next from three; 24 at three values; 6 of 256 at two parities each its own. One of sixteen is the number for that one case.

5. **The living files say the one way of living things, and no more.** *At a living thing, next arrives from prior and now.* *Next as prior inverted is the living step.* The method's break is *a living stable-forming other than this one*. The files' claim for the set of all existing things is another sentence, *Parity changing is the one method of the changing set of all existing things*, resting on *nothing is beside all existing things*. The two are not joined by any sentence.

6. **Existing things outside it, by the files' own words.** The non-living *carrying none of their prior*. *A method is a non-living existing thing.* *A society, a momentary and the universe are three existing and not living.* Colliding, set apart in Exhibit ONE. Each is in the set of all existing things and at no next from a prior.

7. **The set as an existing thing.** Natural Intelligence says *the set is its existing things and nothing more* and *no container added to its existing things*. The claim's eighth link says the set itself an existing thing. And componentwise, the one way at more than one thing goes round four pairs of many, and not each.

8. **Two sentences of the files against the second link.** *Having a stable form is being dead, and discovering next stable form is living.* *Nothing living has a stable form.* The claim says an existing thing is a stable form by the method.

9. **The sixteen are not rules, by the files' own word.** *None is a rule the changing follows.* Fifteen eliminated leave one of the same kind.

10. **No observation is named.** No link says a thing observed that is other if the claim is false. A reader from outside sets beside it things existing and still, a ground state, an equilibrium, a conserved charge; and changing with no two values, a field, a phase.

11. **Two of 256, by the files' own number.** Natural Mathematics: the right spiral step and its other hand are *the only two of the 256 whose square is −1. The square selects no order.* Natural Intelligence: *Which hand is at the momentaries is our universe's one binary, right, the observings all prior.* The hand is from observing and no elimination.
