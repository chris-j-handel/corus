## The largest concern to carry into resolving

**What distinguishes re-forming within a same-existing self or society from the parting of that particular self or society?** The chemical evidence distinguishes changes in membership, connectivity, conformation, phase, shape and activity. None of those changes alone supplies the natural answer across all examples.

The same concern reaches several difficult places:

- The fibre population can grow while individual fibres part. Molecular replication and the same-existing of one fibre are different questions.
- One protein sequence can enter different folds with different partners. The proposed fold scale needs its own resolving.
- A network can exchange connections, and a crystal can carry different local phases while retaining physical integrity. Neither observation alone identifies natural same-existing.
- A catalyst can form an active region before its repeated chemistry begins. Preparation, activation, turnover and deactivation need their own relations.
- “No bonds” can dissolve an additional connector object while leaving binding relations whole. Denying those observings would lose the value being explained.

The binary must address one claim under the same scope. “This measured region has this phase” and its negation form a binary. Different phases in different regions do not contradict one another. Similarly, overlapping classifications do not force an either/or. An unresolved observation supplies neither verdict; that is a limit of our distinguishing, not a third truth value.

A proposed natural explaining gains value by distinguishing the relevant chemical outcomes. If unchanged form, changed form and parting all receive the same explanation without a distinguishing relation, the explanatory concern remains. This session did not establish that the entire chemistry gathering proves the universal exclusivity of natural torusing.
