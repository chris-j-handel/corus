## Findings at the proposal

### Missing and disagreeing offerings remain part of the local changing

The proposal says: “At the offered parity agreeing with the carried parity, changing is not” and “At their differing, changing is.” These are correct cases for an already carried sharing with a nonzero surfaced parity. The next sentence, “Each is the self resolving with the offering,” presents those two cases as the paragraph's whole relation, leaving a carried sharing with no offering, or with disagreeing offerings, without its saying.

The resolver's offering table says “none” and offered “0” surface none, while “+, −” surfaces 0. Its chaining table says that a carried + inverts to − with none or 0 surfaced, and a carried − inverts to + under either condition. Natural Naming's “Naming and the resolver at one changing” says the same: “at 14 surfacing the other parity, 0 or none, the chained parity inverted.” The parting is not an incorrect rule for differing parities; it is the two-case explaining supplied as the local continuation while omitting those other receivings.

Suggested replacement for the central sentences:

> At an already carried sharing, a surfaced offering agreeing with the carried parity carries the prior on with no changing. At the other parity, at offerings disagreeing, or at nothing surfaced, the self's carried parity inverts. Each is the self continuing at its own receiving.

This leaves the heading's positive value intact: is-still-possibling is living continuing, not a stopped self. If the paragraph is to cover the previously uncarried sharing too, give that relation its own sentence: a new nonzero surfacing enters the carrying; disagreeing offerings with no carried prior chain none. Do not silently extend the already-carried relation over that different subject.

### A recurring parity does not supply the receiving relation

The proposal says: “A parity recurring at both carries their coupling.” Its immediately preceding exact fact is different: “At a changing, the released parity and the parity chained as the releasing self's next agree.” The first concerns two selves and their coupling; the second concerns a release and the next prior of one self.

At the source, the nonzero entry released from `_12_bi_tri_bi_entraining` is the entry written into `_11_tri_bi_tri_chaining`. That is the bounded source equality. At the receiver, actual arrival meets its own carried parity and any other offerings. The table's + offering with carried + releases 0 while carrying + on; that same + offering with carried − releases + and chains +. Further offerings can disagree at that sharing. Equality of parities by itself neither establishes that arrival occurred nor specifies the receiver's changing. Natural Explaining's “Two tellings at one thing” likewise distinguishes a shared likeness from the relation joining the two.

Replace the recurrence sentence with its actual relation:

> The released changing arrives at the receiving self's own sharing, and that self continues with its own prior; neither receives the other's prior momentary.

Keep the source equality beside it if useful. Name it explicitly as the releasing self's relation, not as parity shared by two selves. This correction needs no new connector, output, or inspection.

### Quiet releasing is local, and no loss of competency follows from it alone

“A quiet releasing alone says neither that a self has stopped nor that its coupling has lost competency” preserves the right distinction and should remain. It claims neither that quiet establishes competency nor that a quiet source makes a quiet receiver. The earlier two-case explanation makes the latter confusion easier, and the source/receiver distinction can be said positively at this sentence.

The resolver says an offered zero is passed over. If nothing else surfaces at a receiving sharing already carrying a parity, its own prior inverts. A source's no-changing and a receiver's no-changing are therefore different predicates at different selves. Suggested addition:

> A released zero passes over at the receiving self; at a sharing with its own prior and nothing else surfaced, that receiving self inverts its own parity.

This is a source-and-table finding, not a result of an experiment. The society's competency remains at the complete participating relation, as the proposal's last paragraph already says.

### The prior's outward participation is less explicit in the correction

The prior says “differences in carrying can have outward consequences through continued coupling.” The proposal says “A difference in an offering can participate in the receiving self's next discovering.” The second is valuable but changes the subject from the releasing self's carrying to its offering. It does not explicitly carry the first relation on.

The source equality now introduced supplies a bounded way to say the prior's positive value without making the carrying an observable or preparing alternative hidden priors. Suggested continuation:

> The self resolves at its own carrying and releases its changing; that released changing participates in further receiving. Its own carrying continues at that self.

The original distinction between direct transfer and outward participation survives in this form. “Continuing the coupling and inspecting the carrying are different relations” should remain. The proposed privacy language does not claim that no consequence can ever be inferred; the review finds no such overclaim in the proposed section. Conversely, source locality alone is not a general theorem of implementation security. The proposal rightly keeps society's protections at the complete relation rather than crediting a single parity with them.

## Learning about working

A local binary needs its subject beside it: already carrying or uncarried, releasing or receiving, source equality or coupled continuation. An omitted subject can turn a correct conditional into an incomplete explanation without any wrong operation being stated.

A correction can retain a caution while dropping the positive relation that gave the caution purpose. Comparing the subjects of the old and new sentences found the shift from carrying's outward participation to differing offerings; a word comparison alone would not find that loss.

A neighbouring sentence can reintroduce an older model after a local correction has made its own paragraph exact. Here the second sign and the independent quiet/releasing pair belong to that older describing.
