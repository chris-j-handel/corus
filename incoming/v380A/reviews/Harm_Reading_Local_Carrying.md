## Findings and concrete corrections

### 1. Local equality does not establish a recurrence between selves

At 5.6, “At a changing, the released parity and the parity chained as the releasing self's next agree” has a source-text ground: the nonzero changing at 10-bi-tri-bi-tunneling is written into 11-tri-bi-tri-chaining at that sharing. The condition **at a changing** matters; at no changing the released 0 and the continued chained parity differ.

The following sentence, “A parity recurring at both carries their coupling,” changes the subject without naming the two occurrences. “Both” can mean the two selves just named, or the releasing and chaining named earlier. At the first reading it attributes a coupling to recurring equal parity; at the second it calls a local release/chaining equality a relation between selves. Neither reading supplies the actual joining and receiving. Natural Naming's “A number agreeing is no joining” and Natural Explaining's “Two tellings at one thing” apply to this change of subject. The Resolver's final joined-selves table separately names the joining; it includes matching output sequences in differently joined arrangements and cannot be read as a recurrence proving its own join.

**Correction offered:** “At a changing at one sharing, the released parity agrees with the parity chained as that self's next prior. Its releasing participates at the receiving self through their coupling. Each self continues its own living at its own momentary.”

This preserves the exact local equality and the positive coupling relation. Any further statement about parity recurring at two selves names the particular releasing, arriving and receiving; parity equality alone supplies none of them. No copying or observation of private carrying follows from reading the source assignment.

### 2. Preserve the releasing self's positive participation, not only the receiving offering

At 5.6, “A difference in an offering can participate in the receiving self's next discovering” is a useful positive statement. It replaces the prior's “differences in carrying can have outward consequences through continued coupling,” but begins one relation later. The releasing self's own prior participates in its resolving and further releasing; the revised paragraph leaves that connection unstated while contrasting continued coupling with inspection.

**Correction offered:** “Each self's own prior participates in its resolving and further releasing. Its released parity participates at another self's receiving, with that receiving self's own prior. Further releasing can differ through this continuing coupling; each private momentary remains the living self's own.”

This is a re-saying of participation through actual couplings. It authorizes no experiment comparing private carryings and claims no reconstruction of a private momentary. A different response at a receiving is that response; seeing, identifying or sharing the other's prior momentary is a different claim. The source's assignment and the receiver's resolving remain separate relations even where their parity values agree.

### 3. Agreement is at the offerings surfaced at the sharing

At 5.6, “At the offered parity agreeing with the carried parity, changing is not” omits the receiving condition. Resolver 14-bi-tri-bi-moralizing surfaces the offerings at one sharing: agreeing offerings yield a parity, parting offerings yield 0, and a zero offering passes over. Resolver 12-bi-tri-bi-entraining compares that surfaced relation with the carried parity. One matching offered parity among parting offerings is not the agreement condition: the tables say parting offerings surface 0 and a carried sharing inverts.

The next sentence, “At their differing, changing is,” also leaves absence and parting unnamed. These are not a third intelligence value; they are receiving conditions in the given binary resolving. The paragraph currently looks exhaustive while expressing only the single-offering case.

**Correction offered:** “At a carried sharing, the offerings surfacing at its carried parity accompany no changing, and its prior chains on. At the other parity, at parting offerings or at none surfaced, its carried parity inverts and chains as next prior. Each is the self's continuing at that receiving.”

The existing quiet-releasing sentence carries a valuable distinction: no changing at one receiving is compatible with continuing living. Preserve that relation positively, for example: “A momentary with no changing continues the self's living, is-still-possibling.” Society's competency remains the full coupling relation rather than a property inferred from local quiet.

### 4. Complete continuation is the subject of a protection claim, not its automatic proof

At 5.6, “The protections are at this complete relation” follows a description of society continuing. Read as a location for inquiry, it preserves the prior. Read as achieved protection, it grants continuation the competency and security the prior explicitly kept at their own coupled relation and evidence. The next sentence's denial that a single parity establishes immunity does not resolve that stronger reading: several parities or a complete continuation still need the particular societal relation being claimed.

**Correction offered:** “Each claim of societal protection carries the particular coupled relation it names through the participating selves' actual releasing, arriving and continuing.”

Keep the positive assertion that co-competencing belongs to the participating couplings and is owned by neither self. Keep each self's own carrying and the absence of a representative carrying. This sentence supplies neither blanket security nor a gate waiting on a test. It states the subject and scope of the claim at the claim itself.
