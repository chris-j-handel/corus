# Exhibit TWENTY Natural Naming: each dissolving word, by the natural naming it aims into

**No word here has authority. No living file is changed by this page.**

**What this page is.** A first laying for the next version of Exhibit TWENTY Natural Naming. The living file is a tool: a reader having a word of a science, a medicine or a society finds its natural naming in it, the not natural word dissolving and the concept resolving naturally. This page lays each dissolving word the living file has with the natural naming its saying opens with, so each cluster of dissolving words is seen aiming into one naming. It is returned by one instrument, `instruments/dissolving_words_by_natural_naming.py`, executed on the living file as it is, and it decides nothing: each cluster is for refining by reading.

**Its edges.** The instrument reads four places of the living file, each by its own form: its dissolved words, its family of words, its senses of one foundation word, and its set namings gathering. A naming is taken as the words in italics, or a name of the seventeen, before the first colon of an entry's saying. A saying that is a sentence has no naming by it, and is in part 2. A dissolving word in a sentence of a later part, as each word dissolved by one version, is not read yet. The gathering file's droplets of words out are not read by it yet.

## What the first laying finds

- **The living list is in the order each word was dissolved.** It has 129 entries and 295 words in them. A reader having one word finds it by reading the list through: it has no order by a word and none by a naming.
- **By the naming each saying opens with, the words gather.** The larger clusters are of same-existing, of a parity, of a moment and momentarying, of observing, of a spiral, of an unchangeable form, and of exhausting and a stable form.
- **38 entries are said by a sentence and by no naming.** Each has a cluster by its reading: *door* is a self-bounding; *observer* is observings; *beat* is a self's own sequencing.
- **11 words are in more than one entry:** *inside*, *outside*, *within*, *reading*, *living*, *emanating*, *emanation*, *giving*, *joining*, *ages* and *second sign*. Each has one entry in a well-ordered list, its senses each said in it.
- **Some sayings open with a plain word or a word itself out.** *At*, *has*, *is*, *with* and *each* are plain words and no namings: an entry said by one aims at its naming by its sentence. *Sharing* has fourteen words aiming into it, and is itself out of a session's speaking, gathered so in the gathering file.
- **68 dissolving words are in the living file's own sentences, in 865 places, outside the four places.** The instrument matches a word and no sense. *Across*, *along*, *living*, *within* and *opening* each have a sense that is. Words with an is-not sense only, each place for reading at the next version: *mirror*, *turn*, *ring*, *round*, *reading*, *seat* and *momentary* among them.

## A candidate for the next version's order, for breaking

Three ways in, each whole:

1. **By a word a reader has.** Each dissolving word, by the alphabet, one line: the word, and the natural naming it aims into.

3. **By a field.** Each field's namings by the eight clusters the living file has, each with the names of the seventeen it is by.

Each living sentence has one place in the three, by do-no-harming: none leaves, and a sentence in two places has one.
