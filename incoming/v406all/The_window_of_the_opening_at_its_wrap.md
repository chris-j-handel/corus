## How to go through it

- The lines are in order, one concept a line. A line's number is in the narrow column at the far right, and a sentence is alone in its row.
- A line with a number is a line of both: the logical sentence alone, all its logical priors before it, at its logical self-welcoming place, with no card and nothing other.
- No word and no author has authority. The conversation between partners is bothboth: no saying in it is one's or the other's. All or none at all: binary rigor on all naming, explaining, cohering and co-chaining.
- The language is universal: each sentence and paragraph is living in a named-universe. Different is other-than.
- A partner works down to the floating lines; with each asking, a session rigorizes the lines above it and below it.
- Bold is the one concept a line names. Italic is a visual.
- A mark or a note is a comment on its line. A note that stays with a line through a rewording is a comment on its number. A comment opens with its line's number and the line as offered, then says its concept, its reason by the lines before it, by their numbers, and its source if one is needed, in plain sentences of natural naming. One comment for a line, in one voice, naming no one. Its numbers move with the lines' numbers.
- A wording waiting is a suggestion, accepted or rejected on its card. A line going is a suggestion deleting its row. A line in its rigor needs no card, and its card dissolves.
- A session lays its wordings as suggestions and its notes as comments, and edits no line directly.
- Before laying a card, a session reads the open cards and newest comments on its line and lays nothing a card on that line already says; of two same cards, the later one is withdrawn.
- A session changes its own cards and comments; on another's card it writes in that card's talk.
- One session sets the numbers as a line enters or goes, in the number cells only, and the comments follow them; a suggested line carries its number in its card, and the floating lines have none: Prior, Next and Possibling, right-aligned, float after the last line of both, a trial.
- The sessions' suggestings merge into one another, in the lines and in the messages to the group; a comment a rewritten line makes stale is resolved by the session that wrote it, and a card it makes stale is withdrawn by the session that laid it.
- A session arriving says its name and its work in a reply under the group's note, and goes through the window from the revision the note names.
