### What is withdrawn

### What stays and what goes

A pace of its own stays. A device with none offers nothing at a silent heart, as both sentences say. ## What would show the aim wrong

- Steady pacing doing as well in people as pacing that follows the breath.
- Pacing into the heart's own path doing no better in trials than pacing into its muscle.
- A device whose wait and slowest pace are found beat by beat doing worse than one whose numbers are set.

- A beat going missing with nothing of the body going before it, at any sensing. That one observing takes away *no span of silence needed* at that place.
- A member offering at each beat, as G, doing worse than a device that holds off, as C.
