## Five things held of the heart

1. The beat is two parities, each alternating, and their entraining. It is no third thing.
2. The pace is what changing is possible for the living system as it then is, found beat by beat.
3. When a part loses its coupling, each side keeps a beat of its own, and the pace of the whole subsides.
4. A beat laid over at one fixed pace keeps a person living and does worse; laid over a chamber that has its own beat, it harms.
5. The between of the upper and lower chambers has living within it, with a slower pace of its own, pressed down while a faster beat arrives.

## The one thing no device gives up, and why

Each device keeps a count of time beneath everything: with nothing sensed within its set span, it fires. That is a clock, and it is the reason its wearer lives when the heart falls silent. A device with no pace of its own at all would offer nothing at a silent heart.

The heart does not solve this with a clock either. The junction's cells have a slow beat of their own, a living society within the between, and it is pressed down while anything faster arrives. So *floating and neutralling* is read here as E and never as *no pace at all*: a slowest beater in the heart's society, coupled to it, speaking last. What is open is whether that slowest beat can be found from the body's living, as D does at the breath, and not from a set number.

## What is new here, and what is not

Nothing in A to F is unknown one at a time. What no device does is all six with nothing set by a number: the wait at A, the slowest pace at E and the pace at D each found at each beat from what arrives. Whether that can be built is not known to this session.

## What would show the aim wrong

- Steady pacing doing as well in people as pacing that follows the breath.
- Pacing into the heart's own path doing no better in trials than pacing into its muscle.
- A device whose wait and slowest pace are found beat by beat doing worse than one whose numbers are set.
