The main logical problem is a repeated promotion: a useful distinction or compatible resemblance becomes an exclusive explanation without supplying the additional relation that would make it follow. ## 1. Methodological control is repeatedly made equivalent to a physically still subject

**Physics anchors:** 1.2, “takes every stable form as a minimum of a potential”; 2.1, “Reproduce requires rest between trials”; 3.5, “A symmetry is a term held still”; 4.3, “Thermal equilibrium: exchange runs both ways.”

**Issue:** The text moves from holding a comparison or accounting fixed to the compared system being physically motionless, then treats continuing motion as the defeat of that accounting. Its own thermal-equilibrium example already distinguishes a net condition from ongoing exchange. Consequently the still-point list does not, on its own terms, refute all the different kinds of equilibrium named there. Neither a symmetry of a description nor repeatable preparation logically supplies the extra premise that the subject is at rest.

**Core comparison:** Mathematics 6.1's scientific-method paragraph explicitly says, “A fixing is a relation continuing among changing things,” and requires the step to equilibrium to name its subject still “at the same relation and the same occurrence.” NI 1.3 also distinguishes a stable form continuing through changing from a form named still. Those are narrower distinctions than Physics currently makes.

**Preserve:** Comparisons between an observing, its standard and the interpretation placed on it; each distinct still-point example; the difference between net accounting and particular exchange.

**Correction needed:** Rework the whole methodological claim at its actual subject. Identify precisely what remains fixed in each example and what is observed changing. Do not turn a fixed reference into evidence of a stopped physical system by definition. This is an internal entailment correction; it is not a claim that the core's own universal assertions have thereby been independently proved.

## 2. Bell exclusion is promoted into a unique positive confirmation

The Physics receiving expressly marks “Bell is three inversions from 23” for renewed reading as “a depth no observing meets,” and requires instrument response, reconstructed quantity, scientific account and NI reading to remain distinct. Its later general test distinguishes compatible forms from “exclusive necessity not established.”

**Preserve:** The correlated-click observing, the difference between an individual all-or-none event and an aggregate accounting, and the inquiry into a relation neither side owns.

**Correction needed:** State the Bell observing and its stated exclusion at their own scope, then identify the one-coupling reading as a proposed correspondence. Remove the “therefore” and “proof/confirmation” unless the missing exclusivity argument is actually supplied. The user's framework need not owe a manufactured probability as its own ontology; nevertheless keeping the measured observings whole requires retaining their setting-dependent relationships rather than declaring those relationships irrelevant by renaming their accounting.

## 3. A composition curve is treated as evidence of temporal alternation

**Issue:** The given curve supplies an ordering over nuclear composition and the possibility of energy release in selected transformations. That description does not specify a recurring trajectory, the identity carried between successive events, the timing of two phases or a shared dynamically maintained neutral. A pair of arms on a graph and a pair of processes do not establish temporal alternating. The later marking holds the direction labels and energy-as-surplus reaching but treats this prior dynamic identification as already standing.

**Core comparison:** Numbers 10.1 says the changing that crosses from a field is “which alternates with which, which ordering recurs.” The Physics receiving says “A resemblance is no mechanism” and asks for releasing, arriving and continuing rather than pictures or peaks. Its latest receiving states directly that a spatial gradient is no temporal sequence by itself. These same distinctions apply to a composition axis.

**Preserve:** The field's curve, its iron/nickel region as described, the comparison of two energetic directions, and the explicit abandonment of the 23-and-55 fit.

**Correction needed:** Let the curve establish the relation it records. Hold the proposed dynamic pairing itself as the unfinished correspondence, with a concrete question about what continues and alternates, before deciding self/other direction labels. Do not import the grid's temporal behavior through the shared word “neutral.”

## 6. “All living,” “atom is the floor,” and “no floor” have not been reconciled at their subjects

**Physics anchors:** 1.1, “no non-living torus anywhere” and “a particle a self”; 3.6, “Below the atom no self closes” and “Atom is the floor”; the immediately following collider paragraph, “two selves ... new selves”; 3.12, “the chain has no floor and needs none.”

The discrepancy cannot be resolved by assuming different scales when the text is speaking of the same objects at the same meeting.

**Core comparison:** NI 1.1 and 1.3 include both living and non-living and distinguish them by carrying prior at a named scale and momentary. The Physics receiving explicitly says to keep physical, chemical and biological subjects “rather than a non-living/living division,” and says that naming an object physical does not settle living, non-living or carrying.

**Preserve:** The question of what self-bounds at each scale and the distinction between a counted object and a continuing relation.

**Correction needed:** Rework 1.1 and the relevant whole 3.6 passage using actual named participants, scale and carrying. Distinguish the proposed atom-self floor from the claim about decomposition. Neither atom-first-self nor universal particle-self follows merely from the current definition of carrying.

**Correction needed:** Re-receive Numbers at 5.7 and propagate the withdrawn fit's status into 3.9. For 5.8 and 5.11 supply the missing relation between numerical operations and the named physical observing, or preserve them as comparisons with that relation unfinished. A drying-ring check may investigate spacing; it cannot confirm every floating co-agency or specifically 5:6 merely by finding some ratio.

## 8. The proposed breaks sometimes cannot discriminate the explanation they are supposed to test

**Physics anchors:** 3.10 step 6 excludes computation as “inadmissible, neither seating nor breaking”; step 8 asks for a superconducting current shown to dissipate. Section 3.11 step 6 defines a difference that closes as a reconstruction, while step 8 counts only the raw differences that remain non-landing. Section 3.5 calls both strained conservation and clean conservation evidence for the same reading, with the largest surplus assigned to the clean case.

**Issue:** Evidence is being sorted by the conclusion it reaches. If a difference's closing reclassifies it outside the admitted family, then persistence among the remaining family does not independently support the universal persistence claim. Likewise, “a superconducting current dissipates” does not on its own decide between competing explanations of the stated zero-resistance regime. A model's failure or success must be attached to its actual observable and conditions before deciding what it bears on; merely assigning it “dead substrate” removes an avenue of comparison without establishing the positive explanation.

**Core comparison:** The Physics receiving now carries several contrasting reconnection cases under their different conditions and asks whether the proposed relation makes their differing distributions, or merely fits compatible forms. It keeps the scientific account's successes beside regimes it misses. Mathematics' requirement of the same relation and occurrence prevents changing the admitted subject after the outcome is seen.

**Preserve:** Boundaries on what a simulation alone establishes, the raw/reconstructed distinction, and the honest caveat that rotation curves alone do not dispose of all dark-matter evidence.

**Correction needed:** Name the admitted observing and conditions before reading its outcome; keep that subject stable when it agrees or disagrees. Describe what distinguishes two accounts at that subject. This need not impose ranked “openness” on the NI framework: it is the narrower discipline of not changing the subject of a logical claim.
