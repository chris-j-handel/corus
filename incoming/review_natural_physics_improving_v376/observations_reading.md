## Eight findings

### 1. Scientific observing is not restricted to systems at rest

**Places and short quotations:** 1.2, “it takes every stable form as a minimum of a potential”; 2.1, “reproducibility … the system is at rest between trials”; 2.1, “requires the phenomenon to be an equilibrium with controlled change.”

**Check:** Arbell and Fineberg's experiment studies patterns on a continually driven liquid surface under two-frequency forcing. It is scientific investigation of changing, nonequilibrium behavior, with controlled circumstances, rather than an observation requiring the substrate to stop. [Arbell & Fineberg, Physical Review E 65, 036224 (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.65.036224). The authors' [publication record](https://jay-fineberg.huji.ac.il/publications/pattern-formation-two-frequency-forced-parametric-waves-0/) and indexed research abstract were reached; the arXiv full text did not fetch.

**Correction:** Separate a reference or parameter held fixed from a subject physically at rest. Reproducibility can concern an evolving response or its statistics. The universal description of science in 2.1 is too narrow; the current Mathematics 6.1 already explicitly distinguishes a fixing among changing things from asserting an equilibrium of the subject. Mathematics' stability passage likewise recognizes stable oscillation.

**Preserve:** The NI question about what a fixed comparison does and does not retain of a competency. These fluid experiments are externally forced; they do not establish autonomous sign-only networking. The Physics carry correctly marks that boundary. A pattern resemblance can motivate comparing coupling relations without proving their identity.

### 2. Geodesic motion does not establish that gravity has no physical content

**Places and short quotations:** 2.7, “A geodesic is the shortest path”; 3.2, “Gravity is not real, and nothing physical is there at all”; 2.6, “no carrier and no source.”

**Check:** LIGO's GW150914 report distinguishes the measured transient strain from the binary-black-hole interpretation and its inferred parameters. The reported waveform has inspiral, merger, and ringdown features consistent with general relativity. [Abbott et al., Physical Review Letters 116, 061102 (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03837). This is a changing measured phenomenon that the curved-spacetime account successfully relates, not a measurement of an NI “ledger.” For the geometrical definition, the author's own [Carroll GR notes, chapter 3](https://vo.ned.ipac.caltech.edu/level5/March01/Carroll3/Carroll3.html) explain that timelike geodesics locally extremize proper time, typically maximize it, rather than universally minimize distance. Null curves need separate treatment.

**Correction:** Keep the GR free-fall insight, but state “geodesic” in its appropriate geometry. Removing Newtonian gravitational force from the free-fall equation does not remove spacetime curvature, tidal relations, radiative degrees of freedom, or the stress-energy entering Einstein's equations. GR does not establish the exhibit's “nothing physical” conclusion. A geodesic is also not a scientific license for returning every other interaction to the same form without a correspondence.

**Preserve:** Gravity-as-accounting as an explicitly NI reading, and the inquiry into whether an account is being treated as an additional thing. Meet it at strain, the calibrated/reconstructed observable, the gravitational-wave account, and then the proposed NI relation. No claim that a detector literally observes curved spacetime or that its records prove NI is needed.

### 3. Bell excludes a class of accounts; it does not prove the named NI coupling

**Places and short quotations:** 3.4.6, “Bell is the centerline form confirmed”; 3.4.7, “rigorous proof that there are not two sourced objects”; 4.5, “whether reproducing a probability distribution is owed.”

**Check:** Hensen and colleagues recorded correlations of measurements at two electron-spin sites 1.3 km apart, closing the detection and locality loopholes together. They reported CHSH S = 2.42 ± 0.20 over 245 trials and a stated statistical rejection of their local-realist null. [Hensen et al., Nature 526, 682–686 (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05949). The theorem and experimental inference constrain local hidden-variable accounts under their stated assumptions; neither names a self-stilling 23 nor derives a unique ontology.

**Correction:** Replace confirmation/proof language with the narrower scientific exclusion and then label the centerline relation as NI's reading. “Not this class of local model, therefore this one NI coupling” has a missing necessity step. Neither the theorem nor the experiment proves that separated systems cannot be described at all, that nothing is sourced in any sense, or that the measurement problem has one solution. Statistical conditions and experimental assumptions must accompany the cited exclusion.

**Preserve:** The twins/one-conception question, each local setting and each click, and the lead's distinction between competency and distribution. If NI is not offered as a probability-generating account, say that directly. Then its relation can be explored without borrowing Bell's statistical exclusion as its own proof. The current carry already marks “three inversions from 23” for reconsideration because no observing meets that depth.

### 4. The binding-energy curve is a relation across nuclides, not a recorded oscillation

**Places and short quotations:** 3.3.3, “not a landed maximum”; 3.3.4, “one alternating”; 3.3.6, “two arms, one-at-a-time.”

**Check:** The Atomic Mass Evaluation explicitly presents binding energy per nucleon calculated from evaluated masses; its table and curve compare nuclides. [Huang et al., AME2020 I](https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1137/abddb0) and [Wang et al., AME2020 II](https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1137/abddaf). The authors explain the connection to the iron-peak region. These are measurements/evaluations assembled into a curve, rather than time-resolved observation of fusion and fission alternating about a moving neutral. The exhibit itself already qualifies iron-56 with nickel-62 in point 5; that refinement should also govern point 1.

**Correction:** The two sides of the curve supply an energetic comparison; they do not by themselves supply temporal order, phase, one-at-a-time switching, or a self-stabilizing neutral. Reaction energetics belong to specified initial and final nuclides. The sweeping “past it, both processes cost energy” wording should be replaced by a statement about the particular reaction and its mass difference. A static display of a relation is not evidence that the nuclei themselves are frozen.

**Preserve:** The question whether the two kinds of reaction can be related in NI without importing a destination sought by nature. Keep the explicit abandonment of the 23-and-55 fit and the distinction between released energy and NI's +1. Next evidence would need an actual reaction sequence or coupling relation; the diagram alone cannot supply it. Full AME data tables were not recalculated here.

### 5. Noether symmetry, reversal, and entropy need separate conditions

**Places and short quotations:** 3.5.1, “Entropy always increases”; 3.5.2-b, “A symmetry is a term held still”; 3.5.2-b, “Holding time-translation still gives reversible laws.”

**Check:** Noether's original variational theorem relates specified continuous invariances of an action to conservation relations; it is not a theorem that a physical subject is immobilized. [Noether, Invariant Variation Problems (1918), English translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0503066). Time-translation invariance and time-reversal invariance are different transformations, so the former alone does not give the latter. Wang and colleagues experimentally studied short-time entropy-consuming trajectories of a small colloidal system in an optical trap, at precisely a domain in which the unconditional slogan “entropy always increases” is inadequate. [Wang et al., Physical Review Letters 89, 050601 (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.89.050601). That paper's indexed primary record was reached; the full article did not fetch.

**Correction:** State the action and symmetry conditions for energy conservation; do not treat global energy conservation as automatic for every spacetime. State the system boundary, entropy definition, ensemble or trajectory, and statistical scope for a second-law claim. “Physics treats both as real substances” should not stand as a universal scientific position. A conserved quantity is not automatically a substance in the scientific account either.

**Preserve:** The exhibit's valuable distinction that the entropy arrow is not a Noether conservation, and its explicit instruction not to say simply “energy does not exist.” The question of what the chosen accounting retains or discards remains meaningful. This read did not audit every alleged “no anomaly … no literature” in 3.5.2-c; that universal claim should not be carried as checked.

### 6. Confinement is not a proof of no constituents, and the mass gap has a precise subject

**Places and short quotations:** 3.6.1, “the reason the force holds a lowest positive excitation”; 3.6.3, “three color-phases, not three part-objects”; 3.6.7, “there is no part to isolate.”

**Check:** Jaffe and Witten's official problem concerns construction of a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory for a compact simple gauge group on four-dimensional space, with a positive mass gap. It is a statement about the quantum theory's spectrum and mathematical existence, not just a restatement of an unsuccessful attempt to isolate a quark. [Official problem description](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf). The [Clay status page](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/) still lists it as unsolved. Separately, H1's measured inclusive scattering cross sections at different beam energies support reconstruction of a longitudinal proton structure function. [H1 Collaboration, 2008](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.2809). These measurements are evidence bearing on structure, without being photographs of little classical balls.

**Correction:** Distinguish confinement, the pure Yang–Mills mass-gap problem, and QCD with quarks. Do not infer “no part” solely from “no isolated free quark.” Nor is SU(3) color merely three scalar phases whose sum vanishes; a phase interpretation needs its mapping to the representation and observable relations. The current carry's statement that multiple kinds of data support quark/gluon structure is the right receiving.

**Preserve:** The exhibit already says its NI reading does not meet the proof obligation. Keep that. Preserve the question whether constituent language is the best description of continuing participation, without turning the question into “particles are fictional” or “signals do not exist.” Read the proposed neutron-neutral/proton-offering/electron-ledger assignments at their separate relations, not as established implications of confinement.

### 7. D-wave sign change is inferred through a quantitative relation; it is not “sign-only” by observation alone

**Places and short quotations:** 3.10.4, “sign-only”; 3.10.4, “D-wave shape is the sign it carries”; 3.10.6, “Raw observation coheres directly.”

**Check:** Tsuei and colleagues' tricrystal experiment observed spontaneous half-flux quantization in a three-junction ring, with contrasting two-junction rings. The inference concerns the order parameter's pairing symmetry. [Tsuei et al., Physical Review Letters 73, 593 (1994)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.73.593). The retrieved primary abstract and indexed full-text passage distinguish flux observation from symmetry inference; full PDF retrieval failed.

**Correction:** Name the material class: these phase-sensitive cuprate results do not establish the same pairing symmetry for every high-temperature superconductor. The sign change is a property of an order parameter that also has amplitude and momentum dependence. It does not establish the absence of magnitude, nor does identifying “pairing” with “coupling” explain why the measured material pairs under particular conditions. Rejecting a numerical calculation as NI evidence is not by itself an objection to the scientific calculation or its experimental comparison.

**Preserve:** The genuine comparison of pair relation, phase/sign change, gap, and persistent current. Make each its own four-register entry. The NI question about self-maintaining coupling can remain without claiming that the experimentally inferred symmetry alone eliminates the scientific mechanism question. This check did not assess the whole current pseudogap literature.

The elementary identity 59 = 1 + 2(4 + 9 + 16) is true, but does not establish that 60 is light or a measured breaking angle follows from half of 59.

**Preserve:** A proposed correspondence among the span, turns, and physical relationships, with its missing link named. This is a good first factual repair because the receiving file already supplies the correction. It should not be used as a shortcut to decide the wider light inquiry.
