## Proposed §1.1 · A physical coupling

**A physical coupling** continues through prior, now and next. An existing thing arrives into its next existing through coupling with another existing thing. A prior relation completes; the participation through which a different relation becomes possible continues. The ending of that relation alone names no ending of all its participants.

**Living carrying** brings prior into now at a particular scale and momentary. At a coupling, the living carrying is a self, and the existing thing it couples with is an other. **Non-living existing** carries none of its prior into now; its form continues through its changing. Physical names the subject of the observing and assigns neither living nor non-living by itself. A particle, a wave and a crystal require their own coupling and scale to be explained.

**An emanating form** leaves a living self at prior and arrives at another coupling, carrying none of that self's prior. The form's continuing and the self's carrying are differing participations. A recurring form does not establish an unchanging participant, and a recorded distribution does not by itself establish the living carrying from which it arrives.

**Parity changing** is one and then the other, one way at a time at a coupling. The self's carrying and its coupling with the other continue together. A magnitude, a conserved total or a repeated shape supplies no explanation of that continuing by itself. The particular relation joins the recorded changing to its natural explaining; a common numeral or shape alone supplies no such joining.

**A physical observing** carries the instrument's response and the conditions of its recording. A velocity distribution or another reconstructed quantity also carries its reconstruction. The field's account explains that quantity in its own terms. Natural explaining continues at the coupling's arriving, carrying and changing, with the relation between the observing and that explaining explicit. The difference between the field's account and natural explaining leaves the instrument's record available at its own subject.

The fresh reader compares this proposal with the whole original §1.1 and with §§1.2 and 3.6. The proposal must not introduce a new exclusion, overstate the observed continuity of an individual participant, or settle a physical correspondence by naming it.
