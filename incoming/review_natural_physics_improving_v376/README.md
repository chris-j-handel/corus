## One motion at a time

1. Fix the source snapshot and the particular section. Read the section whole with its preceding and following concepts, its other occurrences in Physics, the core relation and the relevant incoming.
2. Complete the three readings separately. Each finding names its section, the actual words, the differing relation, and the value to preserve. Different observations, scales or readings are not made into a contradiction merely because their words differ.
3. Compare findings at their propositions. Resolve an arithmetic or source-reading error locally; keep a missing relation distinct from a demonstrated contradiction. Neither majority agreement nor a preferred wording decides the result.
4. Prepare the section whole beside its prior. Account for each original contribution: carried, corrected with reason, or still needing a named receiving. Preserve the prior while any value remains unreceived.
5. Have the fresh reader compare that section with the original and the current core. Recheck the exact relation changed and the section's neighbours. A reader's correction can change the proposal, and that change needs its own stated result.
6. Offer the completed motion through the expedition's incoming route. Its acceptance, entry into a living file and any source release are separate events. The next section opens from the corrected prior rather than from an assumed whole-file completion.

This procedure was exercised on the opening proposal in this arrival. It remains a method for the subsequent motions, not a claim that all forty sections have been rewritten or independently proved.
