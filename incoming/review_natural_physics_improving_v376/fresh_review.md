## Findings and concrete corrections

1. **Opening continuity changes subjects without explaining the change.** “A physical coupling continues through prior, now and next” is followed by “A prior relation completes; the participation through which a different relation becomes possible continues.” NI §3.3 distinguishes a coupling's completing and uncoupling from the living self's carrying between couplings. The draft moves between a continuing coupling, a completed relation and a continuing participation without saying which continues. “The ending of that relation alone names no ending of all its participants” usefully refuses an inference, but the preceding sentence can positively assert that the same participants persist. This is especially consequential beside Physics §3.6, which discusses new couplings and products.

   **Correction:** Say completing/uncoupling at the coupling and carrying at the living participant, using NI §3.3's relation. Do not infer either destruction or persistence of individual physical participants from an observed relation's completion. A suitable direction is: “At its completing a coupling releases its changing. A living self carries its prior into its next coupling. Completion of the prior coupling alone identifies neither the participants at the next coupling nor their carrying.” This is a proposed correction of scope, not a substitute for the full paragraph: non-living arriving still needs its own following sentence.

2. **The bounded form has lost its bounding.** The first contribution row says the original bounded, alternating, re-arriving form is re-said. In the proposed section, neither bounding nor the relation of bounding to carrying is actually explained. A completed “relation” is not automatically the same concept. NI §3.3 supplies the missing relation: the method bounds at each momentary; living carrying continues between momentaries; neither bounding nothing nor carrying without completing supplies that relation. This is also the opening needed for self-bounding in §1.2.

   **Correction:** Restore that relation in plain words before the physical examples. Explain the completing and the continuing together. If torusing's geometric contribution is being routed, give it an explicit receiving passage and keep its mathematical standing distinct from physical correspondence. Merely retaining “continuing form” preserves only one part of the original contribution.

3. **Two-way offering is named but not explained.** “Parity changing is one and then the other, one way at a time at a coupling” does not make the original self-offering/other-offering relation intelligible. “One and then the other” can refer to participants, forms, or parity; the draft never settles this. “The self's carrying and its coupling with the other continue together” adds coexistence, not the relation by which the two sides offer and their different carrying continues.

   **Correction:** First explain a changing form as one form and then another (NI §1.2), then distinguish that from each side's offering at a coupling. Say the two directions and each side's continuing in ordinary words, naming parity only after that relation is intelligible. Do not identify two forms, two participants and two directions merely through their shared number. The six recursions may receive their detailed explaining in §1.2, but the two-way relation belongs in this opening.

4. **“Physical” makes a useful correction; its subject list is incomplete.** The sentence assigning neither living nor non-living from “physical” alone agrees with current NI's named-scale-and-momentary distinction. It also avoids deciding the original “particle a self” against §3.6's “below the atom no self closes” by fiat. However, the table claims to keep “Field, particle, wave and crystal” visible, while the prose names only particle, wave and crystal. The original's field coupling, selection at a membrane, wave continuation and lattice society also receive no individual disposition.

   **Correction:** Include the field among the subjects. In the accounting, distinguish the physical subjects retained from their proposed NI assignments corrected or carried forward. Record the §1.1/§3.6 selfhood conflict explicitly at the particular scale. Current NI corrects the inference from “physical” to “living”; it does not by itself establish the physical status of any named particle or establish the atom as a lower boundary.

5. **Emanation and observed form are separated well, with one small scope repair.** The statement that recurring form or recorded distribution alone establishes neither an unchanging participant nor living carrying is valuable. “Carrying none of that self's prior,” however, is narrower than NI §1.3's definition of a non-living emanation as carrying none of the prior. It leaves an unintended possibility that the emanation carries some other prior as living.

   **Correction:** Use “carrying none of the prior” and explicitly connect this to non-living existing. Preserve the distinction between an emanation's continued form and the living self's carrying. No observed distribution is required to instantiate a living self merely to remain legitimate physical observing.

6. **“An observing carries” blurs the definition just established.** The final paragraph says an observing “carries the instrument's response” and a reconstructed quantity “also carries its reconstruction.” Immediately above, carrying prior defines living. These may be ordinary documentary uses of “carries,” but without explanation the section can classify records as living or turn reconstruction into the quantity's own participating.

   **Correction:** State the documentary relation directly: a physical record includes the instrument response and recording conditions; a reconstructed quantity is reported with its reconstruction and assumptions. The field's quantity stays exact at its subject. The NI proposal must say the relation it proposes between that record and particular arriving/changing/carrying. “The particular relation joins…” does not itself provide such a relation. A worked physical correspondence can follow later; this paragraph should specify that work without speaking as if the joining has occurred.

7. **The value table conflates correction, routing and a promise.** “Develop … later,” “continue as a specific scale correspondence,” and “receive at the particular field problems” are useful directions, not completed receiving. The Method §§1.2 and 1.5 explicitly distinguish carrying at a destination from a destination merely named. The unchanged source protects this candidate stage. It does not make a later deletion safe automatically.

   **Correction:** Mark each row precisely as retained in proposed prose, corrected with the reason, or carried at a named receiving passage still to be written. For the old bounding-zero/+1 claim, distinguish the obsolete resolver interpretation from any separately specified mathematical construction. Preserve the old proposition as the object of correction rather than treating an unchanged historic copy as current receiving. Do the same for locality, the sign-only relation, absence of a source, and transmission/action at a distance: those are substantive propositions, not exhausted by removing “force” or “magnitude.”

## Adjacent-section dependency

An inserted §1.1 cannot coherently open the existing §1.2 unchanged. That section still says each coupling sums to bounding-zeroing, leaves +1, excludes magnitude, and makes broad claims about all physical stability. These are the very relations the proposed opening corrects or declines to settle. Its six-recursioning account also cannot be validated merely by counting six external connectors or identifying three physical quantities. Explaining §4.7 requires the relation, not shared numbers.

Physics §3.6 is a second, distinct dependency. It asserts or proposes a below-atom selfhood boundary, the neutron/proton/electron assignments, color-as-phase and the mass-gap correspondence, sometimes reporting resolution and elsewhere naming missing correspondence. The proposed opening properly avoids deciding these by naming, but should explicitly carry their next explaining. Its status sentence alone does not repair their scopes. This is not a demand to solve Yang–Mills before an NI proposal exists; it is a demand not to make the opening's improved vocabulary imply that those correspondences have thereby been settled.

## What the reviewer learned about reviewing

A replacement may remove overclaiming while also removing the positive relation the old passage tried to explain. Review needs both checks independently. Clearer vocabulary is useful only where the subject, completing, continuing and participation stay identifiable. A proposed receiving table is valuable review material, but the table must not claim the full contribution is already carried when it only specifies future work.

Precise remaining local corrections:
