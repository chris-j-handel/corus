## Completing and continuing at the opening

**A relation completing and participation continuing are differing claims.** This is the useful opening into the exhibit's own problem. A record of a prior relation ending does not alone establish that all participation ends. It also does not establish that the same named participants persist unchanged. Both inferences would outrun that record.

The current core distinguishes **living carrying** from **non-living existing** at a particular scale and momentary. The old Physics opening declares all physical toruses living and makes a particle a self; later it says no self closes below the atom and calls colliding protons selves. Those passages require an actual account of the participants and their carrying. The word physical cannot settle that account.

The proposed §1.1 consequently develops the physical coupling, prior/now/next, living carrying, non-living existing, emanating, two-way offering, parity changing and bounding in sequence. It distinguishes the coupling's completing from carrying between couplings. It retains field, particle, wave and crystal as subjects requiring their own relation and scale. Its terms belong to the NI account; introducing them is not a scientific demonstration that every physical event has the proposed relation.

**The positive relation must survive a correction.** My first proposal removed overclaiming but also lost the opening's bounding and did not adequately explain the two-way offering. The independent reviewer caught both losses. The revision restores completing/bounding and self-to-other/other-to-self offering, across and along, without identifying two forms, two participants and two directions simply because each is two. Documentary wording also changed: a record includes an instrument response; calling that documentary inclusion living carrying would confuse the very distinction the section introduces.

The candidate's contribution table specifies what its prose retains, what it corrects and what still needs receiving. Specific particle assignments, prime-scale society correspondences, the geometric contribution, locality, force and transmission have not acquired new receiving passages merely because a table names their destinations. Their original propositions remain in the unchanged source. This is a complete proposal for review, not a completed replacement of all the original value.

## A physical observing and its proposed correspondence

**A physical record, a reconstructed quantity, a scientific account and an NI reading have different work.** This distinction was already received from the prior session. It lets the scientific problem remain exact while the NI exploration asks what arrives, what completes, what carries and what becomes possible next. It does not make the record interpretation-free: calibration, selection and reconstruction remain visible where used.

For the next worked case, preserve the actual record and the conditions under which it was acquired. Say which quantity is reconstructed and by what assumptions. State what the field's account predicts, where its comparison succeeds and where a particular comparison fails. Then state the NI relation proposed for those same circumstances. If the claim is that no other relation is possible, its excluding step must be supplied at that subject. An observed binary alone does not establish every additional relation named in an account of it.

This is also the route for treating the user's all-or-none claim seriously. It is not enough to collect agreeable shapes and exempt every difficulty as accounting; nor does one unestablished physical mapping alone refute every core claim. The necessity connecting the core to that mapping is the particular thing to expose and review. The distinction between a missing argument, an incompatible proposition and a corrected reference remains useful throughout.

**The first worked case remains a choice of dependency.** The observations reader recommends Bell §3.4 first, with the already available method and Numbers corrections. The logic and explaining readers recommend the opening §1.1. This contribution chooses §1.1 because the participant/carrying distinction is used by every later case. Bell remains the first proposed externally sourced case after those prerequisites. This order is a coordinating judgment, not unanimity manufactured from separate reports.
