### Skin: prior injury, subsequent repair and subsequent harm

**Statement.** How does epithelial responsiveness persist after inflammation or injury, and what later responses accompany it?

**Accounts.** The papers investigate retained chromatin accessibility, priming of progenitors and regulatory changes continuing through cellular turnover.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** Mouse skin, lineage tracing, repeat injury, molecular assays and perturbations; the later cancer comparison includes a separate carcinogenic challenge. These are distinct experimental preparations.

**Progress.** Following six days of imiquimod inflammation, later mouse wounds closed approximately 2.5 times faster, including at day 180. Distant untreated skin lacked the advantage. Disrupting AIM2-related signalling removed the enhanced response. This experiment did not demonstrate subsequent cancer. [Nature primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5808576/), DOI 10.1038/nature24271, Figures 1–5, 2017.

In a separate wound study, progenitors 3–7 mm from the injury changed despite not repairing the original wound. Later repair improved; an advantage remained at 40 weeks. Previously wounded mice also developed tumours sooner under **DMBA followed by repeated UVB**. Wounding alone was not the tumour-producing exposure reported. [Nature Cell Biology primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10185470/), DOI 10.1038/s41556-023-01120-0, Figures 1, 6 and 8 and tumourigenesis methods, 2023.

A further study followed epidermal chromatin for two years after inflammation: 117 of 934 initial memory domains remained at one year, largely persisting at two. Wound repair was examined at one year; frailty restricted the two-year functional investigation to a molecular response. Its sequence changes used to predict memory longevity were computational, not edited-mouse outcomes. [Science primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13295011/), DOI 10.1126/science.adz6830, 2026. Figure-specific animal counts still need gathering before a complete entry.

**Intake limits.** These papers offer mechanisms of persistent responsiveness with different experimental reach. This intake establishes no universal criterion determining whether every later encounter will benefit.

**Addresses.** Accounts, methods and results are located separately in the linked papers. Their populations and mechanisms are not interchangeable.

### Peanut: changing exposure and continuing or diminishing tolerance

**Statement.** Does protection following early consumption persist after instructed avoidance? Separately, does desensitization in established allergy persist after immunotherapy is stopped or reduced?

**Accounts.** Durable tolerance or sustained unresponsiveness versus desensitization dependent on continued exposure; antibody and basophil findings contribute to the field's explaining.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** Keep initial allergy, age, exposure history, subsequent consumption and challenge criteria with each result. The early-introduction and established-allergy studies are not a randomized comparison against each other.

**Progress.** High-risk infants had originally been assigned consumption or avoidance through age five. Following twelve months of instructed avoidance, allergy was identified at age six in 52/280 original avoiders and 13/270 original consumers. Avoidance adherence was 90.4% and 69.3%; the protocol itself permitted limited intake. This was not one year of verified zero exposure for every child. [LEAP-On primary paper](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1514209), DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1514209, Methods and Results, 2016.

At adolescent follow-up, outcomes were available for 497 participants: allergy in 38/246 original avoiders versus 11/251 original consumers. Later eating was self-selected and partly retrospectively reported; the groups' later intake differed. [LEAP-Trio primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12875682/), DOI 10.1056/EVIDoa2300311, 2024.

In established allergy, 120 participants entered the POISED trial. Maintenance reached 4,000 mg peanut protein and was stopped or reduced at week 104. Of those assigned to stop, 21/60 passed the 4,000 mg challenges at both weeks 104 and 117, versus 1/25 placebo recipients; 8/60 passed at week 156. In the 300 mg/day group, passing declined from 83% at week 104 to 37% at week 156. Failure at that threshold does not establish loss of all protection. Gastrointestinal and skin adverse events occurred; two active-group participants had serious adverse events. [POISED primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31522849/), DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(19)31793-3, 2019.

**Intake limits.** The records distinguish persistence after changed exposure from response during a continuing regimen. They do not establish permanent tolerance from a single passed challenge.

**Addresses.** Each linked paper supplies its own assignment, follow-up and outcome definitions.

### Autoimmunity: remission while B cells return

**Statement.** Can deep B-cell depletion be followed by sustained remission as B cells reappear?

**Accounts.** Depletion of autoreactive populations and predominantly naive reconstitution are investigated as immune resetting.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** Uncontrolled series of 15 people: eight with lupus, three with inflammatory myositis and four with systemic sclerosis. Fludarabine and cyclophosphamide preceded one CD19 CAR-T infusion.

**Progress.** B cells returned in 14/15, with mean aplasia 112±47 days. All eight lupus patients reached DORIS remission; myositis patients had major clinical responses; sclerosis activity declined. All stopped immunosuppressive treatment. Median follow-up was 15 months. Reported harms included cytokine-release syndrome, neurotoxicity and hospitalized pneumonia.

**Own record.** The authors describe their findings as “providing rationale for further controlled clinical trials.”

**Intake limits.** The series cannot separate chemotherapy from CAR-T contributions or demonstrate that every therapeutic cell disappeared.

**Addresses.** [NEJM primary paper](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2308917), DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2308917, Methods, B-Cell Phenotype, Tables 2–3, 2024; [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38381673/).

**Further progress — returning composition.** A seven-person analysis of overlapping SLE participants compared peripheral blood before treatment and after B-cell reconstitution at 9–28 weeks. Single-cell transcriptome and receptor sequencing distinguished fewer memory and class-switched B cells from increased transitional and IgM/IgD populations. CAR T cells were undetected in the sampled post-reconstitution datasets. This does not establish their absence from every tissue. Chemotherapy contribution remains unresolved. [JCI Insight primary paper](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/179433), DOI 10.1172/jci.insight.179433, Results and Methods, 2024.

**Further progress — antibodies and the measuring method.** Serum from six overlapping SLE patients was examined before and three months after treatment. A sensitive multiplex assay found broad autoantibody reductions in five; the sixth remained in reported remission despite persistence of many measured autoantibodies. Earlier assays had classified several as negative. Pathogen/vaccine antibody levels ranged from unchanged to moderately reduced; none became negative. One participant received intravenous immunoglobulin, complicating attribution of antibody persistence. These are antibody measurements, not a demonstrated absence of infection susceptibility. A contribution from CD19-negative long-lived plasma cells was proposed, not established by tissue sampling in this study. [Primary serum study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10514439/), DOI 10.1016/j.omtm.2023.08.023, Figure 2 and Discussion, 2023. This is overlapping follow-up, not six independent confirmations of the earlier series.

**Further progress — blood and tissue.** Paired inguinal lymph-node biopsies in five CAR-T recipients showed depletion of CD19/CD20-positive cells approximately two months after treatment. A five-person rituximab comparison retained tissue B cells despite similarly absent circulating B cells. CD138-positive plasma cells persisted. This small tissue comparison does not establish uniform depletion in every organ. [Primary lymph-node study](https://ard.bmj.com/content/early/2024/08/16/ard-2024-226142), DOI 10.1136/ard-2024-226142, 2024 online publication.

**Further progress — a later opposing outcome.** A person with inflammatory myositis from the earlier series relapsed after nine months. Repeat administration of the same CD19 CAR-T product did not produce the previous expansion or B-cell depletion; anti-CAR reactivity was detected. A subsequent BCMA-targeted treatment was followed by remission. This one-person sequence preserves recurrence and a changed response to repeated treatment; it cannot isolate every contributing mechanism. [Nature Medicine primary case](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03718-3), DOI 10.1038/s41591-025-03718-3, 2025. The earlier remission finding remains bounded to its reported follow-up.

The later BCMA response lasted nine months at reporting; immunoglobulin reduction required replacement, and grade-1 cytokine-release syndrome occurred. An intervening treatment had temporarily benefited the patient and involved CMV viraemia. The later remission does not establish an absence of treatment harm.

**Further progress — prospective extension.** The uncontrolled CASTLE phase 1/2a trial enrolled 24 participants across three diseases; 22 met their disease-specific six-month efficacy endpoints. Those endpoints differed: lupus remission, myositis response and absence of sclerosis-associated lung progression. Reconstituted B cells were predominantly naive in the evaluable samples. One dose-limiting kidney injury occurred amid several concurrent complications. This extends the observation without a randomized comparator or a universal mechanism. [Nature Medicine primary trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13004673/), DOI 10.1038/s41591-025-04185-6, 2026.

### Microbial treatment: dosing ends while introduced organisms remain

**Statement.** Does a three-day microbiome intervention following antibiotics reduce recurrent C. difficile infection?

**Accounts.** Introduced Firmicutes spores establish populations; metabolic changes, including bile acids, contribute to the proposed explanation.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** Double-blind randomized trial, 182 adults with recurrent infection after antibiotic-associated symptom resolution; four capsules daily for three days. Stool composition, metabolites and clinical recurrence were followed.

**Progress.** Recurrence by week eight was approximately 12% versus 40% with placebo. Introduced species were detected by week one and persisted through week eight. Gastrointestinal events were common; serious adverse events occurred in both groups. [NEJM primary trial](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2106516), DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2106516, 2022.

In the same participants, recurrence by week 24 was 19/89 versus 44/93. This extends follow-up; it is not an independent replication. [JAMA primary follow-up](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2797690), DOI 10.1001/jama.2022.16476, cumulative-recurrence table, 2022.

**Intake limits.** Associations among engraftment, metabolites and benefit do not identify every necessary species or establish a complete mechanism.

**Addresses.** Primary trial: efficacy, safety and compositional/metabolomic results. Follow-up: recurrence definitions and table.

### Antibiotic persistence: distinct individual histories

**Statement.** Is prior growth arrest necessary for persistence?

**Accounts.** Pre-existing nongrowth and several survival paths from initially growing cells.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** Individual E. coli MG1655 lineages in microchambers; one preparation received ampicillin 200 μg/mL for six hours, followed by withdrawal and observation of regrowth.

**Progress.** All six persisters among approximately 380,000 exposed cells had grown before exposure. During exposure, some arrested and some continued growth and division with altered morphology. Other preparations yielded nongrowing survivors.

**Intake limits.** Growth beforehand, growth during exposure and proliferation afterward are separate observations. Rare laboratory survivors do not settle clinical outcomes or long-run resistance evolution.

**Addresses.** [eLife primary paper](https://elifesciences.org/articles/79517), DOI 10.7554/eLife.79517, Results and Figure 4, 2025.

**Further progress — an activity without growth.** Individually tracked E. coli neither grew nor divided during carbenicillin exposure, then formed microcolonies after withdrawal. A separate assay measured enhanced antibiotic efflux in isolated persisters, with deletion and inhibitor controls supporting active transport. The transport measurements and reproductive histories were complementary experiments, not a continuous record of both in every same cell. [Molecular Cell primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4850422/), DOI 10.1016/j.molcel.2016.03.035, Figures 1, 2 and 4, 2016.

**Further progress — an unresolved absence.** The eLife study also observed intact-looking, dye-excluding cells without regrowth during the available follow-up. Death was not excluded. Shape, dye exclusion, transport activity and subsequent reproduction therefore retain distinct evidential reach. Nongrowth alone assigns neither death nor persister status.

### Glucose: benefit with continuing measured feedback

**Statement.** Does closed-loop insulin delivery improve glucose outcomes compared with a sensor-augmented pump?

**Accounts.** An engineered algorithm adjusts insulin delivery using sensor information.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** Six-month randomized study of 168 people with type 1 diabetes; 112 assigned closed-loop control, 56 comparator.

**Progress.** Time at 70–180 mg/dL rose from 61% to 71% with closed-loop control; the comparator remained around 59%. Adjusted difference: 11 percentage points, 95% CI 9–14. Time below 70 mg/dL also declined. No serious hypoglycemic event occurred; one ketoacidosis event occurred in the closed-loop group.

**Intake limits.** Benefit was observed with continuing assistance. Independent insulin production or universal absence of harm was not established.

**Addresses.** [NEJM primary trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7076915/), DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1907863, Methods and Results, 2019.

### Renewing skin: a continuing lineage and functioning tissue

**Statement.** Which transplanted lineages sustain regenerated epidermis through subsequent renewal?

**Accounts.** Persistent stem-cell lineages replenish shorter-lived progenitors and differentiated tissue.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** In one child with extensive epidermal loss, cells from a 4 cm² biopsy were genetically corrected and expanded to regenerate approximately 0.85 m² of epidermis. Vector-integration sites marked ancestry in sampled cultures and later biopsies.

**Progress.** The graft remained robust and blister-free through 21 months. Analysis of 687 cultured clones from pregraft material and later biopsies supported progressive replenishment by stem-cell lineages. The estimated 3–4-month half-life referred to transit-amplifying progenitors, not terminal surface cells. [Nature primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6283270/), DOI 10.1038/nature24487, clonal-tracing results, 2017.

Follow-up of the same child at five years five months found stable grafted epidermis and near-control water loss. Mechanical detection remained subnormal despite restored thermal detection. No graft-associated tumours or clonal expansion were reported. This was continuing follow-up, not independent replication or proof of zero future insertional risk. [NEJM primary follow-up](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2108544), 2021.

An independent adult case followed for 6.5 years retained functioning grafted epidermis. Six integration sites were recovered from a small sampled area. Palm-derived cells retained palm-associated differentiation after transplantation to another site. These findings distinguish inherited lineage and differentiation from a newly acquired response to experience. [Stem Cell Reports primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3916757/), DOI 10.1016/j.stemcr.2013.11.001, 2014.

**Intake limits.** Inherited marks support ancestry; repeated functional measures support continuing tissue function. Neither continuously follows every physical cell. No finding here establishes that every original stem cell was replaced. The entire coordination of renewing tissue is not explained by genetic ancestry alone.

**Addresses.** Clonal analysis, biopsies and functional observations in the three linked primary papers.

### Apparently recovered heart function and changed support

**Statement.** Does improved function in dilated cardiomyopathy persist after a supporting treatment regimen is withdrawn?

**Accounts.** Apparent recovery may represent treatment-supported remission with continuing vulnerability.

**Specifications, reach and approaches.** Fifty-one asymptomatic participants had improved ejection fraction, normal ventricular volume and low NT-proBNP. Treatment was continued or withdrawn in supervised stages. Initial improvement preceded randomization.

**Progress.** At six months, 11/25 assigned withdrawal met relapse criteria versus 0/26 continuing treatment. The composite endpoint included ventricular function, volume, biomarker changes or clinical heart failure. It does not mean 44% became overtly ill. Treatment was restarted upon relapse. The pilot was small and open-label; image assessment was masked. [Lancet primary TRED-HF trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6319251/), DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(18)32484-X, 2019.

Over a median six years, 33/51 had experienced relapse; five remained off medication without relapse. Treatment after the original trial varied, and the follow-up endpoint changed. This was not a six-year randomized comparison or a cohort continuously without treatment. [European Journal of Heart Failure primary follow-up](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11798629/), DOI 10.1002/ejhf.3475, 2024 online publication.

**Intake limits.** The observations distinguish current function from persistence after changed support. They do not make the earlier benefit unreal, identify every person's necessary support, or isolate individual drugs.

**Addresses.** Eligibility, withdrawal, relapse definitions and outcomes in the original trial; medication exposure and revised criteria in follow-up.
