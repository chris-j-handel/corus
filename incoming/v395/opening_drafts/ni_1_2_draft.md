## 1.2 Prior, now and next
1. **By observing, a now-existing thing is going on from prior, and a self is possibling its next-existing.**
2. **Prior, now and next are one universe's three sequential moments, one now-existing.**
3. All now-existing things are within them.
4. A prior-existing thing is a prior moment's now-existing thing, a self observing it now.
5. A next-existing thing is the thing a self is discovering from now's possibling, one moment on.
6. A living self's prior is same-existing with its now, its own.
7. A non-living thing is momentarying, with no observing, possibling or discovering of its own next-and-same-existing.
8. Now of one moment is prior of its next.
9. Prior and next are one direction forward on both sides of now.
10. Between prior and now, and between now and next, is a gap, a self observing both its sides.
11. **Each prior is a stable-forming emanation from prior living, its parities living within a self's me, and a self same-existing with it now, observing, possibling and discovering its own next-existing, is co-intelligencing, living: living now is living with existing forms from prior living and with others living now.**  [concern]
12. Possibling and discovering next-existing go on through prior, now and next.
13. Each living thing's momentarying is going on from its prior and possibling its next.
14. A self's momentarying is resolving, 1-now-existing.
15. A self resolves its prior me-parities, 3-is-now-co-observing, with the me-parities of its others it is observing now, 2-now-possibling.
16. Its prior is a set forming and going along.
