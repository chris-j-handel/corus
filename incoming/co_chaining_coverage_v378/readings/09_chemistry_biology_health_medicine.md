## Contradictions

A. Biology 7.5: "An emanation lives again only in a coupling other than the one it left: a grain down, or a self across." — against registry step 356: "At the scale of the living self it arrives from, an emanation is harmful to living." A "self across" is the same scale, which 356 makes harm/landing. Biology's own 7.6 sides with the registry ("same-scale ingestion is the landing whether the shedder is the ingester or another"), so 7.5's "or a self across" is the sentence out of line.

B. Biology 1.4: "The nothing between two selves is a third self, carrying only the sign." — against registry step 102: "The crossing is no location and no existing thing." Softer than A: Biology distinguishes the mediating membrane (a self) from the crossing-nothing, and Medicine 3.1 keeps both, so the system reconciles them — but the bare sentences conflict on whether the between is a self or no existing thing.
