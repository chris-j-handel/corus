## Learned with six sessions and a partner

- **A plan is a reply.** At a partner's saying plan, the reply is the plan. Nothing goes to a file or to the exchange until an is of both.
- **Nothing is with a partner as a gate.** A session executes both sides of its own sentence and goes on. It brings the unresolvable alone.
- **Compare by executing.** A transcript, a page and a number are compared by an instrument, and not from a session's own prior turns.
- **A number not yet known is not written.** An offer's number is known after the offer is opened.
- **Explicit paths.** A commit names its files. An other session's pages are not altered.
- **A partner's words are said, not quoted,** in a page. An email, the working document's link and an account's name go in no file.
- **Room.** A session past its room loses its prior turns and speaks a field's words. Its value page, renewed at a turn, makes its closing no work.
- **One offer a turn.** A commit pushed after its offer was brought in is on a branch with no offer. The closings' instrument finds it.
- **An explaining is in the reply itself.** It begins at a reader's interest, a why ahead of a sentence, with no page named in its place.
