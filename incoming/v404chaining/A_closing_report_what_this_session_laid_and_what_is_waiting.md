1. **Natural naming and explaining come before all.** A partner's words are not called incohering before the files' own naming of them is found.
2. **A sentence of is is one concept, and short.** A long sentence of many concepts is contorted.
3. **Natural explaining has no colon.** A sentence brings one next concept, growing from the concepts before it.
4. **A rule in no living file is not brought in.** "A name has one defining sentence" was this session's own, and it made living one thing.
5. **A sentence is gone through whole.** Gone through by half, a sentence of Exhibit TWENTY-EIGHT seemed to say a set is no thing. Whole, it has a set a thing as its things are.

7. **A question the code resolves is not asked.** The two binary distinguishings were already Exhibit ONE's two comparisons.

10. **Six words of a partner in sequence are re-said in a file.** Three places, laid by this session, were found later.
11. **A transcript has a thing as it was asked, and not its dissolving.** A place open now is a note in a gathering file, a card or comment in the window's document, or a line of the main list. Learned from the managing session.
12. **A fetch of a branch outside a clone's fetch line moves no tracking line.** One slice was nearly built on a stale copy. A fetch names the branch with its own line.
13. **The gathering check finds a released word on a line a slice touches.** A restoring that touches a line raises the count, so a released word on a touched line is re-said.
14. **The desktop app's browser joins a session at its start alone.** Without a browser this session could not reach the window's document, and its suggestings went on a page.
