## D. The ten named still, and the six beside all things

TWENTY-TWO's ten are ways a coupling's third term is "given a fixed thing to be": a first behind, a place, a last, a store, an end, a magnitude, a beat, a value, a side, a cut. The problems above seat at the arriving (12, 127, 207, 253, 103, 154), the middle named as an end (111, 179, 95, 220), the cut (8, 50, 112, 18, 114) and the side (19, 62, 77). The ten say nothing of scale; "one resolving, and no scale of its own" and "the gap stays exactly one at each box and each scale" are the only scale words. TWENTY-FOUR 2.7's six beside all things are a size/ground/floor/scale, a fixed form, a total, a common beat, a store, a doer from outside. A lowest scale is the first of the six (a floor); a top scale is the third (a total across the changing); living and non-living as two kinds is the second (a form named still); a zombie, non-living at every scale, is the sixth with no doer. So the four equilibria the understanding names are already four of the six, and the registries' ten seat them without saying which of the six each is. The understanding's addition is one sentence the ten lack: the thing named still at each of these arrivals is a scale read as a kind.
