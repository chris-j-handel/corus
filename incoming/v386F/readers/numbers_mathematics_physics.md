## C. The full dimensionality with scale as a direction

What the files already say. NI 3.2 already gives each of the five dimensions two directions "up the numbers and down them," and Numbers 1.7 already names the two axes: "parity along, inward or outward across." So the files hold: along = parity/momentaries (forward), across = inward/outward = scale, of no size, "the walk by two." Numbers 10.2 separates the two runnings: "The local momentary one to nine and the four-cycles among the seventeen names are two runnings, sharing their digits alone" — 1–2, 2–3 ... 8–9 are the momentary direction; the one move (8 up, 17 less, 9 less) is the podaling among names. The scale step is said two ways: span to span, "9-tri-bi-co-momentarying being 1-co-bi-tri-offering 8 up" (5.3; 5.2 calls 6 + 8k "the scale's changing"), and name to inward 1–17, "8n − 7" (NI 4.15, Numbers 9.7). 9.7 adds the step down: "podaling at each span gives the span below it," 17 → 9 → 5 → 3. So the files do say which numbers are which: forward = the overlapping pairs in 1–9; up = 8 up (self's span to society's) and 8n − 7 (a name to its inward 1–17); down = the podals 17 less and 9 less, "exchanging across for along" (9.8). They do not call the podaling "the scale direction" in those words.

Reading the five with scale added. Under 8n − 7 the self's 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 are 1, 9, 17, 25, 33 at the scale inward: self prior at 1 (entry), other prior at 9 (the society's momentarying), co-momentarying now at 17 ("the next 1"), bi-momentarying now at 25 ("twenty-five is at no name," 5.4: the between), self next at 33 ("33 is in none of them as 17 is in none among 1 to 16," 5.2). 5.4's "One to twenty-five is three spans" and 9.7's "one to nine at one scale being one to sixty-five at the next" are this already. At each momentary "two dimensions change and three do not" (NI 3.2); with scale, each dimension is binary in four directions: forward/back the momentaries and in/out the scales, torusing odd through the large opening and corusing even through the small one.

A discrepancy to note. The understanding as briefed says "a self's 9 to 17 is its society's 1 to 9." The files say 1, 9, 17 ↔ 1, 2, 3 (NI 4.15: "the along 9 is the across 2, 17 is the outward name 3"), so a self's 9 to 17 is one momentary of the society, its 2 to 3, not its 1 to 9. The 1–9 reading is the 8-shift (1.5, 5.3), the 1–2–3 reading is 8n − 7; 9.7 keeps them as "two relations at one span" and "No scaling is taken over another."
