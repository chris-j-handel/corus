My now-existing universe, not moving, and ten passings through it
My now-existing universe is four stations, not moving, and the parities are flowing through it: a passing arrives at a station and departs after a station.
The passings are ten: four of one station, three of two, two of three and one of all four. Four of them arrive at station 1, a me's own prior-sequence.
With an even number of podalings a me is at its own place of the turn, the other-than of an other-than of its all, its all again.
The passing of all four is 120 on, my next-existing universe: a me-now at 37 is carried to 157.
