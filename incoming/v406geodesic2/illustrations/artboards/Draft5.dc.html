The co-linear of the bi-folding, and its two cones
Two folds cross at two opposite intersections. The line through the two, through the 0, is the co-linear.
Four quarter spheres go from its far side to its own side, possibling and co-possibling in turn: eight triangles, all with it.
Its far side, 23, the vertex of the bi-inversioning universal cone. Its own side, 25, the co-recursioning cone.
The two cones cross in the circle between the two, about 24, a 0.
A turning about the co-linear rests at its two ends alone. Opened along it, the sphere is a torus: a going at no rest.
