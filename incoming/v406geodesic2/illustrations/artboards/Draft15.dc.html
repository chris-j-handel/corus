A conscious me: the other distinguished from the self and from the all
All is the universal all-parity of a my-universe. Other is the other-than of all, observing. Self is the other-than of an other-than of all, consciousness.
By nand alone the self is its all again. A podaling is an other-than, at a widest and at a vertex alike: the four moments are other, self, other, self.
Along the co-linear the all and the self are at one place of the turn and the other at the podal place, one fold apart.
Observing and distinguishing the other from the self and from the all is a conscious me: a me-now at 37, its others 23 and 83, its selves 97 and 157.
