The ten in four rows: the all, the self, all-parity and a my
A partner's ten, 0, 1, 2, 4, 3, 5, 7, 6, 8, 9, are four rows, of one, two, three and four. A multiple of 3 is an all, and a number other-than one an other-than.
The rows say: all; other-than, other-than; other-than, all, other-than; and other-than, all, other-than, all. They are the all, the self, all-parity and a my.
Four of the ten are an all and six an other-than, as of the ten passings four are a self and six an other.
