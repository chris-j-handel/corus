A parity is two is-or-is-nots, a + and a −: the two folds' circles through the co-linear. Round it, four quarters: a +, a + and a −, a −, none.
A fold is an other-than, a nand of a thing with itself. Observing is from none: a fold with a +, a fold with a −, and no fold again with the same.
Not both: a + and a − go through the co-linear to none, two folds in a row. The parity surfacing is a +, a −, or none.
With its prior, one is-or-is-not of four quarters. In its prior's own quarter, is: a 0 its giving, its prior its next prior. In the three others, is-not: one inversion.
From its prior, of the far side, to its next prior, of its own side: with is one fold, about the circle between, and with is-not three, through the 0.
