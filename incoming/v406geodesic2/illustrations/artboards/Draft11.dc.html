A me-now's four moments: other-than, all, other-than, all, and its next universe
Set a me-now at a prime scale of a me, among 0 to 60. A podaling about a cone's widest is an other-than, and a podaling about a vertex is an all.
About 30, about 60, about 90 and about 120, one and then the next, are 60 less it, 60 on, 120 less it and 120 on: a cone on with a moment,
and the fourth moment is next-universing. A me-now's four in its universe, one a cone, are its all-parity. The all between the two other-thans,
about 30, about 60 and about 90, is the all itself, about 60; the all ahead of the two or after them is about 0 or about 120, the universe's two ends.
The eight fours are the 32 places of 0 to 120 no spiral of 2, 3 or 5 crosses. Eight of the fourteen prime scales are in a four of four primes.
