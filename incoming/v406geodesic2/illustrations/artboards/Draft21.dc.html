Six orders of 3, 5 and 7, right spiraling: a living gyroscope
A partner's six orders are all the orders of three. The one line is three turns, a third of a turn on with a three; the other line is three folds, 3 for 7.
Both ways: the three go round on the triangle of three right angles, or an observing goes round them, across. The six are one round of six.
Round eight the three are alike, their multiplying the same with all six orders. Round ten it is with two alone, 357 and 753: the diamond turns about a 5 at its place.
That turning is the gyroscope: steady with all moments, and by one turn it says a number however far. Both closings of a number of no prime are past its horizon.
