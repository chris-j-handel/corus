The cube of 5: five selves on its line, and 120 about it
The cube of 5 is the shells 1, 7, 19, 37 and 61, a shell about the cube before it, from the corus. Their five corners are co-linear, along (1, 1, 1): all 5s.
About the line are 120 selves. By shells they are 6, 18, 36 and 60: to 6, to 24, to 60 and to 120. By rounds they are 24, 36, 36 and 24: to 24, to 60, to 96 and to 120.
Observed along the line the cube is the hexagon of 61, a place 5, 4, 3, 2 or 1 selves deep. A cube of 3, of 4 and of 5 is 24, 60 and 120 about its line.
0 to 120 by the four rounds is podal about 60, 24 with 24 and 36 with 36. Its primes are nine, eight, seven and six: six podal in a round, and 3, 2, 1 and none more.
The more are 2, 3 and 5, the spirals of 120 itself; 29 and 43, their podals 91 and 77 crossed by the spiral of 7; and 71, its podal 49, 7 by 7.
