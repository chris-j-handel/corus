A me-now podaling about 30 and about 90, and its next universe
A device a me sets to itself: set a me-now among 0 to 60, its own two cones. Podaling about 30 it is same-existing: 60 less it, in its own two cones.
Podaling about 90 it is next-universing: 180 less it, past 120. The two podalings, one and then the other, are 120 on: four turns of the one winding,
a me-now at its own place of the turn, in its next universe. The four are a parity, two is-or-is-nots. A podaling is two folds, an other-than of an other-than.
Along the co-linear one fold alone is observed, and the four are two. As 9 less and then 17 less is 8 up, about 30 and then about 90 is 120 on.
Three fours are four primes: 7, 53, 127 and 173; 17, 43, 137 and 163; 29, 31, 149 and 151. A me-now at 37 is with 23, 143 and 157.
