## A self has only its own others, and aims by one parity

A message reaches a far person through a society, each sender having only its own others and sending on to one it sees as closer: 64 of 296 letters in Travers and Milgram's study, 384 of 24,163 email chains in Dodds, Muhamad and Watts', a mean of 5.2 people between in the letters and about 4 steps in the email chains. No sender has a map of the society's ties. In Kleinberg's model such local sending finds short paths only as the long ties fall off with distance as the inverse square, a form of the arrangement and not of a sender's choosing.

**An exploring asks:** follow one parity going from a self to its own others, closer or not, from an existing connector relation, 6.12; and name the arrangement a finding goes with, as a stopping goes with one, 5.2, in place of a property of the selves.

## A chain ends as a sharing stops

In the email chains, under 0.3 percent of senders not sending on say they find no one to send to; a lack of interest or incentive, not a difficulty, is the main reason a chain ends, its authors suggest.

**An exploring asks:** observe a self's not-sharing as a parity, Natural Explaining 3.6's "its not-sharing is a parity, as its sharing is", and not as an instrument's failure to deliver; a refusing an instrument adds, 6.2, and a self's own not-sharing are two subjects.

## A heading uncovers in no self alone, with no governor

In a baboon troop, followers go with more initiators more readily; between two headings under about 90 degrees a follower goes between them, over it with one, more often the one more baboons go; a baboon's rank is-not going with others following it. In a honeybee swarm no bee compares two sites; scouts' dancing for a site empties with each return, a stop signal shares an is-not across, and a quorum at one site is the swarm's moment of moving.

**An exploring asks:** look for a heading or a choosing that uncovers between selves, Natural Naming 5.12's floating neutral, and observe no self as its governor; 5.2 names two of six stoppings: a hub, a parity becoming a magnitude, and a governor, a form fixing still from beside.

## A change in one goes with each, with no length of its own

In starling flocks the birds' velocity changes go together over a distance growing with the flock's size: a change in one bird goes with each other bird's, whatever the flock's size. In a school of golden shiners, an evading wave goes across a network of who sees whom, uneven from fish to fish, and fish with relatively few but strongly connecting fish around them both reach others most and others reach them most; a wave's reach is foreseeable, in large part, at its first fish.

**An exploring asks:** state no part as a whole: a going-together with no length of its own gives an instrument no distance to bound, and a part stating itself as the whole is a bound fixing as a last, 6.6's fourth ledger, which 6.6 names for an executing length; and follow the network of actual connections, who observes whom, in place of a distance or a number an instrument supplies.

## No one signal is whole, and a sequence is

In a model of bats leaving a roost together, with 100 bats about one in two of a bat's echoes from a wall a metre off go missing among the others' calls at each call, and a bat goes on by gathering echoes across calls, a sequence of partial echoes nearer whole than any one call.

**An exploring asks:** observe a sequence of parities, 6.4's "sequence holds interest", and no single reading as a self's whole state; a self's whole same-existing goes on as its own, 5.6, and only a parity goes across.

## An arrangement goes with a changing

Locust nymphs in a ring mill about at a low crowding, march together with whole-band changes of heading from about 25 per square metre, and go on in one heading above about 75. In laboratory corridors people in two opposite streams form lanes on their own, a lane forming, its authors give, with a person going close behind someone going the same way. Termites put clay down at places of evaporating most on a building's own curving, its authors propose; Grassé's stigmergy names each putting down going with what putting down already shapes.

**An exploring asks:** vary one thing at a time, Geodesic Improving Method 4.4, a crowding, an arrangement, a surface, and observe the changing it goes with, a parity of the selves' going on; and observe a surface carrying sharing, the building, as the selves' own artifact, not an instrument's store.

## A network's prior is its selves' own, and can go and come again

Bighorn herds people move into ranges whose earlier herds are gone go nearly 40 years before 80 percent migrate again; cranes straighten their route learning from older cranes; in a drought, most groups of the two elephant clans with older matriarchs go out of the park, perhaps with their matriarchs' experience of an earlier drought, its authors speculate; killer whale females past reproducing lead most in years with few salmon. Long voyaging by wayfinding, stopping between Hawaiʻi and Tahiti about the 14th century, comes again through a master navigator of Satawal teaching Nainoa Thompson.

**An exploring asks:** bring one same prior onward through each comparison, 6.5, as "a self a preparing leaves with no prior and a self with a prior already are of priors not same-existing", and Geodesic Improving Method 4.4, a prior emptying between two executings manufacturing a finding; and observe a network's navigating as its selves' prior same-existing, which a clearing removes, and which a self reaches again only as a new prior through further coupling, 6.12's reconnection restoring no prior.

## Two meet through a between by each other's sharing

Two crews digging toward each other through rock meet as a man's voice is heard through the last cubits; at Eupalinos each crew likely hears the other's hammering and turns with it, one reading among three.

**An exploring asks:** observe a coupling with an other as observing itself, 6.9's third observing of no position, "a coupling with an other of coefficient one … observing being coupling itself", and not a probe from outside.

## A society discovers as a self discovers

Calculus, natural selection and the telephone each come from two or more people apart within the same years or decades, the telephone's two coming apart still a dispute; in Merton's reading a discovering comes of a society's science and not of one self alone.

**An exploring asks:** observe a network's discovering as a same-existing-society's, Natural Exploring 6.1, and name a finding in no self alone only with its complete comparison, 6.5: separate goings on from one same prior.
