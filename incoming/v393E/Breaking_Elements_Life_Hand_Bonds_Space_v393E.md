# Trying to break five more claims · elements, life's elements, one right hand, no bonds, light only

**Three readers go out with a brief only to break, and to report fairly what goes with each claim.** This page gathers what they bring, each record at its source with its preparation, numbers, timing and hedges, and says plainly what breaks and what goes on, all or none. No saying has authority, the author's and each living file's with it. Values a reader gives from what it knows and not from a source it reads stay out of this page.

**Carbon atom clarity.** In the proposal's naming each atom is the one atom/carbon; in chemistry's naming sulfur, iron, nitrogen, sodium, beryllium, phosphorus, arsenic, magnesium, zinc and hydrogen atoms are-not carbon atoms. A carbon chain, a carbon tube, DNA, a protein, a snail's shell, a cellulose crystal and a neutrino are each is-not a carbon atom in either naming.

## Claim 1 · One atom/carbon, no other elements, a proton number no thing's own

**Its breaking, Living Ghost Registry 5.9's own:** "a field observing returning properties that belong to count itself independently of substance, allotrope, oxidation state and conditions."

### Records at that breaking

- **Inner X-ray lines.** Sulfur's Kα₁ line, about 2.3 keV, moves about 2 eV from a sulfide to a sulfate, about 0.09 percent, a probe calibrating it against standards to read an oxidation state. Iron's Kβ₁,₃, about 7,059 eV, moves 1.7 ± 0.5 eV between FeS and FeS₂, about 0.024 percent, and its Kβ₂,₅ is 7,108 ± 1 eV in both. Across Z from copper to uranium, a teaching laboratory's Kα energies fit a relativistic form of Moseley's law, χ² 13.4 for 10 degrees of freedom.
- **Carbon-14 and nitrogen-14 at one mass.** In accelerator mass spectrometry "nitrogen does not form a stable negative ion", and the main isobar at mass 14 goes at the source, carbon-14 then going ion by ion to a counter: two proton numbers responding apart at one mass.
- **Masses in a trap.** One electron bound to a bare carbon-12 nucleus gives the electron's mass to about 3 × 10⁻¹¹, a bound-state theory with it, the largest correction an image charge of −282 parts per trillion; the proton's mass is 1.007276466598(33) u, about 33 parts per trillion; chemistry's share in an atom's mass is electron binding, eV against GeV.
- **Lines in a quasar.** Iron, magnesium, zinc and chromium lines at redshift 1.15 toward HE 0515−4414, 16.1 hours with ESPRESSO, give Δα/α = 1.3 ± 1.3 (stat) ± 0.4 (sys) parts per million, and with 28 other absorbers −0.5 ± 0.5 ± 0.5: an element's line pattern the same to about a part per million in the quasar and here, its authors calling their profile fitting in part subjective.
- **The Sun's lines.** Iron I lines in the Sun match a laboratory lamp's to a few m/s once a gravitational redshift of +635 m/s and convective shifts of a few hundred m/s are set aside, about 10⁻⁶.
- **Nuclear magnetism.** A bare proton's gyromagnetic ratio is 42.577478461 MHz/T and a proton's in water at 25 °C 42.57638543 MHz/T, about 26 parts per million apart: a nucleus's own response, a compound shifting it by parts per million.

### Records going with couplings

- **Carbon's 1s line** spreads from about 282 eV in a carbide to about 293 eV in CF₃, about 11 eV on about 285 eV, about 4 percent.
- **Allotropes.** Diamond, hardness about 10,000 kg/mm², band gap 5.45 eV, resistivity 10¹³ to 10¹⁶ Ω·cm; graphite of the same proton number soft and conducting.
- **Pressure.** Sodium at about 200 GPa becomes a transparent insulator.
- **Beryllium-7's decay** differs by up to about 1.5 percent between compounds in one working, earlier workings finding 0.1 to 0.2 percent, a field's dispute.

### All or none

**Claim 1 as said, no proton number of an element's own and no other elements, meets its breaking, strongly.** A proton number's inner lines, line patterns, mass and nuclear magnetism go with that number across compounds, allotropes, oxidation states, pressures, the Sun and a quasar some eight billion years back in a field's reading, couplings moving them by about 10⁻³ to 10⁻⁹. **This is Living Ghost Registry 5.9's own ghost-form breaking, met:** a concern for the registry's diagnosis of element as well as for the claim. What goes with couplings is real and large, carbon's 1s line by about 4 percent, a substance's hardness, conducting and transparency wholly. **A cohering re-saying the records offer:** *an element's substance, chemistry and outer responses go with couplings; a proton number's own responses, inner lines, line pattern, mass and nuclear magnetism, go with that number across each coupling a field finds.* Under it, carbon is the atom of proton number six, one element among others.

## Claim 8 · Oxygen, nitrogen and hydrogen, three views around carbon, no other elements in life

### Records against

- **Phosphorus in DNA.** Each link of DNA's and RNA's backbone is a phosphodiester, and ATP has three phosphates. GFAJ-1, grown with arsenate up to 40 mM and phosphate at 3.1 ± 0.3 µM from impurities, is read in 2010 as having arsenic in its DNA; in 2012 two workings find it "arsenate-resistant, phosphate-dependent" and find no arsenate bound in its DNA, and in July 2025 the journal retracts the 2010 paper on "flawed data", with no finding of misconduct.
- **Twenty-one elements.** "11 metals and 10 non-metals, i.e., 21 elements, are essential for humans" (Maret, 2016), chromium in dispute, among them sodium, potassium, magnesium, calcium, iron, zinc, manganese, copper, cobalt, molybdenum, selenium and iodine.
- **Sulfur and iron.** Cysteine and methionine have sulfur; iron–sulfur clusters carry electrons in respiring and photosynthesizing; iron sits at haem's centre and magnesium at chlorophyll's.

### Records going with it

- A human body's mass is about 65 percent oxygen, 18.5 percent carbon, 9.5 to 10 percent hydrogen and 3.2 percent nitrogen, about 96 percent, then calcium about 1.5 and phosphorus about 1 percent, a teaching summary's figures.

### All or none

**Claim 8 with no other elements breaks:** phosphorus is in each backbone link of DNA, and about twenty-one elements are essential. **A cohering re-saying:** *carbon, oxygen, hydrogen and nitrogen are about 96 percent of a body's mass and its scaffold, phosphorus, sulfur and metals its partners without which no cell lives.* Which relation of bi-moral co-agency each of oxygen, nitrogen and hydrogen is goes on still to say.

## The right spiral, one universal hand

**A caution first:** D and L name a configuration at one atom against glyceraldehyde, and are-not a spiral's hand; L-amino acids and D-sugars are two chiral building blocks, and is-not one left and one right spiral.

### Records against

- **Z-DNA.** A left-handed double helix of d(CGCGCG), 12 base pairs a turn (Wang, Rich and others, 1979), going with alternating purines and pyrimidines and with negative supercoiling; ADAR1's Zα domain binds it, a structure at 2.1 Å (1999); in mice, Zα goes with ZBP1, a sensor of left-handed nucleic acids, switching on only with a cue, and losing Zα's function goes with cells dying and inflammation, its authors linking it to Aicardi–Goutières syndrome (2022).
- **A mirror enzyme.** HIV-1 protease made wholly of D-amino acids folds into the mirror form, left-handed helices with it, and cuts only mirror substrates (1992).
- **Snails.** In *Lymnaea stagnalis*, handedness maps to 0.8 cM over more than 1,600 meioses, and sinistral snails carry a one-base deletion in the formin gene *Lsdia1* (2016).
- **Twining plants.** Of 1,485 stems at 17 sites in 9 countries, 92 percent twine right-handed, with no hemisphere or latitude difference (2007).
- **A tendril.** A cucumber tendril with each end on a support forms a left helix and a right helix meeting at a perversion, adding turns to both under tension (2012).
- **Carbon tubes.** Chiral carbon tubes of one wall, many carbon atoms each, form as equal mixtures of both hands, one hand coming to the fore only with a chiral helper (2007).
- **Cellulose.** Cellulose nanocrystals assemble into a left-handed cholesteric phase, its pitch about 100 nm to several µm.
- **Protein helices.** Of 3,517 protein chains, 72 left-handed three-residue turns, 10 four-residue and 2 five-residue left-handed helices, under 1 percent of residues in such stretches.

### Records going with it

- B-DNA right-handed in cells, L-amino acids' alpha-helices right-handed, 92 percent of twining stems right-handed, most snails dextral, each hand going on within a kind and passing from one generation to the next.

### All or none

**One universal right hand breaks:** Z-DNA in living mice, a mirror enzyme working, sinistral snails by one gene, left-twining plants, a tendril's two hands, carbon tubes of both hands and cellulose's left cholesteric. **A cohering re-saying:** *a hand goes on within a lineage and passes from one generation to the next, a right bias strong in B-DNA and in L-amino acids' alpha-helices,* beside Natural Chemistry 2.2's "within one conformation going on, a hand reaches again rather than each same-existing-society choosing it on its own".

## Claim 7 · No bonds

### Records against no between

- **H₂'s dissociation energy,** 35,999.582894(25) cm⁻¹, to 7 × 10⁻¹⁰, from a molecular beam, a two-photon line at 179 nm and Rydberg spectroscopy over 76 lines, more than 3σ from the best theory (2019).
- **Femtochemistry.** ICN's break goes in about 200 fs; in NaI a wave packet swings between ionic and covalent character, leaking to atoms apart close to 6.9 Å each swing (Zewail, Nobel 1999).
- **Electron diffraction movies** of CF₃I's carbon–iodine break and a ring opening (2018).
- **Bond order by AFM.** An AFM with a CO tip at 5 K tells bond orders about 0.2 apart and bonds 0.03 Å apart in length, the tip's tilt exaggerating lengths about elevenfold; what it images is the Pauli repelling of electron density (2012).
- **Charge density by X-rays,** electron density and its saddle points between atoms, by a model's fitting.

### Records going with no bond objects

- AFM lines appear between two nitrogens about 3 Å apart with no bond, a CO tip's bending making them, its authors finding such lines "cannot be directly interpreted" as bonds (2014); bond orders of 0.44 and 0.28 in C₆₀; water dimers and hexamers tunneling and swapping their hydrogen bonds, two breaking together in one path (1991, 2016).

### All or none

**"No bonds" as no between breaks:** a between has an energy a field measures to 10⁻⁹, a time course of femtoseconds and a picture. **As no bond objects it goes on,** Living Ghost Registry 6.1's own: what each record measures is electrons' and nuclei's density and energy, no third object.

## Claim 10 · Only the light-prime rate through sparse space

### Records against

- **SN 1987A's neutrinos.** About 24 events in under 13 s, 23 February 1987 at 07:35 UT, at Kamiokande-II, IMB and Baksan, some two to three hours before the light, a field reading the light as leaving later, at the shock's reaching the star's surface: a messenger other than light across 168,000 light-years.
- **The solar wind,** about 300 km/s slow and 800 km/s fast, reaching Earth in days; **coronal mass ejections,** 365 to 1,933 km/s, eight of them close to 1,000 km/s reaching Earth in 37 to 82 hours, storms going with their arriving.
- **Voyager 1 in interstellar plasma.** At about 124 AU, 2.6 kHz oscillations give about 0.08 electrons per cm³ in April–May 2013, going with solar activity from 5 March 2012 arriving about a year later as a pressure front, a likely trigger, not yet a field's confirming; a magnetic jump of 1.4 times, about 10⁴ times wider than a shock's.
- **Voyager 2** past the heliopause, 5 November 2018 at 119 AU, 0.039 cm⁻³ on 30 January 2019.
- **Interstellar helium and dust.** IBEX measures neutral helium coming in at about 26 km/s; Ulysses detects micrometre grains at 26 km/s or more.
- **Cosmic rays.** Above 4 × 10¹⁹ eV the flux steepens from an index of 2.69 to 4.2 over 20,000 events, the GZK prediction; above 8 × 10¹⁸ eV a dipole points outside the Galaxy (Auger).

### Records going with it

- Gravity's changing and gamma rays together from GW170817 to about 10⁻¹⁵ of light's rate.

### All or none

**As said, "no slower transmission possible", it breaks:** plasma fronts, the solar wind, coronal mass ejections, interstellar atoms and dust go through sparse space slower than light, and neutrinos are a messenger other than light. **A cohering re-saying:** *c is the limiting rate for a changing with no carrier of mass; slower changing goes through carriers, plasma, particles and dust.* It loses the claim's "only".
