# Chemistry sorts by declaration, settles by observing

**Incoming gathering for session v392chem, toward a new version of Natural Chemistry (Exhibit SIXTEEN, v391).** This report is the front of the gathering. Its parts are the twenty-one notes files in [`parts/`](parts/), which travel whole beside it. Each part is cited here by its file name. `00_corus_lens.md` gives the corus understanding that every aim points into. `20_verification_pass.md` carries wherever it differs from another part.

Chemistry has no single way of dividing itself. Read as candidate universal sets, almost all of its divisions are gathered by a stated rule of membership. That includes its disciplines, registries, identity tiers, periodic groups, bond classes, reaction types, ordered series and scales. Binary Understanding puts a set gathered "by a saying about membership" on the **is-not** side of a universal set, and Resolving places such a set at **a between fixing as a cut**. Only a few criteria are observed outright: **counted proton number**, **the signs and ranks of measured responses under stated conditions**, and **sharp diffraction**. The rest are declared, or explained by a model and then cut by a declared threshold.

Chemistry describes cycling, sequencing and podaling with three different strengths of evidence:

- **Cycling.** A cycle's closing is nearly always inferred (turnover above one, plus mass balance) rather than watched.
- **Sequencing.** Order is observed only where a clock, a counter or a separation axis is built into the experiment. Elsewhere a model assigns it.
- **Podaling.** Pairs about a centre are exact by definition or symmetry, approximate with observable breaks, or conventional until a reference rule fixes them.

Unravelled down to its observings, chemistry's state–flow–state account rests on a short list:

- compositions in time, reached from both sides;
- path heats and works;
- measured differences;
- identity-tracking exchange at constant composition;
- net circulation in driven cycles.

Everything else is explaining or accounting: state functions, the equilibrium constant, chemical potential, the transition state, rate constants and detailed balance. Nothing in the gathered record shows a literal rest, which answers Natural Chemistry 1.5's open question as it is written.

The frontier notes and the disputes in note 11 hold about 250 binaries. They split along one line:

- **Observing binaries** close by replication on a second, independent platform.
- **Explaining binaries** close only when a criterion or partition is stated, and many of them dissolve rather than resolve once it is.

I propose resolving first the binaries that a living Natural Chemistry sentence already leans on, and that a coming observing can decide:

- prenucleation clusters and graded nuclei, against 4.7's "all or none";
- the water liquid–liquid critical point, against 4.2;
- the measured parity-violating energy difference and the sign of the homochiral seed, against 2.3, now that the 2026 Nobel has settled amplification in the laboratory;
- reproduction of the 2026 self-synthesising ribozyme;
- direction by kinetic asymmetry rather than a power stroke, against the "single catalytic stroke" ghost.

Nothing gathered here is ready to enter a living file as written. Several parts opened no web page at all, the verification pass corrects five other parts, and Natural Chemistry v391 itself carries stale pointers, a reversed use of "podal", and citations that now belong in its Carryings.

## Divisions are declared sets, and few cut where nature cuts

### The institutional and registry partitions disagree and are all conventions

Each major institution divides chemistry differently, and none of the divisions coincide (`01_divisions_identity_classification.md`):

- **IUPAC** has eight divisions ([IUPAC](https://iupac.org/who-we-are/divisions/)).
- **The ACS** has 32 technical divisions, joined by self-enrolment ([ACS directory](https://www.acs.org:443/content/dam/acsorg/about/governance/governance-directory.pdf)).
- **Chemical Abstracts** has 80 sections in five groups. Each abstract goes into exactly one section, chosen by its novelty, with cross-references to others ([CAS](https://www.cas.org/support/documentation/references/ca-sections)). In 2010 section sizes ran from 240 to 73,273 abstracts, and about 80% of assignments matched the authors' own preference ([Bornmann et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.7498)).
- **The RSC** has 72 interest groups and eight communities ([RSC](https://www.rsc.org/membership/benefits/community-and-networking?e=1)).

Principles are mixed even inside one scheme. Sections can be built on a compound class, a phenomenon, a method or an industry: Fluorine, Rubber and Law all stand side by side. **Systems chemistry, origins-of-life chemistry and mechanochemistry are divisions in none of the four schemes.** That absence is an observing. It matters for this expedition because Natural Chemistry's own frame (sharing, inversioning, tunnelling, surfacing, same-existing, inseparating) cuts across every institutional cut. Read as candidate universal sets, every one of these partitions is gathered by an institutional rule. They are installations, not discoveries.

Registries sharpen the point.

- **PubChem** (12 September 2024) listed 118,596,691 compounds and 322,395,335 substances ([NAR Table 1](https://academic.oup.com/view-large/499326936)). The ratio of about 2.7 substances per compound is produced by PubChem's merging rules. It is not a fact about matter.
- **The CAS Registry** passes 290 million ([CAS](https://www.cas.org/cas-data/cas-registry)).
- **ChEBI** holds over 195,000 entries and keeps chemical roles apart from structural classes ([ChEBI](https://ebi.ac.uk/chebi/about)).
- **ClassyFire/ChemOnt** fixes 4,825 SMARTS-defined categories up to eleven levels deep, and has been static since 2016 ([Mungall et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.18470)).

Each registry is a set whose membership a stated rule decides. Each also re-forms only when its curators re-declare it, which is the opposite of a universal set whose membership "re-forms with each now".

### Identity tiers already carry a timescale, which is chemistry's nearest approach to a re-forming membership

IUPAC's identity tiers, and their kinds of criterion, are the most useful single finding for the species question that `00_corus_lens.md` leaves open (`01_divisions_identity_classification.md`):

- **Molecular entity.** Distinguished by constitution or isotopic make-up "at the precision the purpose needs" ([Gold Book M03986](https://goldbook.iupac.org/M03986.html)). This is a declared criterion grounded on a measured property.
- **Chemical species.** A population of entities with access to the same set of energy levels within the time of the experiment ([Gold Book CT01038](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/CT01038)). **Conformers count as separate species when NMR resolves them, and as one species in a slow reaction.** This is a declared criterion with an explained dial: the experiment's own timescale sets the membership.
- **Substance.** Defined by constant composition, a measured property. It bends for Berthollides, polymers, UVCBs and alloys.
- **Element.** Has two senses: a kind of atom fixed by proton number, and a simple substance ([Gold Book C01022](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/C01022)). These echo Paneth's basic substance and simple substance, and Scerri's reading of them ([HYLE](https://hyle.org/journal/issues/11-2/scerri.htm)). The 1923 IUPAC decision tied the table's positions to Z, a choice Needham and Hendry still dispute over isotopes ([SEP Philosophy of Chemistry, rev. 11 March 2026](https://plato.stanford.edu/ENTRIES/chemistry)).

Natural kinds in chemistry also cross-cut: benzyl alcohol is both an alcohol and aromatic ([SEP Natural Kinds](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/natural-kinds)).

The aim into corus is direct. No living file defines species. Binary Understanding places a set gathered "by a saying about membership" on the is-not side of a universal set ([BU L75–L90](/root/corus/incoming/v391/Binary_Understanding_v391.md#L75)), and Resolving answers natural kinds (HPR 16) and species boundaries (HPR 20) as **a between fixing as a cut** ([RES 10.5](/root/corus/Exhibit_TWENTY-TWO_Resolving_the_Hard_Problem_Registry_v391.md#L3257), [RES 10.20](/root/corus/Exhibit_TWENTY-TWO_Resolving_the_Hard_Problem_Registry_v391.md#L3407)). A registry species is therefore a cut. The IUPAC chemical species is a cut too, but its experiment-time clause makes membership relative to an observing self's own pace. That is the field's own concession that its kinds are cut by a self.

**My inference:** a chemical species is a same-existing-society, and so a self ("A same-existing-society is a self, so it is a me" ([ME L241](/root/corus/Exhibit_THIRTY-ONE_Me_v391.md#L241))), only where its members actually bi-couple. A hydrogen-bond network, a crystal lattice and a micelle qualify. A bottle of dilute solute whose molecules never meet shares the now only through the universe's tri-bi-co-momentarying ([NN 5.52](/root/corus/Exhibit_TWENTY_Natural_Naming_v391.md#L1482)), not as a society. A new Natural Chemistry could say this in one sentence and leave the population rung of Societies reaching, as Societies already does ([SOC L229–L320](/root/corus/Exhibit_SEVEN_Natural_Societies_v391.md#L229)). Three regularities run through the table.

**First, the strongest naturalist anchor is atomic number.** It is counted, it fixes the element, and the end of the table is still model-dependent beyond it (`02_periodic_order_elements.md`). The strongest conventionalist cases are the metalloid lists, the electronegativity cutoffs, the choice of acid–base scheme, the institutional divisions and the ontologies.

### Rules are scoped regularities whose explanations move while their observings stay

Database tests show how far the classical rules reach (`03_rules_bonding_structure.md`):

- **Pauling's rules.** Only 13% of about 5,000 oxides satisfy rules two to five jointly, though rule one fits 66% ([George et al.](https://research.dial.uclouvain.be/handle/2078.5/93572)). The defence is that the radii are conventions and should be re-stated on electron density ([Gibbs et al. 2022](https://msaweb.org/MSA/AmMin/TOC/2022/Abstracts/AM107P1219.pdf)).
- **Goldschmidt's tolerance factor** is right for 74% of 576 ABX₃ compounds and 33% of iodides. Bartel's τ reaches 92% ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.07700v1.pdf)).
- **Hume-Rothery's size criteria** are necessary but not sufficient ([npj](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s41524-020-0308-7)).

Recent "breaks" move boundaries rather than abolish rules. Anti-Bredt olefins have been trapped ([2024](https://www.spectroscopyonline.com/view/scientists-break-century-old-barrier-to-synthesizing-anti-bredt-olefins)). A 20-electron ferrocene was made in 2025 ([OIST](https://www.oist.jp/node/46139)). An inverted singlet–triplet gap of −11 meV was reported ([Aizawa 2022](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v609y2022i7927d10.1038_s41586-022-05132-y.html)), disputed, and answered. In M(CO)₈ a single IR band is read three ways ([Wu 2018](https://www.cup.lmu.de/ac/kluefers/homepage/L/kc2/literature/pse2-m8co-2018.pdf); [Landis 2019](https://www.cup.lmu.de/ac/kluefers/homepage/L/kc2/literature/pse2-m8co-comment-2019.pdf)).

**Hund's first rule keeps its observing while its explanation has reversed** ([QMC](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0408147)). Wade's n+1 outlives its rationale ([Hoffmann 2003](https://old.roaldhoffmann.com/sites/all/files/489s_0.pdf)). Pauli exclusion is a postulate with a VIP-2 bound ([Entropy 2020](https://www.openaccessrepository.it/record/73490/files/entropy-22-01195-v2.pdf?download=1)).

Reactivity rules come in pairs whose selecting condition belongs inside the criterion (`04_rules_reactivity_thermo_kinetics_series.md`). Their breaks fall into four kinds: a violated precondition, loose wording, a linear rule meeting curvature, and an external field or history. Examples:

- Le Chatelier fails, for instance once the N₂ mole fraction passes 0.5 ([Corti & Franses](https://journals.flvc.org/cee/article/download/122716/121755)).
- Curtin–Hammett fails under conformer-specific tunnelling ([Nat. Chem. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nchem.2609)).
- Woodward–Hoffmann selection inverts under force, and the allowed transition state vanishes above 1.4 nN ([Germann & Meisner 2025](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2025/sc/d5sc04954g)).
- The triboelectric series is set by contact history ([Nature 2025](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v638y2025i8051d10.1038_s41586-024-08530-6.html)).

**Force is therefore a third inverter of pericyclic selection, beside heat and light.** That bears on Natural Chemistry 2.4.

## Cycling, sequencing and podaling are described with three different strengths of evidence

### Cycling is inferred closure, and only driven cycles have a direction

`07_cycling.md` separates six senses of "cycle":

- catalytic;
- metabolic and biogeochemical;
- accounting (Hess);
- engine;
- temporal recurrence;
- conformational or mechanical.

The loop itself is rarely observed. What is observed are proxies:

- turnover number above one;
- resting states;
- period-four flash yields in photosystem II ([Shinkarev](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00029473));
- F1-ATPase rotation filmed directly ([Noji 1997](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v386y1997i6622d10.1038_386299a0.html));
- colour swings in oscillators;
- isotopic scrambling such as C¹⁶O¹⁸O.

**Returning has two senses**: material identity, where the same carrier comes back, and configurational identity, where a state of the same kind comes back. What does not return is the fuel's waste, the side products, the heat and the entropy. Cycle intervals run from 10⁻⁵ s for a ring flip to years for stratospheric chlorine. Industrial cycles nest: in the Wacker process, palladium is re-oxidised by copper and copper by oxygen.

The formal backbone is the most important aim in this section.

- **Detailed balance forbids net circulation around any closed loop at equilibrium.** That is Wegscheider's condition, in which the product of the equilibrium constants around the loop equals one.
- **A driven cycle needs reservoirs at different chemical potential, and its direction comes from kinetic asymmetry** ([Astumian 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6707331)).
- Note 11 adds that chemistry represents "kept changing by a flow" as a cycle whose forward and reverse legs differ chemically, so the cycle's ratio K_r is not one (`11_nonequilibrium_flow_driven.md`). For a fuelled assembly, K_r ≈ 1.6×10¹⁰, about 58 kJ/mol (Ragazzon & Prins, [manuscript](https://www.research.unipd.it/bitstream/11577/3286551/1/manuscript.pdf)).

These line up exactly with the Equilibria Registry's chemistry row:

- **NY29**, "All three reactions of the closed A ⇌ B ⇌ C ⇌ A cycle balance with their own reverses, at positive fixed total and consistent constants satisfying K₁K₂K₃=1", is Wegscheider's condition, entered as a rate fixing as a value.
- **NY30**, "All reactions in A+F ⇌ B+W, B ⇌ C, C ⇌ A balance under the specified unit ratios and positive maintained f=w", is the unfuelled limit of a driven cycle. The registry's own proof says "That balance is only with f=w" (EQ 5.3, L927).

The gathering supplies field observings of the f ≠ w side:

- the 2022 autonomous rotary motor, with a 71:29 directional bias and over 97% of fuel passing through the motor cycle ([Borsley 2022](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v604y2022i7904d10.1038_s41586-022-04450-5.html));
- 85.7–89.5% enantiomeric excess from a fuelled ratchet ([Berreur 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12328200));
- broken detailed balance measured as net probability flux in flagella and cilia ([Battle 2016](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1427814)).

These are observings in the field's own words. They show that net circulation exists only where a reservoir difference is maintained. NY29 and NY30 rest on that, and Natural Chemistry 5.1's "natural energy is an engine, a cycle cycling" can lean on it without importing equilibria words as what nature does.

Steady-state efficiency is described by four formalisms (`07_cycling.md`):

- turnover number as initial turnover frequency × half-life / ln 2 ([Steven 2025](https://public-pages-files-2025.frontiersin.org/journals/catalysis/articles/10.3389/fctls.2025.1720991/pdf));
- the energetic span model ([Kozuch & Shaik](https://cris.iucc.ac.il/en/publications/how-to-conceptualize-catalytic-cycles-the-energetic-span-model/));
- Hill's cycle kinetics;
- degree of rate control. This was reopened in 2025: the kinetic degrees sum to +1 and the thermodynamic to −1, so the total is zero even in transients, and earlier proofs were incomplete ([Bamidele](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/3009658)).

Oscillating reactions were resisted on second-law grounds until the mid-1960s ([Zhabotinsky & Rossi](https://www.witpress.com/Secure/ejournals/papers/ECO0104001f.pdf)). Chemistry distinguishes the limit cycle from Lotka's neutral orbits. Turing patterns have been observed since CIMA in 1990 ([Front. Phys.](https://frontiersin.org/journals/physics/articles/10.3389/fphy.2024.1358766/full)).

The aim into corus is to torusing and four-cycling:

- A catalytic cycle that returns its carrier through one opening, the active site, is a torusing.
- A nested industrial cycle returns by a long way round through other cycles, which reads closer to corusing ([NN L1082](/root/corus/Exhibit_TWENTY_Natural_Naming_v391.md#L1082)).
- The photosystem II S-state counter, with its period of four, is chemistry's own observed four-cycling. That is a candidate for the "six to go on with" style openings at Natural Chemistry 3.1 and 4.5, which the parts do not otherwise fill.

### Sequencing is observed only where the experiment carries a clock

`08_sequencing.md` finds that a mechanism is an ordered list of mostly unobserved steps, and that "elementary" rests on negative evidence. IUPAC's control function makes rate control a sensitivity that moves with conditions ([Gold Book R05139](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/R05139)). Several common readings of order turn out to be weaker than they look:

- Post-transition-state bifurcations put one transition state before many products ([PAC 2017](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/pac-2017-0104/html)).
- **The intrinsic reaction coordinate is not a trajectory.**
- Steady-state and pre-equilibrium approximations give the same rate-law form, so the form alone cannot order the steps.
- The third explosion limit of hydrogen with oxygen needs nonlinear chains ([Liang & Law](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/an-analysis-of-the-explosion-limits-of-hydrogenoxygen-mixtures-wi/)).

Synthesis is planned backward and executed forward ([Nobel 1990](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1990/ceremony-speech/)), and even step counting is disputed ([Chemistry World](https://www.chemistryworld.com/opinion/what-counts-as-a-step-during-chemical-synthesis/2500261.article)). Template copying gives dispersity one, while copolymer sequence follows a Mayo–Lewis Markov model.

Order is observed where a clock, a counter or a separation axis is part of the experiment:

- Time-resolved spectroscopy records a signal against a delay.
- Sanger, Edman, mass-spectrometric ladders and nanopores decode a physical order into symbols.
- PCR counts its cycles as 2ⁿ.

Elsewhere a model assigns order. **Madelung filling order and ionisation order differ**: scandium is written [Ar]3d¹4s² yet loses 4s first (`02_periodic_order_elements.md`).

Stage orders such as nucleation then growth, Ostwald's step rule and the folding funnel are models. One 2026 observing bears directly on Natural Chemistry. Atomic electron tomography (Yuan et al., Nature Materials) shows order fading gradually toward a nucleus's edge, against classical nucleation theory's sharp interface (`08_sequencing.md`). That stands against 4.7's "each emerging is sharp, all or none", and it is short-listed below.

The aim is to the seventeen names' prior, now and next. A sequence a field observes with its own clock is a co-chaining, "that same going on with its along relation" ([NN L390](/root/corus/Exhibit_TWENTY_Natural_Naming_v391.md#L390)). A sequence a model assigns is an accounting. Natural Chemistry 6.3's "phase-sequencing, three-then-progress" should say which of the two it is.

### Podaling: exact pairs come from definition or symmetry, and approximate pairs break observably

`09_podaling_pairs_dualities.md` finds four kinds of centre:

- an exchanged unit, such as the proton between conjugate acid and base;
- a symmetry element, such as a mirror plane;
- a spectral or energy midpoint;
- a path midpoint, such as a transition state.

Some pairs are exact by definition. Conjugate acid–base pairs are defined by the exchanged proton ([Gold Book C01266](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/C01266/plain)). Reductive elimination is defined as the reverse of oxidative addition ([Gold Book R05223](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/R05223/plain)).

Other pairs are approximate and break observably:

- The Coulson–Rushbrooke pairing of Hückel orbitals breaks beyond Hückel and PPP models ([Green & Hele 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.09013)).
- Mirror nuclei ⁷¹Kr and ⁷¹Br have different ground states (2025, [IFIC](https://news.pcuv.es/en/home-pcuv-institutos-pcuv-ific-led-experiment-challenges-one-principles-nuclear-physics)).
- Hoogsteen pairs are populated at 0.08–2.73% ([Alvey](https://link.springer.com/10.1038/ncomms5786)).
- Only six of eight quasiracemates pack as expected ([Wells](https://pubs.rsc.org/os/content/getauthorversionpdf/D1CE01214B)).

Conventional pairs need a reference rule. D/L was a convention until Bijvoet's 1951 confirmation of absolute configuration. **B-DNA and Z-DNA are diastereomers, both built on D-deoxyribose; the true mirror of B-DNA is L-DNA.** That corrects the observing at Natural Chemistry 2.2.

The aim into corus is Naming's and Numbers' podal and straddle:

- "a pair is a podal, 17 less within 1 to 16, and an even number's straight-across, k and k + N/2, is another relation" ([NN 5.45](/root/corus/Exhibit_TWENTY_Natural_Naming_v391.md#L1384));
- "Podal competency is a straddle and not a pairing" ([NUM L474](/root/corus/Exhibit_THREE_Natural_Numbers_v391.md#L474));
- "A nearest straddle is by one" ([NUM L122](/root/corus/Exhibit_THREE_Natural_Numbers_v391.md#L122)).

## State–flow–state rests on five observings, and the rest is accounting

### What is observed and what is constructed

`10_state_flow_equilibrium_kinetics.md` shows that instruments record path quantities or differences. Calorimetry gives heats along a path. EMF, vapour pressure and osmotic pressure give differences. State functions exist only at equilibrium, and only as a model premise, which is why Hess cycles close (`04_rules_reactivity_thermo_kinetics_series.md`).

The declared zeros are of three kinds:

- **reference floors**: the formation enthalpy and Gibbs energy of elements, the SHE at 0 V at every temperature, and p° = 1 bar ([Gold Book S05917](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/S05917));
- **a theoretical floor**: Planck's S(0) = 0;
- **a re-declared unit**: the 2019 SI, which moved the kelvin's definition with no practical effect ([RSC AMC](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2019/ay/c9ay90028d)).

Single-ion activity is unmeasurable. The absolute SHE potential of 4.44 V is extra-thermodynamic, average bond energies are conventions, and lattice energy cannot be measured (`06_properties_scales_observables.md`).

Ice shows the gap between theory and observing precisely. Its residual entropy was measured at 0.82 ± 0.05 in 1936, against Pauling's R ln(3/2) = 0.806, Nagle's 0.81480 and simulation's 0.81550 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0609211)). **Theory is about two orders of magnitude more precise than the observing it explains.**

Equilibrium itself unravels into an observing and its explanation. Guldberg and Waage observed a limiting composition reached from both sides; the opposing "forces" were posited ([translation](https://web.chemteam.info/Chem-History/Concerning-Affinity.html)). "Dynamic equilibrium" (Williamson 1850) is observed only with identity-tracking markers:

- radiolead exchange (Hevesy and Paneth 1913, [lecture](https://nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/hevesy-lecture.pdf));
- the exchange current I₀ ([Gold Book E02238](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/E02238/plain));
- fluorescence correlation spectroscopy (1972, [Physics Today](https://physicstoday.aip.org/news/fluctuations-at-chemical-equilibrium));
- EXSY (1979);
- single-molecule FRET (2003, [NSB](https://www.nature.com/articles/nsb883)).

At constant composition, exchange goes on in every system tested. That is the gathered answer to Natural Chemistry 1.5's open, "a chemical process whose stability has a literal rest rather than opposing changings going on, and record returns none": the record still returns none. It also supports the Living Ghost Registry's "equilibrium as rest" ghost.

Detailed balance is stronger than stationarity, and net probability flux breaks it (Battle 2016). Onsager reciprocity for reactions is thin. Tests were meagre in 1960 and inconclusive in 1965 ([Caltech](https://thesis.caltech.edu/135/)), with one confirmation in 2011 ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.1056)). **Natural Chemistry 1.5 cites Onsager's L_ij = L_ji as an observing; for chemical reactions the field's own observing record is one experiment.**

The transition state, at about 10⁻¹³ s, is a model construct. Its "observations" are assignments through simulated surfaces, whether by femtochemistry ([Nobel 1999](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1999/press-release/)), anion photodetachment ([Neumark](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2005/cp/b417886f)) or the 2023 F + NH₃ work ([Nat. Chem.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s41557-022-01100-1)). The 1913 Michaelis–Menten constant was Vmax/Km, not today's Km ([Johnson & Goody](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3381512)).

Chemistry conflates three things in its own vocabulary:

- "Mass action" names two laws.
- Dynamic equilibrium is filed under steady state.
- "Equilibrium" has three referents: thermodynamic, stationary and regulatory.

A 2023 student survey shows the cost ([Leicester](https://journals.le.ac.uk/index.php/new-directions/article/download/3900/3677/13531)). Up to 48% confuse "constant" with "equal", and up to 83% take a closed system as a feature of equilibrium. Note 10's cleanest discriminator is operational: **isolate the system; an equilibrium persists while a steady state relaxes.**

Note 11 gathers the non-equilibrium side. Minimum entropy production holds only in a linear model. Maximum entropy production is several principles and has retreated to an inference algorithm ([Dewar](https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/publications/maximum-entropy-production-as-an-inference-algorithm-that-transla/)). The thermodynamic uncertainty relation is proved for Markov jump steady states and fails as a universal law ([Barato & Seifert](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05944v2)). Kinetic proofreading lowers errors from e^(−βΔ) to e^(−2βΔ) ([Brown & Sivak](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.09180)). **Positive observings settle these disputes and negative ones do not, and one word ("power stroke", "drive", "MEP") often carries several criteria.**

## What the gathering adds that the project did not have

Chemistry's own definitions already concede what the corus understanding asks of a universal set, though only partway. The IUPAC chemical species is gathered by a declared criterion, but its membership turns on the experiment's own timescale: interconverting conformers are one species or two depending on who is looking and how fast. A new Natural Chemistry can therefore stop treating chemical kinds as either natural or conventional and say what the field itself does. The second novelty is structural. The Equilibria Registry's chemistry row and the Hard Problem Registry's chemistry resolutions have been built from opposite ends. The row is two columns of formal reaction networks; the resolutions are twelve dissolvings by fixings. - exchange at constant composition, observed by tracers, FCS, EXSY and smFRET;
- net circulation only where a reservoir difference is kept, observed in fuelled motors and flagella.

Entering those first, before the frontier binaries, would let NY29 and NY30 stand on observing rather than proof alone. It would also turn the frontier short list from a set of disputes into tests against sentences the living file already makes.
