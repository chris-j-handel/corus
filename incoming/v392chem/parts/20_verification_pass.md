## Life's chemistry (notes 17)

- **The 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** was awarded to Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai for discovering nonlinear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis. It was announced on 7 October 2026.
  - The committee chair, Heiner Linke, presented it as an answer to how homochirality can emerge spontaneously. The press reports seen do not tie the prize to the origin of life itself.
  - **Observing:** a small excess of one hand in a catalyst or product is amplified in the next products. Kagan found this as nonlinear effects in asymmetric catalysis; Soai found it as asymmetric autocatalysis.
  - **Explaining added:** that such amplification accounts for life's single hand.
  - **Aim:**
    - homochirality binaries F10–F12, on whether the sign is set universally or locally;
    - Natural Chemistry 2.2–2.3 (chirality; a lean that violates parity);
    - Hard Problem Registry entry 63, Homochirality.
  - **Binary standing:** the prize recognizes amplification. Amplification is settled in the laboratory. The origin of the sign is still open.
  - **Sources:**
    - [Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2026](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2026/)
    - [Al Jazeera, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/10/7/henri-kagan-kenso-soai-win-chemistry-nobel-for-mirror-image-breakthrough)
    - [SCMP](https://scmp.com/news/world/europe/article/3370059/nobel-chemistry-prize-awarded-henri-b-kagan-and-kenso-soai)
- **QT45 polymerase ribozyme (F1).** The paper is Gianni and colleagues, Holliger laboratory, MRC LMB, in *Science*. The astrobiology.com reprint of the abstract is dated 16 February 2026, which corrects "2024" in notes 17.
  - **Observing:** a 45-nucleotide RNA was selected from about a trillion random sequences and then evolved. In eutectic ice it made its complementary strand and itself, in two separate reactions. It took 72 days to reach a 0.2% yield.
  - **Explaining:** the authors call this self-synthesis, not self-replication, because the two steps do not yet run one after the other in one pot. They read the result as suggesting that small replicases may be more common in sequence space than expected. That reading is an inference. The structure shown is an AlphaFold3 prediction.
  - **Standing:** narrowing. It has not been reproduced, and full self-replication has not been shown.
  - **Sources:**
    - [MRC LMB news](https://mrclmb.ac.uk/?p=14716)
    - [Chemistry World](https://www.chemistryworld.com/news/self-synthesising-rna-strand-offers-clues-to-lifes-origins/4022966.article)
    - [Europe PMC author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618777)
    - [astrobiology.com, 16 Feb 2026](https://astrobiology.com/2026/02/16/a-small-polymerase-ribozyme-that-can-synthesize-itself-and-its-complementary-strand/)
- **Bennu soluble organics (F12; notes 18 B7).** Glavin and colleagues, *Nature Astronomy* 2025, report abundant ammonia and nitrogen-rich soluble organic matter. The amino acids are reported racemic, as already in notes 17.
  - **Source:** [Penn State record](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/abundant-ammonia-and-nitrogen-rich-soluble-organic-matter-in-samp/)

## Catalysis, Earth and ocean (notes 15 B21; notes 18)

- **Dark oxygen.** The claim is Sweetman and colleagues, *Nature Geoscience*, July 2024, from benthic chambers in the Clarion–Clipperton Zone.
  - **Observing:** oxygen rose inside chambers over nodule-bearing sediment, and voltages were measured across nodule surfaces.
  - **Explaining:** water is electrolysed at nodule surfaces.
  - **Critiques:**
    - The Metals Company submitted a rebuttal. It was posted on EarthArXiv and was under review as of January 2025.
    - A published critique by P. Downes (The Metals Company) was presented at Seabed Minerals 2026, Bergen, 24–26 March 2026. It argues three points. Comparable chamber studies record only oxygen consumption. Measured voltages fall well below what electrolysis needs. Controls point to an artefact of the measurement system, and no co-produced hydrogen was reported.
  - **Replication:** a three-year expedition led by Sweetman and funded by the Nippon Foundation was due to sail in January 2026, to test replication and microbial contributions.
  - **Standing (March 2026):** open, contested and unreplicated. No retraction is known.
  - **Sources:**
    - [geo365, 23–24 Mar 2026](https://geo365.no/the-dark-oxygen-controversy)
    - [Smithsonian, 2025](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/scientists-who-found-mysterious-dark-oxygen-on-the-ocean-floor-plan-a-new-expedition-hoping-to-settle-disputes-180985889/)
    - [Nippon Foundation initiative](https://tnfsa.nippon-foundation.or.jp/news/13238305)

## Water and interfaces (notes 12)

- **Mpemba effect in water.** Janni, Botero Ampudia and Dahlberg (University of Minnesota) published in an RSC journal, online 16 January 2026.
  - **Observing:** they ran 176 samples in 25 trials, 35 mL each, in a chest freezer at about −14 to −17 °C, logging thermocouples every 2 s. A hotter sample reached 0 °C first in 46 cases and reached the freezing plateau first in 58. About half the samples supercooled.
  - **Explaining:** they attribute the first kind of event to uncontrolled air convection in the freezer, and the second to variable supercooling. Cooling otherwise follows Newton's law.
  - **Standing:** the effect occurs conditionally and is not intrinsic to water. This agrees with Burridge and Linden 2016, who found no evidence of a genuine effect in controlled cooling to 0 °C.
  - **Sources:**
    - [RSC 2026, d5lf00296f](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/lf/d5lf00296f)
    - [Burridge & Linden 2016](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5121640/)
- **Microdroplet H2O2.** The claim is spontaneous formation at the air–water interface (Zare group, from 2019).
  - **Negation side:**
    - Eatoo and Mishra, arXiv 2505.21175, 27 May 2025, argue "no O2, no H2O2". In their account H2O2 comes from reducing dissolved O2 at solid–water interfaces, consistent with the halide order of pitting corrosion. Aluminium and titanium surfaces produce more H2O2 under acidic and alkaline conditions respectively. Their measurements were NMR, potentiodynamic polarization, electron microscopy and a fluorometric assay.
    - An earlier "busting the myth" paper made the same point about solid contacts and dissolved O2 (PMC10901496, 2024).
  - **Standing:** contested, and narrowing toward explanations through solid contact and dissolved O2. Proponents still argue for interfacial electric fields.
  - **Sources:**
    - [arXiv 2505.21175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21175v1)
    - [PMC10901496](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10901496/)
    - [Chemistry World overview](https://chemistryworld.com/4018721.article)
- **Anomalous underscreening.** Skarmoutsos and Mossa, *J. Chem. Phys.* 2025, doi:10.1063/5.0258084, ran molecular dynamics of LiBF4 in ethylene carbonate.
  - **Observing (simulation):** two length scales appear. The screening length shrinks with concentration, while ionic clusters grow.
  - **Explaining:** the long decay measured in surface-force experiments is attributed to the cluster scale, not to a longer electrostatic screening length.
  - **Standing:** open. The disputes are over whether the long decay is real and how far it reaches, and the question is now reframed around clusters.
  - **Source:** [CEA highlight, 22 Sep 2025](https://irig.cea.fr/drf/irig/english/Pages/News/Highlights/2025/30-Stefano-Mossa.aspx)

## Phases and crystallization (notes 16)

- **The seventh crystal-structure-prediction blind test** was published as Hunnisett and colleagues, *Acta Crystallographica B* 80 (December 2024), covering structure generation, with a companion paper on structure ranking (22 groups).
  - It had seven targets, including a large, highly polymorphic drug candidate.
  - Many methods found the observed structure of a small flexible agrochemical.
  - Ranking is still the hard step: free-energy corrections did not reliably improve it, and success on complex systems was limited. Two groups predicted disorder for the first time.
  - **Source:** [Cambridge Network, 21 Oct 2024](https://www.cambridgenetwork.co.uk/news/overcoming-pharmaceutical-and-other-materials-issues-they-exist)

## Materials and superconductivity (notes 19; notes 13 B22)

- **Room-temperature superconductivity claims (Dias).**
  - Nature published the CSH paper on 15 October 2020 and retracted it in 2022.
  - It published the N-doped lutetium hydride paper on 1 March 2023 and retracted it on 7 November 2023, at the request of eight co-authors.
  - PRL retracted a related paper on 15 August 2023.
  - A University of Rochester investigation found research misconduct (reported 20 March 2024). Dias was no longer employed there by 19 November 2024.
  - By 2025, five papers had been retracted and five carried expressions of concern.
  - **Standing:** settled as retracted. The binary's claim side is withdrawn.
  - **Sources:**
    - [Wikipedia, with primary notices cited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranga_P._Dias)

- **Ambient-pressure superconductivity in La3Ni2O7 films.**
  - "Signatures of ambient pressure superconductivity in thin film La3Ni2O7", *Nature* 638 (2025), doi:10.1038/s41586-024-08525-3 (Stanford). A further report concerns compressively strained (La,Pr)3Ni2O7 films (APS March Meeting 2025).
  - **Observing:** resistive transitions and diamagnetic signatures at ambient pressure in films under compressive strain from the substrate.
  - **Explaining:** strain stands in for the hydrostatic pressure under which bulk La3Ni2O7 superconducts near 80 K.
  - **Standing:** narrowing as an observing. The mechanism is open.
  - **Sources:**
    - [IDEAS/RePEc record of the Nature paper](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v638y2025i8052d10.1038_s41586-024-08525-3.html)

  - **Standing:** the claim of novelty is contested for many of the 41 compounds. This is an observing dispute: what the diffraction patterns show.
  - **Source:** [Chemistry World](https://chemistryworld.com/news/new-analysis-raises-doubts-over-autonomous-labs-materials-discoveries/4018791.article)

## Reaction dynamics (notes 14)

- **Vibrational strong-coupling chemistry.**
  - A careful replication of a reported cavity-modified reaction rate found no effect: Imperatore and colleagues, *J. Chem. Phys.* 2021, "Reproducibility of cavity-enhanced chemical reaction rates in the vibrational strong coupling regime".
  - Later work points to artefacts from non-uniform cavity length in the spectroscopy (ChemRxiv 2024), and to dynamic Fabry–Pérot cavities as a stricter test.
  - **Standing:** contested. Whether ground-state rates change under vibrational strong coupling, with the outside field absent, has not been settled by independent reproduction.
  - **Sources:**
    - [Penn State record of the reproducibility paper](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/reproducibility-of-cavity-enhanced-chemical-reaction-rates-in-the/)
    - [ChemRxiv on cavity-length non-uniformity](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/chemrxiv/article-details/66b39ceb01103d79c50f5cb9)
- **Chiral-induced spin selectivity.** Evers and colleagues' review of CISS theory states the gap: measured spin polarizations exceed the magnitudes that spin–orbit coupling in light-atom molecules predicts. Its mechanism remains open in 2025–2026 preprints.
  - **Source:** [Theory of CISS: progress and challenges](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2108.09998)

## Electrocatalysis and plasmonics (notes 15 B12, B20)

- **Aqueous electrochemical N2 reduction.**
  - Choi and colleagues, *Nature Communications* (November 2020), set out how to identify and eliminate false positives: ammonia and NOx contamination, and labile nitrogen in catalysts. They found that most reported transition-metal results do not survive rigorous controls.
  - The lithium-mediated route is the accepted exception.
  - **Standing:** settled against the aqueous claims at the rates reported. Several retractions [unverified count].
  - **Source:** [PMC7641139](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7641139)

  - The dispute continues, with work on thermal versus non-thermal effects (Sivan and colleagues) and on separating electronic from thermal enhancement by in-operando X-ray diffraction.
  - **Standing:** open, judged case by case and dependent on thermometry.

  - **Standing:** narrowing on the computational side. No experimental thermodynamic discriminator was seen in this pass.
