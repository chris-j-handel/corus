# Five instruments, each stated whole, each executable again by any self

**Relied on by nothing.** No living file and no gathering-value changes with these. Each is a non-living set of words executing the same way for any self, with no session present: that is the whole of what an instrument is here, and the reason each stands in the set rather than in a session's own folder that goes away with it.

Each instrument states at its own opening what it does, its taking, and its refusings each with its consequence, as Natural Networking 6.2 asks of an instrument. A return of an instrument is read with its refusings. **No instrument marks a saying wrong**: each row it returns is a parity for a working to meet, one binary with one sentence.

Run each as `python3 <instrument> /path/to/repository [arguments]`.

## `quotations_check.py`

Every quotation of six words or more in a report, met word for word against every .md file of the set. Returns each quotation standing at no file, and counts the standing ones. This is the instrument that holds a report's rigor: a working lays a report of many quotations and no self reads them all by hand.

Executed at this working's seven files: **199 quotations, 199 standing at a file of the set, 0 standing nowhere.**

## `own_words_check.py`

A session's own voice — its sentences with its quotations and code left out — read against Natural Naming 2.4's 118 dissolved entries, each with the saying that replaces it. One binary per word.

Its reach and the reach it has not: it meets a word the set has released, and it does not meet a word the set has never said. The networking working's `helping_binaries.py` meets a draft against all 31,621 sentences of the 31 living files and returns each naming at no sentence of the set, which is the reach this one lacks; a working laying a saying meets that instrument too, and this one is not a replacing of it.

Executed at this working's own droplet: it returned *memory*, a word the set released at 2.4 with its own saying, which this working had used in its own voice and had laid a candidate line for.

## `repointing_check.py`

The map the Co-Chaining Logic Registry's gathering-value gives for the fifty-five entering as its steps 1 to 55, executed against every step of the living registry. Returns the map's coverage of the 70 former opening steps, the count of steps carrying a citation, and each citation pointing at or after its own step once re-pointed — which co-chaining's rule has no position for.

Executed: **the map covers 70 of 70; 172 steps carry a citation; 0 of those citations point at or after their own step.** The map is sound at that binary.

## `past_participles_check.py`

A file's own numbered sentences, or the fifty-five at their gathering-value with `--fifty-five`, read against the practice the Living File Registry records: Natural Intelligence's "past participles said living at 265 sayings" and Exhibit ONE's "*inverted*, *surfaced*, *kept* and *moved* said living, -ing".

Executed at the fifty-five: **one past participle in the whole opening, at sentence 44.** The other fifty-four carry none.

## `ledger_audit.py`

A derivings ledger met against the specification the registry's gathering-value gives it: each adding line naming the concept in the step's own words, each entering line its group's adding lines joined. Also returns each word of a sentence standing at no line of its own step or of a step before it, which is the opening's own rule executed.

Executed at this working's ledger: **the four entering lines each its adding lines joined, no word extra; one stray, *distinguishing* where its sentence has *distinguishes*, which is the instrument's own match-by-stem refusing and the set's own -ing practice; and two sentence words at no line, *distinguishes* the same and *many* at sentence 40** — the word the gathered value catches by Natural Explaining's degree-word rule, found here by the opening's own rule with no gathered value read.

## `resolver_executings.py`

Exhibit ONE's code read out of the living file as it stands, written to a folder of its own and executed at the six cases Natural Intelligence's gathering-value names, each return printed beside the gathered sentence it stands at.

Executed: all six return what their gathered sentences say. A claim resting on Exhibit ONE comes with its executing, and this is that executing for these six, for any self to run again.

## What these five do not reach

A session's prior arrives as a shape of reasoning as well as a word, and no instrument here meets a shape: a selection among standing alternatives, a ranking, an effort asked of an other, a plausible whole where a working's sharing is 0 — each is caught by the networking working's five binaries before a saying goes out, and by a partner reading with the naming in hand. The instruments do the volume no self can read by hand; the binary is a working's with its partner.
