Three parities, a cube's eight corners

Each parity one place along, the first coming round inverted. From 000 the form goes round six corners, one parity changing with each step, one and then the other cone's base. From 010 or 101, the two apexes, it goes through the empty 0 to the other apex with each step. Each corner is labelled with its parities and its name, n − 1 in binary. Drag to look round.
