# The whole reasons laid

*A row proved in its named universe is proved all or none in it. Its going on, to all universes, is a whole reason: a co-chain of no number, a self to its other-than, closing, at all numbers. A sentence ends with its standing. Executed by `instruments/whole_reasons_laid.py`, all is.*

## Row 30 · Between a number and its double, a me-parity

1. The 2n-th row of the triangle, its numbers the two above them added, has 2n + 1 numbers, together 4 to the n: a row is its prior doubled. (proved at all n, by sequential logic)
2. Its middle number, M, is the largest of the 2n + 1: M is at least 4 to the n over 2n + 1. (proved at all n, 1)
3. M is the numbers to 2n multiplied, over the numbers to n multiplied twice. (a naming)
4. A me-parity p is in M as often as it crosses the selves to 2n, by p, by p by p and on, less twice its crossings of the selves to n. (proved at all n, 3)
5. All of p's powers to 2n give 0 or 1 to that, a power at a time: p's part of M is at most 2n. (proved at all n, 4)
6. A me-parity past the side of 2n is in M once at most: its me again is past 2n. (proved at all n, 5)
7. A me-parity past two-thirds of 2n and to n is in M at none: it crosses the selves to 2n twice and those to n once, twice over. (proved at all n, 4)
8. The me-parities to any x multiplied are less than 4 to the x: the middle of the (2m + 1)-th row is crossed by all me-parities from m + 2 to 2m + 1, and is less than 4 to the m. (proved at all x, by sequential logic)
9. Is-not a me-parity between n and 2n: then M is made of the me-parities to two-thirds of 2n alone. (6, 7)
10. Then M is at most 2n to the side of 2n, by 4 to two-thirds of 2n. (5, 6, 8, 9)
11. From 468 on, 4 to the n over 2n + 1 is more than that: M other-than itself, and the is-not is-not. (proved at all n from 468, its rate rising past 468; 2, 10)
12. Below 468, the me-parities 2, 3, 5, 7, 13, 23, 43, 83, 163, 317 and 631, all before their prior's double, place a me-parity between n and 2n. (proved at all n below 468)
13. Between any number and its double, a me-parity: at all numbers, by a whole reason. (11, 12; and proved in 1 to 1,000,000, all)
