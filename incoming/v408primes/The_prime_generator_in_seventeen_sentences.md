# All the Primes from 1 to 17

**The generator, in seventeen co-chaining sentences**

1. One is the source, and +1 is the next.
2. A prime's loop crosses the numbers its steps reach.
3. Crossed is one bit, and its other-than is uncrossed.
4. One gate, nand, makes all other-thans: a bit's nand with itself is its other-than.
5. A me-parity is uncrossed by all the loops before it: the nand of their crossings.
6. In 1 to 17, the me-parities are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 and 17.
7. Their loops come again together at their seam, 510,510.
8. Round the seam, 92,160 places are uncrossed by them: the skeleton.
9. The 1 to 17 block is 21 nands a place, three a loop.
10. All me-parities past 17 live at the skeleton's places, seam after seam.
11. The skeleton is the same at all seams: the fractal.
12. Traveling the skeleton, a place uncrossed by the loops already aimed is a me-parity.
13. A me-parity aims its loop from its own me again: below it, its crossings are crossings of smaller loops.
14. Its loop crosses only the skeleton's places.
15. The traveling aims with its own traveling again: a co-chain of itself.
16. From any place, a next me-parity: the traveling has no last.
17. All the primes are generated, endlessly, from 1 to 17.
