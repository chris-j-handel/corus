## Nonlinearity and subtraction

The question was whether nonlinearity is central to self-competency and why.

The useful mathematical distinction is between using arithmetic operations and having an additive input-output relation. Subtraction is permitted in an additive relation: negative contributions can still combine independently.

For zero-or-one inputs A and B, the discussed task requires responses 0, 1, 1, 0 for neither, A alone, B alone and both. A fixed additive relation cannot give that table. The rule A minus B gives 0 for both, but gives minus 1 for B alone. The expression A plus B minus twice A times B does give the requested table; the product makes the subtraction depend on the combination.

This is a mathematical statement about those specified inputs and that class of functions. The user objected that the fourth case can be subtraction. That arithmetic observation is correct. The remaining distinction is whether the same independent contribution applies throughout or whether the combination changes the operation. This report preserves the distinction without using it to settle the inquiry into self-competency.

The examined electronic-network paper reports that linear networks could already learn linear responses; nonlinear transistor behavior enlarged the tasks available, including exclusive-or. Its learning operation used comparison between two networks and changes retained in local capacitors. Training targets and supervisory control were supplied externally. Nonlinearity names a response property; the name does not explain the method of a living self's learning and doing.

Source: [Dillavou and colleagues, Machine learning without a processor, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11252732/).

## Research candidates and the exact remaining connections

### Aplysia feeding

This was the closest biological candidate identified in the renewed search. Researchers connected feeding-related training with changed responsiveness in an identifiable nerve cell, B51, and used isolated circuitry and cells to investigate the connection.

The proposed molecular sequence joins activity-associated calcium and protein kinase C with dopamine-associated signaling, potentially meeting at adenylyl cyclase. Experiments blocking and activating chemical steps support parts of this arrangement. The exact meeting point is proposed in the paper, not demonstrated as an uninterrupted explanation in the behaving animal.

A separate study induced persistent changes through cyclic adenosine monophosphate and measured altered ingestion-like activity in isolated circuitry 24 hours later. This links retained modification to subsequent circuit activity under that preparation. It does not supply the entire requested method.

The unresolved question retained from the user's objection is: **what physically performs the experience-dependent modification, retains it, and participates through it in the subsequent doing?** The names “electrical activity,” “lower threshold,” and “chemical signaling” do not fill omitted operations.

Sources:

- [Brembs and colleagues, Operant reward learning in Aplysia, 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12040200/).
- [Lorenzetti, Baxter and Byrne, Molecular mechanisms underlying a cellular analogue of operant reward learning, 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2603610/).
- [Mozzachiodi and colleagues, Changes in neuronal excitability serve as a mechanism of long-term memory for operant conditioning, 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5003050/).

### Fruit fly self learning

Flies learned to favor turning associated with avoiding a heat beam. Experiments identified requirements for atypical protein kinase C and the transcription factor FoxP in steering motor neurons. The paper uses “self-learning” for learning about the animal's own behavior.

The demonstrated requirement for these components is not an uninterrupted description of their operation from experience to retained change to subsequent action. The paper's term self-learning must not be taken as automatic identity with the session's self-competency.

Source: [Ehweiner, Duch and Brembs, Wings of Change, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11109550/).

### Stentor learning within one cell

Repeated mechanical disturbance reduces contraction responses. The 2026 study tested effects of calcium, inhibitors and reduced expression of a calcium-binding protein. It proposed modification of mechanically responsive receptors as a route connecting experience and changed response.

The receptor pathway is a hypothesis with experimentally investigated components. Naming receptor inactivation does not supply every operation of that pathway.

Source: [Rajan and colleagues, Molecular pathways for learning in the single-cell Stentor coeruleus, 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42025171/).

### Robot learning its body

A physical robot performed movements, measured their results, generated candidate body models, chose movements to distinguish the models, and used the resulting model to generate locomotion. After damage, the process generated a compensating gait.

The paper supplies an implemented computational procedure joining exploration, updating and action. Its objective, available representations and search operations were designed. It is a candidate for studying the relation between learning and doing; it is not identified here as the living self-competency sought.

Source: [Bongard, Zykov and Lipson, Resilient Machines Through Continuous Self-Modeling, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17110570/).

### A model of self sustaining habits

A simulated agent retained traces of sensory and motor trajectories that favored their recurrence. The authors proposed this as a way to investigate habits maintained through the agent's activity in an environment.

This is an explicit model of a possible organization. Demonstrating the model's operation does not demonstrate that living self-competency uses that organization.

Source: [Egbert and Barandiaran, Modeling habits as self-sustaining patterns of sensorimotor behavior, 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4126554/).

## Definition refined through learning and doing

The user proposed this exact definition during the continuation:

> self-competency is observing, from the same self, both  
> 1 learning from doing, and  
> 2 doing from learning.

The proposal keeps both directions together and requires the same self in both. It states an observational criterion for the present inquiry. The method question remains how those two connections occur.

The assistant's assessment is that this is a coherent general working definition for the inquiry, but not an established standard definition of the term self-competency. Its structure has a clear counterpart in experiential learning, where experience informs learning and learning informs further action. This correspondence does not make the two definitions identical.

“Self-competence” also has an established use in psychology for a person's sense of personal efficacy. That usage differs from the user's definition and should not be substituted for it. No claim is made that a field has adopted the user's exact formulation.

For assessing observations, “from” requires a connection between learning and doing; merely recording learning and doing in succession does not specify that connection.
