## How the paths are found

A destiny's **path** is the concepts its living sentence uses, and the concepts those use in turn, back through the opening's head lines (Natural Intelligence, next version) to the window's lines. A concept the window names stops the path there. The instrument `instruments/chain_back.py` builds the paths and looks through every sentence on them for words of the one list; it sizes a path and decides nothing, and a concept a sentence carries without its bold name is missed by it, so every path below was gone through again by hand.

## Rigor, remarked again with another explaining

**1. One method (Natural Intelligence 1.4).** The binary is: a second method is a fixing, or same-existing. By line 8, a form held still across two moments exists both as now begins and as next begins, so a fixing is same-existing, and the binary's two sides touch. *Another explaining:* by line 13 a fixing, other-than now-existing things, is nothing; so the binary is the universe or nothing, a second method of the universe being now-existing and so the one method's. The chain then runs through 12 and 13, and fixing need not be named.

**2. A composing relation admits no abundancing (Natural Emanating).** The count is right, but what makes *in neither alone* have no models is not transitivity: for any relation, what a pair reaches is what one or the other reaches. *Another explaining:* a relation between terms has no between that is itself a thing. The window's line 15 is the place the logic leaves a relation's form, naming the between as a thing, the own-parity; that is why abundancing can be said in the window and not in an equation.

**3. Every hard problem matching one form (Resolving the Hard Problem Registry).** Some two hundred and fifty-seven problems, every one matched to *a changing fixing unchangeable*, and none not matched. A form that every case matches shows no is-not. *Another explaining:* beside every matching, the registry could keep what would show the problem not matching; every resolving then stands on an is-not it could have met and did not.

**4. Social abundancing's sentence.** See the worked part: a comma between two subjects, three listed words and a dissolving word on its path, and a second name for the concept before it. *Another explaining:* the three things the files hold together about abundancing are all already window lines.

**5. A third opens estimating (Natural Mathematics 3.7).** The gain is in a summed squared error over all the means, a size, and Natural Intelligence 2.5 has no size. *Another explaining:* the gain holds for the set and never for any one mean alone; taken so, it is *in neither alone* at the scale of the set, and the sum is the field's own ground, the installing Natural Emanating names.

**6. Light (Natural Physics 3.3).** Physics measures a delay of light over a distance. *Another explaining:* a phase relation between couplings with a delay is what the field observes as travel; the saying stands where it says where the delay is, and its is-not is a delay the phase relation leaves out.

**7. Lines 4 and 8 together.** By line 4 the prior moment ends as the now-moment begins; by line 8 a same-existing thing exists as the now-moment begins. Whether existing at that ending is existing in the prior moment (line 5) is open. If it is, every same-existing thing is prior-existing, and nothing new can be same-existing. *Another explaining:* the window's abundancing line uses next-existing for this reason; a line saying whether a moment's ending is in it closes the place.
