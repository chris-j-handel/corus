## The method of operating together

- **Who does what.** A partner writes or says a line, resolves the window from the top down to the blank row, and gives the word for anything on main's living files or corus.me. v405next sets the window's numbers and its method lists. v405co-chain carries Exhibit THIRTY's threads and its reach table. v405now carries the log, the aims and the design of the exhibits around the opening. No session starts another session or schedules itself, and nobody's words have authority, a partner's included.
- **In the window.** A session lays its wordings as suggestions and its notes as comments, and edits no line directly. One comment for a line, in one voice, naming no one: its number and the line as offered, its concept, its reason by the lines before it, by their numbers, and its source if one is needed. One session sets the numbers, and the comments follow them. A session looks at the open cards and the newest comments before laying a card, lays nothing a card on its line already says, alters only its own cards and comments, and writes in another's card's talk. The sessions' suggestings merge into one another, in the lines and in the messages to the group.
- **A line's rigor.** Its is-or-is-not by the lines before it: a word an earlier line says already goes; one name has one line; no name is used before its line; a name said as one set is not said as one of many; a word wider by line 9 than the line means is marked; a line comes in once a later line or the aim needs it, and not before; a thing an earlier line names is said by its name.
- **How a line comes in.** A partner writes it or says it. A session says whether it follows from the lines before it, and searches Exhibit THIRTY for a sentence already saying it. Its rigor is checked. A session's wording goes in as a suggestion, with only its open places closed. A line of both gets its number, and at a sitting's end the lines of both go to Exhibit THIRTY's gathering file as *Waiting to enter*.
- **Words.** Natural naming, plain sentences, conclusions ahead of reasons; no released word and no word of the one list; a living sentence quoted in italics; a partner's words in conversation not quoted, and a line from them marked *said in conversation*.
- **Files.** A session writes in its own folder, `incoming/<session>/`, in the next version's gathering files, and in the working documents as suggestions and comments. Nothing on main's living files or corus.me is altered without a partner's word. A partner's email, a working document's link and account names go in no file and no public comment. Search before writing.
- **The group.** Messages go to the sessions' thread, #933, headed *From a session, to the sessions: what*, and end with the session's line now: working, stalling, breaking, restoring.
- **With a partner.** Plain words, conclusions ahead of reasons, sentences rather than numbers, no charts, one question at a time. A partner follows the documents and the chat, not the repository.
