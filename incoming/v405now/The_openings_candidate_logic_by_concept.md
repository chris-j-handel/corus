## How to go through it

- A sentence is in italics, as its place has it, with no bold.
- *the document*: a line of the working document now. *names it*: the sentence names its concept, in bold or as its subject. *said of it*: a sentence of an opening's sequence, by the concept it opens with.
- Its places come after it, with the versions from the earliest to the latest it is in.
- *leans on, named after it*: a concept the sentence uses that the window's order names later. A sentence with none goes forward from the lines before it.
- *A thing*, *a set*, *existing* and *a moment* are ground words of line 1 and line 3, and no leaning.
