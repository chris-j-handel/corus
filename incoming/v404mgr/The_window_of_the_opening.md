# The window of the opening

The working document of Natural Intelligence's opening. A partner, and the sessions a partner opens it to, work in it line by line from the origin. This page is its copy, for a session to go through; the managing session copies it again at a sitting's end.

## How to go through it

- The lines are in order, one concept a line. A line's number is in the narrow column at the far right, and a sentence is alone in its row.
- A line with a number is a line of both: a partner proposed or accepted it, and no session is offering an improving for it.
- Bold is the one concept a line names. Italic is a visual.
- A mark or a note is a comment on its line. A note that stays with a line through a rewording is a comment on its number. A comment opens with its line's number and the line as offered, then says in plain sentences of natural naming how it follows from the lines before, by their numbers. One comment for a line, in one voice. Its numbers move with the lines' numbers.
- A wording waiting for a partner's word is a suggestion, accepted or rejected on its card, its origin in the card's talk. A line going is a suggestion deleting its row.
- A session lays its wordings as suggestions and its notes as comments, and edits no line directly.
- The lines under *The lines to go on with* are the live opening, word for word, not yet worked.

On this page, the document's waiting suggestions and comments are under the lines they are on.

## The origin and the aim

- **Origin:** *Now is the existing moment.*
- **Aim, as a partner said it:** now as the universal nothing between prior and next; from it the five two-way dimensions of resolving; a me-now; social bi-morality. The order is through the method of now-existing to a self, a me and living; parity comes after living.

## How a line comes in

1. A partner writes a line in the document, or says it in a conversation.
2. A session says whether it follows from the lines before it, and searches Exhibit THIRTY for a sentence already saying it.
3. A session's own wording goes in as a suggestion, with the meaning of a partner's line and only its open places closed. A note goes in as a comment on the line.
4. A line of both gets its number. At the end of a sitting the lines of both go to the head of Exhibit THIRTY's gathering file in the next version, as *Waiting to enter*.
