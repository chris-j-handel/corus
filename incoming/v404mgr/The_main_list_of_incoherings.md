# The main list of incoherings, in the open

*The session v404mgr, 18 November, by a partner's word: the incoherings of the living file set lie in the open, in one list, for a session to pick up, cohere into the work, and return with its improving suggesting laid beside the incohering it is of. A session needing a resolving picks it up from the same place.*

The list is [`The_main_list_of_incoherings.csv`](The_main_list_of_incoherings.csv), beside this page: one line for one note of a gathering file of the next version, a possible incohering or an asking. On 18 November, written again from the next version with offers 999, 1000 and 1002 and main's 1001 in it, it is 2364 lines of 33 gathering files: a suggesting in it 355, an open sentence 239, answered 11, open 1759.

## A line

- **Its name**: its gathering file's title and six letters, the same while the note's opening is the same. A session names a line by it.
- **The part it is with**, and **the living sentence it is beside**.
- **Its open sentence**, is-or-is-not?, with a line of the form.
- **What is with it**, the opening of the note's own words.
- **Its state**: open; a suggesting in it; an open sentence; or answered, with an is or an is-not of both partners said in it.

## A session with a line

1. **Pick up.** A session names a line at [the four sessions' thread](https://github.com/chris-j-handel/corus/issues/933), by its name. Two sessions with one line is no harm: two suggestings lie with it.
2. **Cohere.** Before a word of its own, a session finds: the line's living sentence whole, in its living file; the sentences of Exhibit THIRTY Co-Chaining Logic Registry and of the living files that say its concept already; and its own words by [the list of words of no natural naming](Words_of_no_natural_naming_with_their_natural_naming.md). A sentence in natural naming is likely living already.
3. **Return.** Its suggesting is laid after the note's own words, in the gathering file of the next version, opening with the words *A suggesting:*: one concept, with the living sentence it is from quoted whole in italics. It is offered into the next version, and the managing session brings it in with the checks.
4. **A gap remaining** is offered by the form, at the thread and to a partner: the sentence of is, a sentence of the co-chaining; *( is-or-is-not? )*; what its is resolves and what its is incoheres; and the same for its is-not.
5. **Resolved.** A line is settled by both partners' is alone. The session at the line's living file re-says the living sentence by it, shown as it was and as it is.

A suggesting does no harm: it re-says no living sentence, and it lies with its incohering for a session needing it.
