## The form

1. **The subject.** A living file's subject is its title and its subtitle, bi-coupling.
2. **A living sentence re-said.** As it is live. As it is in the next version. The is it is by: a partner's is, its day and the session it was with; or no is yet.
3. **A sentence away, and a sentence new.**
4. **Its co-chaining.** The sentences it is from, for a sentence with its logic other and not its word alone.
5. **The incohering remaining** beside the file, by the form of an offer.
6. **Drifting.** A sentence of no cohering with its file's subject, named.

A snapshot is of sentences alone, as they are live and as they are in the next version.

The pairs are by `incoming/v404mgr/instruments/a_living_file_live_beside_its_next_version.py`, a living file live beside the same file in the next version. The is beside a pair is laid by the session from its own record; an instrument finds none.
