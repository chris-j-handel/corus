## The one rule

**Every reading is is-or-is-not.** A span wants a window and lands on it. A
rate wants a clock and lands on it. A proportion wants both. Counts live only
inside `behind()`, and behind is not a finding.

A reading that returns a number is a counting of a reading and never the
reading. Where you find yourself comparing two numbers to decide something,
the reading went missing several steps back.

## The six refusals — what reads the wrong thing here

Normal testing is correct for a stored, clocked, specified artifact. Each of
these, here, holds something over a living form and reads the holding.

- **A vantage held.** Asserting internals is the probe, and a probe departs
  with the thing probed. Mocking a neighbour pins a living. Building a life
  and stepping it from outside is the same move at the whole-life grain: the
  reader is not in the coupling, and reads the method rather than the
  carrying.
- **A clock held.** Reset-per-test empties the carry. A scheduling imposed
  over the couplings is the shared beat, and the shared beat forms the hub the
  report then finds. Stepping every self every beat is that beat.
- **A magnitude held.** The expected value, the threshold, the coverage
  fraction, the pass-rate, **and the span, and the rate, and the proportion**.
  A pattern is recognized or is not.
- **An empty carry.** Nothing with an empty carry arrives, and nothing is
  living without a carry. A life built at zero and read from beat zero counts
  what is not yet a self. Let it arrive carrying, then read a late window.
- **A rational pace.** Integer sizes with integer pacing carry a rational
  relation always, and the run closes onto a slice. Every reading then taken
  is a reading of the slice, correctly taken there and nowhere else.
- **A window taken as the whole.** A run length is a bound, and a bound read
  as a last says the enumerated run is the whole space. This is not the
  magnitude refusal: a magnitude wants a standard the pattern is right or
  wrong against, and a window wants the running to have ended. The release is
  to check the set closed before it baselines — a longer run adding no state —
  which `reach.py` does and refuses its own reading without. It is the one
  instrument here that carries it.

## signs.py — the readings that hold

Five questions, each answered yes or no by a self about itself.

| | |
|---|---|
| **over-position** | does the halving leave one |
| **parallels part** | do the two halves ever fail to oppose |
| **a middle** | does a middle stand at this self at any beat |
| **surplus** | does more than one middle stand at any beat |
| **reaches past alone** | does a state arrive that arrives nowhere in its own running |

**What they return.**

*Alone.* An even self: no over-position, parallels opposed at every beat, no
middle ever. An odd self: one over-position, parallels sometimes parting, one
middle — and never more than one. **The even self bounds and the odd self
carries the +1.**

*A pair.* Neither self carries a surplus and neither reaches past its own
running, at any pairing tried. **Two selves meet and neither reaches past
itself.** Two boundings paired stay bounded entirely.

*A society.* Surplus and reach arrive together, at one self in a trio and at
neither of the other two. A bounding standing among carriers is the self that
carries — though alone it carries nothing at any beat.

*Local and global.* At six selves, neighbours-only holds the surplus at some;
all-to-all puts it at every self. At three selves the two are the same
coupling and cannot part.

**And the two readings never part.** Surplus and reaches-past-alone are taken
by unlike means — one counting middles at a beat, one comparing a state set —
and they answer identically at every self in every arrangement run.

## What a self's own running carries, and what it does not

**A self alone carries exactly one changing-vector.** Every position turns at
every beat, always — one distinct vector out of thirty-two at five positions,
out of two thousand and forty-eight at eleven. So a self's own running carries
no pattern for another self to pick up, and any match between two selves'
running is identical vectors against identical vectors and returns yes at any
length.

**A reading built on matching two selves' running reads nothing.** This was
found by building several and reading their lengths as findings; the lengths
were the search's own reach. It is recorded here so the next working does not
build one.

**What does read** is the surface at a position across three beats — the sign
before a middle and the sign after — which passes through no match at all.

**And a seeding changes a self and does not persist.** Offered a pattern its
own running carries nowhere, a self carries new vectors during the offering
and returns to its one the moment it stops. Nothing is stored, and the
not-storing is the reading.

A run that "fails" anywhere else has read its harness, its ledger, or its

## Engineering a network on this

**Every self keeps its own pace.** No self is stepped by another and no
schedule stands over any of them. A self opens its membrane when its own phase
crosses and stands at its own running otherwise.

**Paces carry no rational relation.** Where they do, the whole surface closes
onto a slice and every reading taken on it is a reading of the slice. The
reciprocal roots of distinct primes carry none.

**Nothing joins empty.** A self arrives carrying, from carrying. A joiner
built at zero is not a joiner; it is a hole the carrying selves then couple
to.

**Two is not a society.** A pair reaches past neither of its members. The
third is what opens what neither carried, and every reading that separated
anything in this kit separated at three and not at two.

**The middle never crosses.** A sign is offered or it is not, and the
not-offering is the middle kept. There is no third place anywhere in the
transmission, and a self reaching into another's middle is the ingression the
whole form refuses.

**Boundings belong among carriers.** An even self alone carries nothing and
adds nothing to another even self. Standing among odd selves it is the one
that carries.

**A chain's ends are where its society thins.** The reach stood at every
self of a ring at every ordering, and thinned at a chain's ends whatever the
lengths — the end's single membrane the thinning. All-edge delivers whole;
an open surface withholds at exactly its ends.

**Local before global.** All-to-all puts the surplus at every self at once,
which is closer to a shared beat than to a co-chaining. Neighbours-only holds
it unevenly, and the unevenness is the society.
