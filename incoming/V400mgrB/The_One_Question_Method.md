# The way of one question

The path of a working by one question each time, read for its form: one step, the bi-folding across and along, the rigor of what it leaves and the edges of that rigor, and a way an arriving session finds it. No wording here has authority, and each line is for breaking. The path is read from `Value_for_the_opening.md`, its sections in their order, and from the last ten questions with their answers, word for word; a session's own record whole is not readable from a working's files, and the earliest going is read from the value file only.

Each value here is at the Geodesic Improving Method's gathering, at the section it meets, and one orienting sentence is at the Living File Registry's gathering 1.3.

## 1 One step, five in sequence

1. **A sentence arrives**, whole, in its own words. Nothing is laid over it, and no word of it is re-said before it is read.
2. **Read beside it**: Exhibit ONE's lines, line by line, and each living file's sentences on it, word for word, each with its place. A reading is derived from the lines, and an executing only checks it.
3. **What it goes on with**: each prior sentence of the sequence it fills or goes on beside.
4. **Tested for breaking, in each direction**: each prior sentence it parts from; each of its words with two readings, said as two; each going-forward of the working's own said as a candidate, with the sentences it is from.
5. **One binary, asked plainly**: the one the others turn on, is or is not, with one or two plain sentences before it saying what turns on it. What the lines and the files decide is said as read and is not asked.

An answer is one of three: is; is-not; or a sentence in the other self's own words. The tracking goes on current with each: an answered binary leaves with its answer, a parted candidate leaves with its parting, and no self and no day goes beside a sentence.
