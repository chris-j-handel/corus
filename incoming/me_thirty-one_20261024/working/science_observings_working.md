## 3.2 · Waking me and sleeping me
- **Sleep older than brains** ✓ (Nath … Goentoro 2017, *Current Biology*; Kanaya … Itoh 2020, *Science Advances*): the jellyfish *Cassiopea* and the freshwater polyp *Hydra*, with no brain, rest with a raised arousal threshold and rebound after deprivation; in *Hydra*, sleep deprivation suppresses cell proliferation. *Agreeing*: each living me with both states; the inward society at work in sleeping. *Parting*: *Hydra*'s cycle is about four hours, apart from the day. https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/psp7j-nmv35 · https://www.kyushu-u.ac.jp/en/researches/view/196
- **Sleep across animals** ✓ (Raizen … Pack 2008, *Nature*; Shaw, Cirelli, Greenspan & Tononi 2000, *Science*; Keene & Duboue 2018, *J. Exp. Biol.*): worm lethargus beside each moult, fly rest with rebound, sleep near-universal in animals; whether sleep belongs to circuits or to single cells is open. *Agreeing*. https://www.nature.com/articles/nature06535 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10710313/ · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6515771
- **Plant sleep, a daily cycle on a continuous circling** ✓ (Darwin 1880, *The Power of Movement in Plants*; Linnaeus, *Somnus plantarum*, 1755): leaf sleep movements a modified circumnutation, two cycles in one movement. *Agreeing* (two circles, one movement); *parting*: a posture, read by its field as warmth, apart from awareness. https://gutenberg.org/files/5605/5605-h/5605-h.htm
- **Same torus, waking and sleeping** ✓ (Gardner … May-Britt & Edvard Moser 2022, *Nature*; Peyrache, Lacroix, Petersen & Buzsáki 2015, *Nature Neuroscience*; Chaudhuri, Gerçek, Pandey, Peyrache & Fiete 2019, *Nature Neuroscience*): a grid module's activity lies on one torus, each cell's place on it the same from waking to sleep; the head-direction ring goes on in REM at waking speed and in NREM as fast coherent sweeps. The state changes how activity goes on the torus, the torus the same. *Agreeing*: same-existing me, sleeping and waking. https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v602y2022i7895d10.1038_s41586-021-04268-7.html · https://mcgovern.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/s41593-019-0460-x.pdf
- **Experience within sleep** ✓ (Siclari … Postle & Tononi 2017, *Nature Neuroscience*): dream reports from NREM and REM, with a local drop in slow activity over posterior cortex predicting a report. *Parting*: sleeping, by the field, is no uniform unconscious. https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:96505

## 4.1 · Observing, possibling and discovering a me's next (in sleeping)
- **Replay** ✓ (Wilson & McNaughton 1994, *Science*): place cells active together in waking fire together more in the sleep that follows — the prior observing again. *Agreeing*. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8036517/
- **Sleep and insight** ✓ (Wagner, Gais, Haider, Verleger & Born 2004, *Nature*): over twice as many found a hidden rule after sleep as after waking, with training first — discovering on waking. *Agreeing*. https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/nature/v427y2004i6972d10.1038_nature02223.html
- **Synaptic homeostasis** ✓ (Tononi & Cirelli 2014, *Neuron*): waking learning strengthens synapses overall; sleep scales them back. *Agreeing* (candidate: improving waking, do-no-harming sleeping). https://cirelli-tononi-lab.psychiatry.wisc.edu/?p=1666
- *Contested, for the gathering only:* sleep's clearing of the brain (Xie et al. 2013, *Science*) and its counter-finding (Miao et al. 2024, *Nature Neuroscience*).

## 5.3 / 5.4 · A same-existing-society, unconsciously us; momentaries and the day
- **A colony with no sleep of its own** ✓ (Fuchikawa, Eban-Rothschild, Nagari, Shemesh & Bloch 2016, *Nature Communications*; Nagari, Brenner & Bloch 2017, *J. Exp. Biol.*; Klein, Klein, Wray, Mueller & Seeley 2010, *PNAS*): a hive's social cues set its bees' clocks without contact and over light; nurse bees tend brood around the clock; sleep-deprived bees dance less precisely. The colony, its labour shared, has no sleeping or waking of its own; its bees sleep and wake. *Agreeing*: unconsciously us. https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/natcom/v7y2016i1d10.1038_ncomms11662.html · https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/8df1e321-ddd4-46ee-bd99-90f01313ce59/full
- **A me's day and the earth's day** ✓ (Czeisler … Kronauer 1999, *Science*; Wright … Chinoy 2013, *Current Biology*): a human's own period about 24.18 hours, the earth's 24; light brings them together each day; a week of natural light alone brings biological night with sunset to sunrise. *Agreeing*. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040141918
- **Two circles on a torus, locking** ✓ (Strogatz, *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos*, section 8.6; Tokuda, Schmal, Ananthasubramaniam & Herzel 2020, *Frontiers in Physiology*): two coupled rhythms are a flow on a torus; a rational ratio locks (Arnold tongues predict a circadian clock's entraining), an irrational one winds densely, its closings an empty set. *Agreeing*: natural torusing of a me's sleeping and waking with the day; the torus a state space. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2020.00334/full · https://www.tud.ttu.ee/web/dmitri.kartofelev/YFX1520/LectureNotes_8.pdf
- **Social time** ✓ (Wittmann, Dinich, Merrow & Roenneberg 2006, *Chronobiology International*; Roenneberg, Allebrandt, Merrow & Vetter 2012, *Current Biology*): the gap between body time and work or school time, *social jetlag*, about 70% of 65,000 people over an hour, tied to smoking, wellbeing and weight. *Agreeing* (a society's time with its mes); *parting* (its time against theirs, at a cost). https://read.qxmd.com/read/16687322/social-jetlag-misalignment-of-biological-and-social-time
- **Sleep and helping** ✓ (Ben Simon, Vallat, Rossi & Walker 2022, *PLOS Biology*): a night without sleep dampens the network for understanding others; less sleep, less helping the next day; US donations fall about 10% after the spring clock change, apart from regions keeping one time. *Agreeing*: a me's sleeping with its social moral competency. https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2022/08/220823143827.htm

## 3.2 / 5.1 · Species-specific domain
- **Species-private signals within a shared one** ✓ (Federle & Bassler 2003, *J. Clin. Invest.*; Papenfort & Bassler 2016, *Nat. Rev. Microbiol.*): each Gram-negative species makes its own acyl-homoserine lactone, read by matched receptors; AI-2 is shared across many species. *Agreeing* (a species-specific domain of common parities); *parting* (no consciousness shown in bacteria). https://www.jci.org/articles/view/20195
- **Self-recognition with a mark that matters to its kind** ✓ (Kohda et al. 2019 and 2022, *PLoS Biology*; Kohda et al. 2023, *PNAS*): cleaner wrasse scrape a parasite-like mark seen in a mirror, and recognise photos of their own face. *Agreeing*; *parting*: whether the mark test reaches self-awareness is disputed. https://boris.unibe.ch/140924
