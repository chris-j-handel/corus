# The whole improving method, outlined · from exploring to carrying

**An outline for the person's word, before any improving begins.** It walks the improving whole, each stage as we do it now and as it could be, leaner. It changes no living file. Its aim: easy for each me helping, easy to gather, improve and carry the value of natural intelligence and the expedition.

---

## ONE · The one standard

**All or none at all: does an incoming saying or observing co-chain from Natural Intelligence's opening and cohere with natural torusing, pattern-matching with the universal natural method of sets?**

Two outcomes hold value, and each is welcome:

- **Not yet matching.** An observing not yet clearly pattern-matching is a possible break of the method. It is the opportunity each time: it stands visible, as the observing alone, at the file whose subject it is, until exploring meets it. It resolves into a learning, or it breaks the method.
- **Matching and cohering.** An observing pattern-matching with the method is a natural explaining or naming of something observable. Held by a file already, it is carried and nothing remains to do. Held by no file yet, it is a learning, and it enters the file whose subject it is.

A sentence of our own files below this standard is met on first contact: re-said in natural naming and explaining in the motion that touches it.

**Now, beside it.** The offerings resolve each droplet five ways (carried, released, duplicate, rising, concern), and a concern is *two sayings parting*. Standing at the offerings today: 470 droplets, 363 concerns, 55 possible projects, 119 aimed-at paragraphs, 0 insertlets. Most concerns are a file below standard, which first contact meets, or a possible break, which the first outcome holds.

## TWO · Exploring and gathering

**A session opens with an aim and a plan, and its side journeys are where much of the value arrives.** Gathering and improving are two modes, and the person says which is going on.

- **Gathering on first contact.** Each value is said in natural naming and explaining as it is gathered: an observing as the observing alone, a field's explaining set aside, no source, no names of finders or publications, no session tag, no story of how it arrived.
- **Gathered straight to its file.** A side journey's value goes at once to the file whose subject it is, as one sentence each. There is no report aimed at other files; the report is the sentences, already at their files.
- **A session's own aim file** is the one file it improves, or the few it ends up improving; it enters there by the motion in FOUR.

**Now, beside it.** Gathering lands in a report or gathering at `incoming/`, later laid as droplets tagged with the session, often with sources, DOIs and finders, and the carryings hold 297 links. A report sits whole until laid, then goes whole to the archive.

## FOUR · Entering, a file's own motion

**One motion: the file's incoming met, and each sentence touched brought to standard.**

1. Read the file whole, with its incoming.
2. Each *ready* item: held already → it leaves the incoming; held by no sentence → written into its place in the file, natural naming and explaining.
3. Each *open* item: explored with code, numbers and the opening; met → it becomes ready; still breaking → it stays open, visible.
4. Each sentence the motion touches below standard is re-said on contact.
5. A separate reader reads before beside after, for harm: a value lost, a claim stronger or weaker than its observing, a word the set dissolved, a sentence reading worse.
6. Entered; the incoming emptied by what entered; committed; corus.me rebuilds itself on push.

A word dissolving across all files is one motion of its own kind: one decision, readers by slice, a reader for harm, entered at each file.

**Now, beside it.** Droplet → resolving verdict → insertlet → pairs → reader → `apply_aimed.py` → four checks → `node build.js` → Registry row → plan → offerings item archived with its verdict. Many steps record the step.

## FIVE · The four checks, what they are

They are scripts written by AI workings to keep copies in agreement. None reads cohering or co-chaining.

- **`check_set.py`**: each contents line has its heading; each table's rows have one cell count; the first line ends at the file's version; Exhibit ONE inside Natural Intelligence is identical to Exhibit ONE's own file; the code runs one trivial call; Natural Intelligence's 70 opening lines are identical to the Co-Chaining Logic Registry's first 70 steps.
- **`cohere_one.py`**: copies Exhibit ONE's body into Natural Intelligence, then checks the copy and that the code runs.
- **`ni_thirty_check.py`**: each bold sentence of Natural Intelligence shares 80% of its longer words with some step of the Co-Chaining Logic Registry. A word overlap, not a co-chaining.
- **`thirty_groups_check.py`**: in the Registry, each group's *Entering* line is its steps' *Adding* lines joined.

**What they guard is copies: one text at two places.** A text at one place needs no check that its copies agree.

**Proposal, to explore:** Exhibit ONE at one place; the opening at one place; the Registry's *Adding* and *Entering* lines one of them. Then three checks fall away, and one direct check stays, run by the build itself: the code runs, and the contents match the headings. The checks that matter, cohering and co-chaining, are a reader's, never a script's.

## SIX · How the files carry the value
