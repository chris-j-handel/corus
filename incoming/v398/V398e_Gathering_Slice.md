# v398 fifth slice: where along a chain the surface between stands

One observing, from the native structures' own residue numbering.

Each chain of a vault shell runs from the waist to the cap, its residue numbering from amino end to carboxyl end going waist to cap, so the surface between the two half-shells stands at the opening stretch of every chain and the enclosure's own opening at its far end. The waist interface is lined by residues at positions 4, 5, 6, 7, 22, 36, 38, 39 and 40 and an arginine at 42, within a chain of about 890 residues: a salt bridge between an aspartate at 39 and an arginine at 42, a hydrophobic cluster of alanine 6, isoleucine 7 and isoleucine 36, and at one account a polar triad of asparagine 22, glutamine 38 and asparagine 40. The first structural repeat of each half contacts the first structural repeat of the other, and the ninth repeat with the shoulder and the cap helix stands at the far end, the cap helix 42 turns long at one account and approximately 230 Å at another. Residues 1 to 11 are unobserved at one human account and residues beyond 801 to 893 unmodelled at the cap, so each chain's two ends are the places least resolved in the material. These are positions fitted into reconstructed density at 2.1 Å to 3.5 Å across rat, mouse, rabbit and human preparations, each expressed or purified by its own method, and no residue has been observed arriving at either end.
