## 4. One numeral route, tested and broken

## 5. The hand: an observing that has not been made

Natural Biology v392 §3.3 holds that co-sequencing is right-spiral, that a left form is what one right spiral emanates, and that a left form stands on outward-facing surfaces rather than with a pattern-matching core; it names its own break conditions. §3.7 holds that chirality changes and changes back by scale, three chains winding left at their own scale then about each other right at assembly scale, opposing hands locking. The towel correspondence asks for right-spiral co-sequencing of three edges.

All of this turns on a hand. So this session went looking for the vault's.

**No published vault structure reports the chains as skewed, tilted or spiralled.** Every structure that describes the wall describes it as straight: vertical staves of stacked domains, a quasi-linear spoke structure, three tryptophans on an almost straight vertical line, forty-eight ribs around the wall, twenty-six parallel strands in the cap barrel. No skew angle per chain is reported for any species by any method. The angles that do appear are other quantities: a cap flatness angle of about 34° for L05 against about 52° for the human vault; about 3° of relative rotation between halves; 16° of wall-curvature change in a variant; about 2° of cap rotation with a 10 Å lift between two conformations of one recombinant particle; 80° rising past 97° between neighbouring first repeats in a proposed opening.

**And the handedness itself is mostly inherited rather than measured.** The 9 Å rat shell had its enantiomer assigned during model building, the authors noting that at that resolution the analysis would not identify the true phase set. The 3.5 Å rat crystal structures do not discuss hand. A later engineered-vault reconstruction states plainly that its 13.5 Å map could not establish handedness, and reconstructions at 38- and 40-fold did not resolve it either. The recent human, rabbit and in-situ structures contain no handedness determination at all; each begins from an earlier model and imposes symmetry. **The one structure with a documented hand test is L05, the supplied paper**, which started from a smoothed predicted shell because its own ab initio maps did not distinguish the vault from its mirror image, confirmed the hand by fitting ideal α-helices against their mirror images, and tested predicted whole-vault handedness by agreement with the human vault — which itself inherits.

**What this is, in the one binary's terms.** It is not a break. A field describing a wall as straight and a natural explaining asking for right-spiral co-sequencing part only if the field has measured the thing and found none; here the field has mostly not measured it, and where it did, it anchored to a structure that inherits. It is still-possibling with an unusually concrete next step: the deposited maps already contain whatever hand there is, and the measurement — a per-chain skew angle relative to the long axis, and an independently determined enantiomer — has not been made and could be.

**How this finding could be wrong.** It is a negative result from a literature and structure-database search, not a proof. It would be overturned by any paper reporting a per-chain skew, by a supplementary file not reached, or by a reader measuring the skew directly in a deposited map — which is the point: the map is public and the measurement is a morning's work. Several publisher pages refused retrieval during the search, and those are named in §15.

**What it is not.** It is not evidence for a right spiral. The field's own descriptors run the other way, and a correspondence that needed the wall to be visibly spiralled would be in trouble on the present record. Natural Intelligence §3.2 and the Co-Chaining Logic entries on co-linear lines do not require every material segment to lie parallel to one laboratory axis, which is why this is open rather than closed — but that latitude is a reason to make the measurement, not a reason to stop needing it.

## 6. One sequence, three forms: the relation that does hold

The prior working's strongest observing was that copies of one sequence take different forms within one assembly, and that a constituent's sequence alone does not specify its conformation in the assembled setting. That observing is now much stronger than it was, because it recurs across independent systems with different counts.

| System | Method | Barrel | Cap | How identical chains differ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human brain vault | cryo-EM, 3.1 Å whole, 3.6 Å cap | 39 per half, D39 | C13 | Two of every three monomers sequentially excluded from the pore; three arrangements from one sequence; a 26-stranded parallel β-barrel about 38 Å across, with a 13-stranded inner barrel about 16 Å proposed tentatively |
| Rabbit liver vault | cryo-EM, 2.3 Å whole, 2.25 Å cap | 39 per half, D39 through residue 803 | C13 | From residue 804 the 39 chains take three folds — an asymmetric homotrimer forming outer, middle and inner rings; constriction about 15 Å |
| L05 phage vault | cryo-EM, 3.2 Å | 48 per half, 48 ribs preferred without imposed symmetry | 16-fold | Four conformations A, B, C and C′; A folds into the lumen, B toward the exterior, only C reaches the cap-disk centre; C and C′ alternate, taking the pole to 8-fold |

**The relation that survives across all three is grouping in threes while the total count varies**: 39 to 13 in two vertebrates, 48 to 16 in a phage protein, with L05 carrying one further halving at the tip. The count is not the relation; the three is. That is precisely the distinction Natural Chemistry §4.5 asks for, and here the observings supply it rather than a numeral resemblance.

Two cautions the record itself carries. A third recent human structure at 3.09 Å does not report the cap mismatch at all, and an earlier engineered-vault study found 39-fold failing at the cap top without characterising what replaced it — so the C13 finding is not yet universal across human structures. And the three forms are neighbouring spatial conformations in a frozen assembly; no observing follows one chain through them.

**A conflict to carry whole, not to settle.** Copy number parts in two families. One descends from eight-fold petal electron microscopy and a 9 Å rat structure with 96 copies, 48 per half, phased with 48-fold symmetry. The other, now dominant, gives 78 copies, 39 per half, from 39-fold crystallography and cryo-EM. The field named the discrepancy and moved to 78 without reconciling it. The irony worth noticing: L05's 48-per-half matches the numbers of the eukaryotic model the field abandoned, in a different protein, by a different route. That is a coincidence to hold at arm's length, exactly as §4 holds the seam.

## 7. What deletion does, and what it does not

Mice with the major vault protein gene disrupted are viable, born at Mendelian ratios, breed normally, show no clear physical abnormality to sixteen months, and show no microscopic difference in lung, liver or gastrointestinal tract, nor in bone marrow or peripheral blood counts. Across twelve cytostatic agents, no statistically significant difference in half-maximal inhibitory concentration appeared between knockout and wild-type embryonic stem cells. In vivo, twenty-one wild-type against twenty-three knockout mice given doxorubicin showed similar weight loss, similar white-cell decline and similar recovery. Separately, knocking the protein down in a resistant lung-cancer line did not change where doxorubicin went or how sensitive the cells were, and raising it in sensitive cells did not confer resistance.

Against that: the same germline deletion does raise susceptibility to *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, and the protein is recruited to lipid rafts on infection. A bacterial effector binds it to evade autophagy. A cell-free system reports nuclear-pore insertion requiring it — which sits awkwardly beside viable, fertile, histologically normal null mice, a tension the sources do not resolve.

**Why this belongs in a natural-intelligence opening.** The particle is present at ten thousand to ten million copies per cell, conserved across the eukaryotes examined, and its removal returns almost nothing measurable. Natural Biology §9.2 has a sentence for that shape: *A clean cut is larger half: biggest abundancing and least attention.* The field's own words for the situation are that function remains unclear, enigmatic, a mystery. That is an openly named question rather than a residue-word, so it is not a clean cut in §9.2's strict sense — but the abundance-to-attention ratio is the one §9.2 describes, and the section is where this observing meets the files.

One methodological flag. The claim that vaults are absent from the nematode, the fly, budding yeast and the mustard plant traces to a homology search the authors themselves called cursory, plus inherited literature. Absent and undetectable by that search are not distinguished, and one of those same authors states the protein's distribution beyond deuterostomes is too distant for direct homology search with available methods. This is the weakest-method claim in the whole subject and should not be carried as an observing of absence.

## 9. One new observing: forming that depends on alignment along a line

Human major vault protein refolded from full denaturant forms vault-sized particles only while bound to a linear scaffold.

**The observing, with its method.** Expression in *Escherichia coli*; lysis in 6 M guanidinium hydrochloride; purification in 8 M urea, wash at pH 6.0, elution at pH 4.0. Refolding in 100 mM sodium phosphate at pH 7.0 by the methods and pH 6.5 to 7.0 by the results, 50 to 100 mM sodium chloride, 1.5 mM magnesium chloride, 27 to 400 mM residual urea, at 22 °C then 4 °C for one to two days. Light scattering returns radii of 26.8 nm with a C-terminal DNA-binding tag and copurified DNA, 26.4 nm with an N-terminal DNA-binding tag, 30.75 nm with a hyaluronic-acid-binding tag and its ligand, about 60 nm bodies for untagged protein, and 254.5, 223.5 and 255 nm for scaffold-only controls. Electron microscopy of the first variant returns 23 per cent of particles near 27 nm, 38 per cent near 42 nm, 38 per cent near 57 nm, 45 ± 13 nm overall, mostly half-vault-like with full forms rare; the second returns full forms near 40 by 75 nm at n = 4 and half forms near 40 nm at n = 8. Copurified DNA is 1.2 mg/mL, length unknown. Nuclease treatment abolishes assembly of the DNA-binding variant; the hyaluronic-acid variant assembles only with its ligand present. Untagged protein and two non-binding control tags give no such particles. One variant assembles within 20 minutes, another over two days, a third immediately. No yield or efficiency is reported. No individual particle is followed.

**Partings the paper carries**, kept whole: a wash at pH 6.0 against a wash at pH 5.3; a refolding buffer of 100 mM phosphate against 20 mM phosphate at pH 6.6 in later sections; a length unit printed where a molar unit is expected.

**The field's explaining**, marked as the field's: scaffold-binding tags attach copies to a linear molecule, aligning them rotationally and along it, standing in for guidance by a polyribosome. The authors state this is inferred, not observed, and leave open whether the particles carry the canonical form, why full forms are rare, and whether the objects are assemblies or misfolded material.

**The between.** The field's inferred mechanism is copies of one sequence aligned rotationally and translationally along a line, forming only when so aligned. The proposed natural explaining is co-chainings sharing orientation along and across co-linearly. The two are close enough that the resemblance is worth naming and close enough that the resemblance is the danger: alignment along a laboratory molecule is not a society's co-linearizing, and the paper's own word for it is a mimicry of a cellular arrangement.

What makes it more than a resemblance is that the observing is a dependence rather than a picture — remove the scaffold and nothing forms. A dependence can be pushed, and §11 says how.

A conflict stands beside it. The same polyribosome-templating idea, proposed from static electron microscopy and fractionation of insect-cell material, was tested in intact cells by tomography, which found no polyribosomes beside partial vaults and ribosomes largely excluded from the granule beds where vaults sit. Both accounts go across whole.

## 10. Concerns: two sayings parting, both carried

## 12. What to distinguish next, in order

**12.1 The hand.** First, because it is cheap, because the deposited maps already hold it, and because three sentences of the living files turn on it. What is wanted: a per-chain skew angle relative to the long axis, and an enantiomer determined independently rather than inherited from an earlier model. On the present record the wall is described as straight and the hand is mostly assigned — so this is the measurement that would either reach §3.3 and §3.7 or break them.

**12.2 The scaffold dependence, pushed.** Vary a scaffold's length and stiffness and see whether particle size follows; fix a length and see whether chain count follows. A size that follows a scaffold separates alignment along a line from a shell finding its own closing, which no present observing separates.

**12.3 Closing, at one particle.** Every closing count above is a property of a preparation. The registry entry states its own breaking condition. Of the methods that could supply it: single-particle mass measurement now follows one molecule for up to an hour at kilodalton precision, but its demonstrated floor is changes above about twenty kilodaltons on analytes above two hundred — and one chain on this shell is roughly one and a third per cent of its mass, far below that. High-speed atomic-force microscopy reaches tens of frames per second and would see a subunit-sized topographic change without being able to weigh it. Time-resolved cryogenic microscopy gives separate grids per time point, never one particle. Single-molecule energy transfer is the natural method for a contact releasing and re-forming, and has never been applied to vaults at all.

**12.4 The three edges, measured rather than counted.** The towel correspondence asks for two wide ends, a narrow tightly wrapping centre, and three edges stiffening and relaxing into and out of that centre in right-spiral co-sequencing. What would reach it is a measurement returning the centre's span and the ends' spans together, through one loading and its release, on one particle. The existing traces return one height. Three cap conformations must not be taken for the three edges on the strength of the count alone — that is the error §4 already demonstrates.

**12.5 Carbon ingesting and exhausting.** Furthest from any observing, and nothing this session changes that. What would begin it is material entering and leaving an identified participant at the participant's own grain, distinguished from material occupying the particle's central space. Natural Biology §7.5 holds the rule such an observing must respect: an emanation lives again only with a coupling other than the one it left, a grain down or a self across, and the test is a ratio intact rather than the going across itself. The present record contains no single-particle measurement of anything entering or leaving a vault; every cargo claim is a static snapshot or a simulation.
