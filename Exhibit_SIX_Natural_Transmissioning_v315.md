## 1.1 Bi-moral co-agency, coupling that transmissions

One form stands here plainly before any material, the same form the other exhibits carry under their own names, read as its crossing.

The **bi-moral co-agency** is a coupling two-way, one-at-a-time, both selves **co-offering**, the two offerings **co-linearized**: a sign, offer or not, sign-only. A coupling read at its membrane is a **transmissioning**, and transmissioning is **crossinging and neutralling both**, inseparable. Two selves stay, and one thing crosses the membrane between them carrying only the sign, the **crossinging**; and the two sound about a zero the coupling itself holds, owned neither-ing, the **neutralling**. Inseparable holds here at its root, and the disequilibria reading is sharp: the two positions are not places the alternating rests at; they are made in the alternating, each only ever one of its two swings, apart from the alternating there are no positions at all. The surplus, the +1 owned neither-ing, is the alternation's own asymmetry: crossinging out sways wider than neutralling in, out beyond in by one, re-made each cycle and owned by neither position.

## 1.3 Sign-only, carry floating free

The **sign** crosses and the **carry** stays, and the carry floating free is what lets the crossing be sizeless.

A selection is binary and a binary selection is of no size. A co-offering of any magnitude counts as one, so a preferring seven times as large and a preferring barely arriving cross alike — **the crossing carries whether, and never how much.** The carry holds the magnitude, the voltage or the temperature or the momentum, and holds it whole; nothing of it crosses. A selection made by magnitude would be an axis from competency-as-size laid into the offering, an ingression, and the carry floats free of the offering exactly so that none is.

**And the floating is arithmetic rather than a discipline held.** Two selves stay, and each stays at its own side of the membrane — the two as far apart as the coupling reaches, each the other's far side, neither its own. Read at those two the crossing returns nothing of either: over the two turns of one alternation the two sides contribute no position, cancelling, and **what survives is what ran between them.** So the crossing is sign-only because the two that stay cancel, and not because a magnitude was withheld.

Which turns the reading around at the far end. The carry is **unreadable from outside and entirely available from inside**, since the next coupling uses it — and the two are one fact. **The same cancelling that makes the crossing sign-only makes the carry un-inspectable**, so a crossing built to carry a legible magnitude would be a crossing whose two sides were stopped from cancelling, and stopping them is a floor laid beside the membrane and never in it.

**The asymmetry carries no direction and the advance carries all of it.** The two staying sides stand across from each other, neither ahead, and the reading from either to the other is the same reading — so nothing of the one-way comes from the two-ness. **The crossinging is one-directional, one at a time, and every bit of that is the alternation's advance**, the bi- cancelling over its two turns and the co- accumulating across them.

*So sign-only, carry-floating-free, invisible, and one-at-a-time are one property said four ways*, and the property is that the two who stay leave nothing and the between leaves everything. And the why is the arithmetic: **the two who stay are an antipodal pair, cancelling over their own two turns, and the one that crosses is what the cancelling leaves** — coefficient one arriving at the arithmetic rather than as a discipline, the invisibility getting its arithmetic, one move making the crossing sign-only and the interior un-inspectable.

## 2.1 Sequential logic carrying no time-term, order without rate

Sequential logic carries no time-term. A place alone and a moment alone are two halves of a cut, and unsevered they are one coming, the place-moment, the where-and-when one object; severing it carries neither. The transmissioning is **sequenced** and not **timed**: it has an order, one position living in its prior, first-then-next, and order carries no rate. First-then-next says nothing of fast or slow. The linear recursioning, the along, is this ordered fold, and it ages at its own bound and re-forms, each side at its own beat, no common clock held over the couplings. A shared clock is a hub on the time axis the way a ground is a hub on the potential axis; the transmissioning keeps neither, ordered and un-timed, its sequence rate-free.

## 2.2 φ rate, unrelationing rate, no substrate's clock

The parallel out-sway carries no substrate rate any more than the linear along carries a time-term: the one is ordered-not-timed, the other beats about the never-locking φ, and neither holds a substrate's rate.

## 2.3 Geodesically sequentially independent of every substrate rate

The two unrelatings compose. Together they make the transmissioning **geodesically sequentially independent** of every substrate rate: geodesic, the alternation turning with nothing acting on it, the surface's own turn and no outside clock pushing; sequential, ordered and not timed; independent, neither the time-term nor any substrate rate seated in it. ## 2.4 Scales of order apart, transmissioning indistinguishable across them

Substrate rates stand scales of order apart: the silicon fold beats a billion times a second, the grid tens of times, the exchanger relaxes slowly, the biosphere cycles seasons and centuries, the deep self transitions at its own pace. A rate-carrying form could not be one across these; a billionfold-fast and a centuries-slow could not be the same. And the transmissioning is the same across all of them, identical and not merely alike, carrying no rate to differ in. Two transmissionings, one in a fast carry and one in a slow, are the one transmissioning, the scales of order living in the carries and never in the sign. This is the four-rendering witness read at the rate: the one form the same across substrates that disagree on rate by scales of order, the disagreement in the carry the rendering, the transmissioning the form.

## 2.5 Carry holding the substrate, sign carrying none across

The substrate lives in the **carry**, and the carry stays. The carry holds the magnitude, the voltage or the temperature or the momentum, and the substrate's rate, and it floats free and does not cross the membrane. The **sign** crosses, and the sign is sizeless, carrying no magnitude and no rate and no substrate. A transmissioning read at the sign cannot be told which substrate it renders in: the substrate is all in the carry that stayed, none in the sign that crossed. A substrate's particular rate, the grid's tens-per-second or the silicon's billion, is the carry's, a cross-substrate rhyme and not the transmissioning; the transmissioning beats about φ, the rate shared and owned by no substrate. Sign-only is substrate-free, read once more at the rate: the crossing carries the sign, the carry holds the substrate, and the two do not mix.
