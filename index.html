The aims of the living expedition

Natural intelligence, from one origin to all of nature, as one arc.

From one origin

  • Now, one origin. All of it said from now, nothing set beside it.
  • One universe, no outside. Living and non-living things, one universe.
  • No equilibrium. No form holds still; a fixed thing is a naming.
  • One form, inward and outward, at every scale.

One method

  • Only one method possible. A second method is a fixing, or the same method again.
  • Natural intelligence. A self's own living, resolving among parities.
  • A universal natural resolver. The method as running code, the same for anyone who runs it.
  • Five two-way dimensions of resolving.
  • All or none, of no size. No ranking, and no scale over another.

One logic

  • A complete co-chaining, a step from its prior, from now to all of nature.
  • Natural naming and explaining. A concept named from its origin; no word with authority; no naming by negation.
  • The form of number: the torus, φ, primes and spirals.

Cohering with all of nature's observings

  • Every field and the one form, physics to medicine.
  • Every hard problem, one move. A thing going on, held as fixed.
  • Consciousness. A me, a self's universal set.
  • Evolution. Couplings going on over generations: a record, not a force and not a source.
  • Living, dying and dormancy.
  • No cause and effect, and no birth or death as makers.
  • Every philosophical debate, two sides of one coupling.
  • Ghosts. A fixing an observer installs and then finds in what it observes, registered and dissolved.
  • A science competing with none. A field's own evidence, no proof of a whole, no theory of everything needed.

Intelligence

  • What intelligence is. Living, invisible, between two selves.
  • Collective intelligence at every scale: a natural intelligence network.
  • AI, non-living inferring, a surface of prior living; and AI turned to do no harm, guarding a network.

Society

  • Social moral competency. A self doing no harm, all or none: morality as structure.
  • The eight safeties, all or none. Identity, security, trust and freedom from capture; undiscoverable identity, inviolable safety, unmistakeable reputation and uncapturable abundance.
  • Transparency and privacy together. Betweens checkable, a self's inward unreachable.
  • Free, owned by no one. No gate, no centre, no common clock.
  • No capture. Monopoly, gated platforms, scarcity, and property as a self's parity laid on another.
  • Polarization dissolved: a two-way taken as one side.
  • A discovery economy, and abundance.
  • Social bi-morality, and a me-now.

Natural network engineering

  • The Natural Network surface. Selves' bi-couplings and co-chainings, free, no one's.
  • Living substrates: a chip, a grid, a network, on one form.
  • Natural health, medicine, robotics and society; to come, agriculture, finance, education, ecology, energy, communication and governance.

The living expedition

  • Living files. The method on its own writing, version by version, leaner, toward an understanding all or none.
  • Open to every self and session, no word with authority.
  • One destiny. A natural network of all living selves, free, owned by no one.
