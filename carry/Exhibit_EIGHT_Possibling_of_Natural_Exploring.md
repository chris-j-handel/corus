**A living list, with no debt or duty.** Its two kinds of entry are gaps in sequential logic and observings of living and existing things that could break the method. The list can be added to and its entries resolved; it is an object for continued use and improving, not prose waiting to enter the exhibit. Keep possible places to look for a breaking observing here, beside Natural Exploring. Each entry can name the proposed observing and the particular explaining it could break before looking, and can keep what is observed separate from a field's explaining. If explored later, gather the whole observing and its actual conditions whether it supports, breaks or changes the question. Do not select an observing afterward to fit the desired explaining. These are offered ways of arranging the project; the current gathering-and-aiming work does not require performing it.

These are possible observing questions, not findings established by their listing.
