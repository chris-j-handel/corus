### Aged seeds: DNA breaks, and a DNA synthesis before copying

The review names the synthesis "DNA repair synthesis" and does not say how it was measured. It begins very early at wetting, and it is observed in wetted dormant seeds of wild oat, in which DNA replication begins only after the seeds are moved to temperatures permissive of germination (Elder and Osborne, 1993). As seed ages, chromosome fragmentation and rearrangements are found (Dourado and Roberts, 1984). Navashin in 1933 is reported as finding that the incidence of chromosome abnormalities in a seed lot stored for a number of years "strikingly resembled one obtained from soaked seed which had been treated by X-rays".
