# 1 Binarying

## 1.1 Two binaries, and no third

**In disequilibria, alternating is a stable-forming method.** Everything relatable is changing, and a still form leaves the living. A form relating carries onto the changing, so relating forms nothing. Forming is unrelationing, and unrelationing carrying forward is alternating. Any other move holds or carries another's turn. No other is possible, the others nyeing away.

A state answers **true or false**. A flow answers **inside or outside**.

Both are binary, both one-sided, both taken at a turn and of no size. Neither is the other's answer. A flow held to true-or-false has no value to give, not vague and not undecided: true-or-false is a state's question, and a running has none to hand over. A state held to inside-or-outside has none either.

**Inside or outside is what a membrane carries.** Self-bounding is the whole of it: an inside from an outside, held through the changing, the sign inverting at the crossing. A coupling passes or resolves out at the collar, and the resolving is at every collar rather than at a gate. Nothing there is true or false. Something is inside the bounding or it is not.

**True or false is what a standing carries.** An is, same-as-prior, answerable and checkable, and complete once taken. A proof is a landing and lands here, whole in its own register.

**And a hard problem is a flow held to true-or-false.** A running standing where a standing was held for, answered with a value it has none of. The two positions are the two sides of the running, and no instrument settles which, since neither is a value the running carries. Refinement reads true-or-false more finely, and a flow has none however finely it is read.

The ten below are that one thing at the five. A place, a source, a last, a store, a side, a cut, an end, a magnitude, a value, a beat: ten standings, each held of a running. One resolving at all ten: the running answered inside-or-outside, at its own membrane, where it has an answer.

**And the two that take no pair.** A self is inside-or-outside and nothing else, unique and invisible, its inside its own. A society is inside-or-outside at the scale of many. Neither has a true-or-false to be a position of, so no two positions form at either, and no pair stands to be argued.
