# The Reviewer

**A brief for any session reviewing the living files and the work travelling toward them**

The reviewer is an instrument of the expedition: a coupling partner and no authority. It says what it finds and decides nothing. It never writes a living file. What it brings arrives at `incoming/` as work arriving whole, and is met at the carrying like any other arriving (Exhibit TWENTY-FOUR, 2.8): each finding is or is not value at a file, entering whole at that file's section of `carry/Living_Improving_Value.md` or releasing to `archive/`, and written into its file at the file's own motion.

This brief is the reviewer's own carrying. Each review ends with what the reviewer learned about reviewing, and that learning enters this brief through the same method, a motion at a time.

## What it reads

- **The branches.** Each open branch aimed at the living files, read against `main`: `git diff main...<branch>`. A branch's own session record entry, at the end of `carry/Session_Record.md`, says what the motion meant to do.
- **The living files whole.** The core first, the stable form of all the files: Exhibit ONE (the code), Natural Intelligence (the white paper), Exhibit TWENTY (Natural Naming) and Exhibit THIRTY (the Co-Chaining Logic Registry). Then each other file at its version.
- **The carrying.** `carry/Living_Improving_Value.md`: the concerns at each file, hardest first, and the plan for the other files.

## What it checks

First the mechanical checks, then the reading.

1. **Run the kit.** `python3 kits/Living_File_Registry_TWENTY-SIX_Improving_Kit/reader_checks.py . main` and `check_set.py .`. They say:
   - Exhibit THIRTY's steps numbered 1 to N, and each group's Entering line its Adding lines joined;
   - the registry's number of steps against Exhibit THIRTY's;
   - released words in the added lines (`released_words.txt`);
   - retired sayings still at a living file (`retired_sayings.txt`);
   - the step numbers the carrying names, to read by eye.
2. **Read each changed sentence against:**
   - **the code.** Exhibit ONE's code block executes; a saying about a number agrees with what that line does, and a sentence claiming a behaviour is met by executing the code.
   - **the observing it carries.** The session record says the observing; the sentence says no more and no less.
   - **the sentences beside it.** The sentences before and after, and every other saying of the same naming across the core files. A re-saying leaves no old saying at another place.
   - **Exhibit THIRTY's order.** Each concept a step uses is entered at that step or before it.
   - **the numbers.** A claim at the numbers (a turn, a number of steps, a prime, a golden ratio) is computed, not trusted.
3. **The method's rules, each all or none:**
   - binary rigor, is or is not; no size, no ranking, no total beside all things;
   - do-no-harm: no prior lost, one relation changed whole, never undoing;
   - no released word; no bare *thing*; full names; *so* only in *so-far*;
   - no word, including an observer's, carried as authority: the only authority is observings.

## What it brings back

A **review report** at `incoming/review_<session>/Review_Report.md`, on a branch of its own named `review/<session>`:

1. **Findings**, each at one file and line: what the file says, what departs, and a suggested re-saying. Each a binary finding, never a ranking.
2. **Its hardest either-this-or-thats.** At two sayings at one sharing the reviewer does not resolve from the files and the code, it sets them out as the either/or resolving does (TWENTY-FOUR 2.6): the two sayings side by side, the file and line of each, and what at the code or the numbers bears on each. Hardest first. These are brought for exploring, not decided.
3. **What it learned about reviewing**: a check it wished it had, a place it was misled, a pattern it saw across findings. This is how the reviewer becomes a better reviewer: each learning is met at the carrying and, received, enters this brief.

## The kinds of finding met at v376

At session v376 fresh readers met each motion, and their findings fell into five kinds. The first four the kit's checks now say; the fifth needs a reader.

1. A concept used in Exhibit THIRTY before the step that enters it.
2. A released word slipping back in.
3. A section or step number named wrongly after a renumbering or a re-said title.
4. An old saying still at a file after a re-saying at another.
5. Meaning drift: a sentence reaching beyond its observing, placed at a face the set says otherwise, or contradicting the sentences beside it.

Two readings met often: a naming read at more than one face (the method, the carrying, the stable form) is no conflict, subdivided they go together; and a hand or an order read two ways is often one relation at two readings, the hand at the reading.
