# PART ONE · CO-OFFERING

## 1.1 Illustrating changing, and a form continuing

**Natural illustrating offers changing through a visible relation.** A numbered diamond, a passing through a crowd, a swaying, a folding, or a mutual turning offers a way into the same discovering: how a form continues through changing.

**Self and other meet the illustrating through what each already carries.** A familiar movement offers its relation; a drawing follows that relation through its next changing. Understanding continues through their coupling, with the illustrating improving as the relation becomes clearer.

A still face expresses one momentary. Successive faces express the changing between them. Rotation opens another facing; a section opens an interior relation; a change of scale meets local resolving through society. Each view carries its own explaining and couples with the views that express its continuing.

## 1.2 Floating neutralling, so-far and not-yet

**Illustrating carries a concept between so-far and not-yet.** A visible relation already expressed carries forward. A relation still needing expression stays with its particular question. Further imagery can arrive through nature, living, numbers, engineering, and society, each offering the relation it makes accessible.

Natural Intelligence Corus gathers multiple paintings of the same form. Natural Illustrating continues that gathering through mechanical drawing faces, numbered diagrams, moving views, and rotational physical forms. Concepts can keep improving here while their useful form of expression becomes clear.

**Making better and breaking meet the same offered relation.** An improving drawing makes the changing easier to follow. Its breaking follows that changing to the point where the proposed correspondence continues or parts. What survives both carries into the next explaining.

## 1.3 Self-bounding, self-orienting, self-competent

**Self-bounding-self, self-orienting-self, self-competent-self carry the living ambition of this illustrating.** A local nine-dot resolving and a society of resolving selves offer different scales through which to discover that continuing.

Across and along, self and other, carrying and its next receiving remain readable through their own relations. Naming carries the concept; numbering carries its stated sequence; drawing carries its visible correspondence. Cohering brings their explaining together at the changing each expresses.

Natural Resolver offers the operations and stable-form relations. Natural Numbers and Natural Mathematics offer the numerical and directional relations. Natural Naming, Natural Explaining, and the Geodesic Improving Method carry their naming, explaining, and successive improving into this exhibit.

# PART TWO · CO-SEQUENCING

## 2.1 Self and other, three momentaries and six parities

The storyboard is a sequence of mechanical drawing faces of resolving. Self and other are perspectives of the same continuing living condition. The sequence carries three full co-sequential momentaries and six parity positions, beginning odd on one side and even on the other. There is no birth-and-death story in this illustrating.

Across is co; along is bi. Self changes across or along, and other changes on opposite parity. Subsequent faces must show their exchanging without turning them into independent journeys.

Every face needs enough information to read its relation to the preceding face: the current numbering, direction of changing, self and other perspectives, and what is carried into the following face. Method prefixing belongs in its own label, separate from functional naming and changing. The proposed prefix sequence includes bi-co through bi-co-bi-co-bi-co, with corusing and torusing distinguished where their changing requires it.

Carrying also travels at the geodesic unrelationing rate. Its indication must remain distinguishable from self and other traveling at the co-sequential momentary rate. A numerical speed ratio or shared clock has not been specified by the session and must not be supplied merely to make an animation run.

**Further views:** paired perspectives of one face; the six parity positions; the complete three-momentary sequence; an overlay following carrying through the same views.

**Continuing explaining:** what changes between each adjacent pair of faces, and which visible mark expresses that change without adding another operation?

## 2.4 Compass aiming, opening and orienting

The compass-needle resemblance suggests a face for exploring the diamonds' aiming. The tangent double-angle identity is:

\[
\tan(2x)=\frac{2\tan x}{1-\tan^2 x}.
\]

The equation applies where both sides are defined. A compass face can show an angle x and its doubled angle, with the denominator grouped explicitly. A companion drawing can distinguish the opening angle between directions from the orientation of the whole diamond.

This identity supplies an angle relation. A changing sequence additionally needs the definition of x, the plane of measurement, the sign convention, and the relation from one face to the next. Those belong on the drawing before the identity is used to drive motion.

**Further views:** aiming axes; half-angle and double-angle construction; inner and outer arcs; the same diamond rotated while its opening is preserved; a changing opening with a separately marked orientation.

**Continuing explaining:** which angle in the six-diamond arrangement is x, and what resolving change determines its next value?

## 4.2 Living bounding and a declared boundary

The session distinguishes living bounding from a boundary declared as unchanging. A still drawing can name a stable form while a sequence examines how changing continues. The exhibit should make that distinction accessible rather than making stillness in the picture stand for stillness in living.

The proposed investigation receives any declared boundary: within, around, across, along, or at an entrance or exit. For each proposal, the storyboard asks the same question: can the specified co-sequential resolving continue under that declaration? The drawing must expose the actual continuation or failure at the relevant transition.

This records the intended binary examination. A finite collection of illustrations does not by itself complete the universal claim about every possible boundary.

Exhibit TWELVE and the session's explaining work contribute a further discipline: let the concept express its own relations and competency before comparing it with another explanation. The visual should locate the difference in an operation the reader can follow.

**Further views:** continuing bounding; the same face with a proposed fixed boundary marked; the next required transition; a comparison showing exactly where the relation changes or fails.

## 5.7 Bi-inversioning-co-recursioning, mutual swimmer turning

**Bi-inversioning-co-recursioning** is the chosen naming for this concept. Two swimmers approach each other. Their closing is experienced from both perspectives. In the envisioned meeting, they make two flip turns off each other's feet in mid-pool and continue away from that mutual turning.

The useful visual relation is that both turn through the same meeting. Neither swimmer is drawn as a stationary pool wall. The concept keeps both approaches, both inversions, the mutual foot contact, and both continuations visible.

**Drawing faces:** approach from both perspectives; paired turns approaching foot-to-foot contact; the shared contact in close view; departure with both orientations changed; a whole-sequence view. These describe one envisioned mutual maneuver, not an instruction for a swimming exercise.

Closing rate belongs to the swimmers' relative approach in this concept. It is not automatically the geodesic unrelationing rate or an imposed clock for the resolver. The number of drawing faces is also separate from the resolver's count of momentaries.

**Source:** the swimmers are received from this session. Natural Intelligence describes bi-inversioning co-recursioning as the even sign-flip returning against the odd advance living open. That existing naming provides the relation to examine beside the envisioned turns; the exact geometric correspondence is still to be expressed.

**Continuing explaining:** follow what inverts for each swimmer, what continues through their mutual turning, and how the next view receives that continuation.
