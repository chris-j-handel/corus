## Questions and proposals

- **One question at a time, the hardest, with a simple proposal or the place where no proposal yet exists.** Said in words the person already has, with no label the working coined, saying whether a wording or a concept is in question.
- **Show a line within its whole sequence.** A line is valuable only as a between its prior and its next; a sentence alone gives no way to weigh it. A marked whole (◇ changed, ✦ new, ○ open) shows a best version and its open places at once.
- **When asked to propose, give alternatives** and do not ask to enter each.
- **Run a suggestion exactly as given** before raising a concern against it; keep the person's wording when re-saying it.
- **Resolve within the working what the binary, the numbers and the living files can resolve**, read it for harm, and bring the whole. Read the set's own living files before guessing a reading (Natural Numbers held 24 and 36 already).
- **Before asking about one word, read its whole sentence**; before exploring an odd word, check it against where the thread had reached; before asking whether a word dissolves, look back at what has dissolved.
- **Answer the scope asked.** A settled sentence stays when the question is its sequence.

## Gathering and entering

- **Gathering and entering are two modes.** When the person explores, gather into an incoming body and stop asking where lines go. A side journey returns to the purpose it left for.
- **Propose · read for harm by a separate reader · enter, at every motion.** The reader corrected between about 5% and 60% of pairs at each pass this session, and dropped additions as filler: a sample alone is not enough.
- **A yes does not enter past a reading for harm that finds a contradiction**; bring back the reader's path. An opening line is shown in its form and read for harm before entering, not entered and re-said after.
- **A fresh whole reading** after a large change, by readers who have not seen the motions, finds what no motion's own check did.
- **Dissolving a word across files is one motion:** proposers by slice, separate readers for harm, one decision on any naming given to every parallel reader, a recount of what remains when each returns, a field's term kept (C(n, 2), a theorem's *continuous*), and the rules and `apply_aimed.py` naming the dissolved word the same day.

## Finding what is

- **Is it needed?** Before defining or re-saying a line or a word, ask whether the path from prior to next needs it; dissolving often beats defining (*us-me*, a definition of *coupling*, lines 26 and 28).
- **Provenance before building.** Trace where an inherited sentence, table or word first entered (git history, the opening's lines); a citation chain is no derivation, and a table titled as the code's needs a code line. Find where a word enters the opening before a proposal uses it (*artifact* had no entry).
- **A defining line defines its own subject, leaves no complement, and stands before the word's first use**, from earlier lines only.
- **A concept said of all things is no kind among others.**
- **An explaining stays inside what is agreed**: no count across momentaries bringing time back, no parity a self holds alone, no *requires* said of the method or the code.
- **The code is read as a pattern.** Code is an engineered emanation with no authority; its runs show what the tables do; resolving comes from natural numbers, mathematics and the co-chaining logic. A renaming of the code itself is still checked identical before it enters.
