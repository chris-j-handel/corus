# Saying each gathered item by no one

**A sweep re-says items already gathered, keeping each value whole, with who offered it, the path of learning and our own experiments' details leaving.** Read `GATHERING_BRIEF.md` first: its method stands here.

## The method

- No one's words have authority: a person's, a working's, a reader's, a field's, a living file's.
- A value is the natural pattern or cohering it reveals, said by no one.
- Who said or offered what, sessions, versions, dates of a working, the path of learning, which code or set-up or step a run used, sources, publishing years, prizes, and pointers or cross-references with no need: each leaves. Git keeps it.
- A saying carried only by its offering, parting from an observing or from co-chaining, leaves; a pattern it reveals stays. A concern whose two sides are each carried by an observing or by co-chaining (a definition in a sequence counts) keeps both, said as two sayings or two namings, never decided.
- Do no harm to gathered value.

## Keeping the standing while the who leaves

- **The who leaves; the standing stays.** Unchecked, reported, inferred, calculated, modelled or proposed is said "an anticipated observing:" with the claim, and never a plain claim or observing. There is only observing and explaining: an interpretation is said plainly as an explaining, and no item says "a reading".
- **A condition is no record.** "Resting on…", "given…", "with priors…", "only while…" are conditions a pattern needs; they stay.
- **Keep scope.** "No file it read" stays limited; an explaining never becomes a finite claim about all.
- **A quoted saying in no living file** stays marked as in no living file.
- **Add nothing.** No concern frame where material was only set beside a sentence; no new word, claim or side.
- **A line left as only a list of files** says what it opens as still-possibling, or leaves.
- **Lines sharing a pattern go alike.** Scan the file for the leftover words after re-saying: offer, offering, proposed, proposal, working, session, reader, reported, executing, set-up, step, the person, the field's, a version tag.
- **A concern removed** has its pointers in other gathering-value files found and re-said.
- The kind label (`- **An explaining** · `, `- **Possible exploring** · `, `- **Possible incohering** · `) stays first; a quoted living sentence stays word for word; every number stays whole; Natural Explaining's craft; no released word added and no word swapped to avoid one. Two namings of an atom, carbon atom or not, stay as two namings, by no one.

## Output

The re-sayer writes `<NAME>_replace.json` (`[{"file", "old", "new"}]`, each "old" loaded exactly from the file, never retyped), `<NAME>_remove.json` (exact lines), and `<NAME>_notes.json` (`[{"old": first 100 characters, "why"}]`). The separate reader writes `<NAME>_reading.json`: `{"fix": [{"old", "new", "finding"}], "restore": [exact removed lines], "remove": [exact lines whose value was only an offering, missed], "checked": N}`. `replace_lines.py` and `remove_gathered.py` apply all or none.
