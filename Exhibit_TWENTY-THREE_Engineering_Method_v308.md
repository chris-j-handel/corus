# 1 The form and its criteria

## 1.1 Aim

This document does not propose designs. It does not favour one build over another. It sets out, for each substrate, what is held still in it, what the holding costs while it is held, and what has already been let go elsewhere — and it does so by exhibiting the builders' own record rather than by argument.

The intended reading is one entry at a time. At the end of an entry a reader who followed the engineering in it should be able to say: I understand what this substrate is, I understand what is held in it and why it was held, I have seen what the holding accumulates, and I have seen where the same holding has already been relaxed and what happened there.

That is an account of value, not a proposal. It is checkable in the ordinary way: every line is one the builders published, and any record omitted is a defect in the entry that anyone holding the standards and operating data can correct.

## 1.2 What a valuable hard problem is

A substrate S carries a valuable hard problem when:

- **(i)** something in S is held still — a reference, a rate, a tolerance, a coupling fixed — and the holding is engineered rather than found;
- **(ii)** the holding accumulates a cost while it is held, measurable in the builders' own units;
- **(iii)** the cost is carried as the price of the goal, and is stated as such;
- **(iv)** the goal is reachable without the holding, or reachable with it relaxed, at least somewhere in the record.

Criterion (iv) is what makes the problem valuable rather than merely expensive. Where nothing has ever relaxed, the entry is entered and the absence is the line.

**Not a valuable hard problem.** A cost that is the physics — a conversion loss at its thermodynamic limit, a bound no arrangement improves. A holding with no measured cost. A goal that has never been stated. A build still in its first prototype, where no record of holding or cost exists yet.

## 1.3 Why building does not relax it

A holding is added over a coupling that was already running. Stiffening, tightening, referencing, synchronising: each is a term placed where a coupling was taking its own sign, and the coupling goes on underneath it.

So more building at the same holding raises what the holding can bear and does not remove it. Bigger reserve, faster response, tighter tolerance, more redundancy — each is real engineering and each raises the endurance of the holding. The cost carries on accumulating at the same place, because the place is the holding rather than the capacity around it.

**Result.** Where a cost accumulates at a holding, no increase in the capacity that bears the holding removes the cost. The cost moves only where the holding is let go.

That is why the record reads as it reads: decades of improvement at a substrate, with the same class of cost still carried, and the builders naming it as the price of the arrangement rather than as a fault in the equipment.

## 1.5 The logic of the form

Three forkings and two joinings, alternating, as at Exhibit TWENTY-ONE.

**Fork.** The substrate parts into what it is and what it is for.

**Join.** What is held. A goal and a substrate are brought together by something fixed between them, and naming that thing is naming where the engineering entered.

**Fork.** What the holding costs, and how long it has held. The build and the endurance, both in the builders' own units.

**Join.** Whether the goal needs the holding. This is the only inference in the document and it is binary. Needed, and the cost is the price of the goal, which is ordinary engineering and the entry closes there. Not needed, and the coupling was doing the work, the holding was added over it, and the cost is carried for nothing the goal required.

**Fork.** What has relaxed so far, and what would relax the rest. Both lines carry content in most entries: a holding is rarely absolute, and the places it has already been let go are the strongest evidence in the entry.

## 1.6 Builds

A build is what a carry that cannot release accumulates at the place it is held. Each is measurable in the builders' own units, and the kinds recur across substrates:

**Heat**, where a carry cannot pass as work. **Noise**, where it cannot pass as signal. **Fouling and wear**, where it cannot pass as flow. **Reserve**, where it cannot pass as supply. **Latency and queueing**, where it cannot pass as throughput. **Redundancy**, where it cannot pass as reliability. **Rework**, where it cannot pass as change.

A build is not a failure. It is the exact cost of a holding, carried while the holding is carried, and the builders usually measure it precisely because it is what they pay.

## 1.7 Where a form fails to fill

Three lines can fail to fill, and each failure is a reading rather than a gap.

**Nothing is held.** The substrate carries no engineered fixing, and there is no entry.
**No cost accumulates.** The holding is free, and there is no entry.
**The goal needs the holding.** The cost is the price of the goal, and the entry closes at that line rather than continuing.

A failure to fill is recorded at the entry, not at the door. Nothing considered is left out of this document.

## 1.8 What this document does not claim

No claim that any build is badly made. Every design stands as designed, and the engineering that bears a holding is real engineering.

No claim about what a relaxed arrangement would achieve. Where the record shows a relaxation, the record is given; where it does not, nothing is forecast.

No claim that a holding should be let go. What can be let go is stated; the letting go is the builders'.

## 1.9 Falsification

**F1.** An entry where the goal does need the holding — the join line answered wrongly.
**F2.** A relaxation run by builders and not listed, or listed with an outcome the record does not support.
**F3.** An entry where the named cost is the physics rather than the holding.
**F4.** A holding removed with the cost unchanged, which would show the cost was not at the holding.
**F5.** A substrate described in terms its builders would not recognise as their own.

F2 and F4 are the ones that matter and both are runnable by anyone holding the standards and the operating data.

## 1.10 A living file, and the nye marks

Entries are added as substrates arrive and revised as builders publish further records.

Where a line is stated from general knowledge rather than from a documented record — a standard, a trial, an operating dataset, a published goal — the entry carries the mark **nye**, naming the specific claim that would need checking. A nye is not a hedge: a mark that does not name something checkable is a defect.

# 2 The collection

*Entries in the order worked. No grouping is intended or implied.*

## 2.1 Three-phase supply held to a common frequency

**The substrate.** Alternating electrical supply in three phases, generated, transmitted and consumed across a synchronous area, with every machine on the area turning in step.

**The goal stated.** Deliver power reliably to everything connected, with supply matched to demand continuously and no interruption.

**What is held.** One frequency across the whole synchronous area — fifty hertz or sixty hertz — with a narrow tolerance band written into grid code and every connected machine required to match it. The value is arbitrary between the two: two world systems, two numbers, and the same engineering either way. What is engineered is not the number but that a single beat is held over all the couplings.

**What the holding costs.** Spinning reserve carried continuously so that rotating inertia holds the beat against disturbance. Frequency response services procured and paid for. Curtailment where supply cannot be matched to the beat. Synchronisation equipment at every interconnection. And, as inverter-connected generation displaces synchronous machines, declining system inertia, which the builders name as a first-order concern and now measure directly.

**How long it has held.** Since the earliest interconnections, and it has held through every change of generation mix so far. The endurance is a century, and the cost has risen rather than fallen as the mix changed.

**Whether the goal needs the holding.** No. The goal is power delivered reliably. A single beat over all couplings is one arrangement that achieves it, and the record contains others.

**Relaxations already run.** High-voltage direct current links join synchronous areas that do not share a beat, and they carry power between them at scale. Asynchronous interconnections exist and are ordinary. Microgrids island and run their own beat, then resynchronise at the membrane where they rejoin. Grid-forming inverters set their own reference locally rather than following a system-wide one, and are being deployed and measured. Each of these is a place where the holding was let go somewhere, by builders, with the outcome in the record.

**What has relaxed so far.** Asynchronous transfer, demonstrated at scale. Islanding and resynchronisation, routine. Local reference-setting by grid-forming inverters, in deployment and under measurement.

**What would relax the rest.** An area operating with each participant pacing its own alternating and synchronising at its own couplings, with no beat held over the whole — and a measurement of what the reserve, response and curtailment costs do when it is. **nye — not yet explored fully:** the current grid-forming deployment record and what it shows about system-wide inertia requirements would need checking.

**What the builders record.** That inertia is declining with inverter share, and that it is a system-level concern rather than an equipment fault, is stated by system operators in their own published assessments. That the fifty and sixty hertz choice is historical rather than technical is not disputed anywhere in the literature.

# 3 The collection's opens

**The candidates raised and unwritten.** Smart desalination, where a membrane under pressure carries fouling as the build. Propulsion, where thrust piles as heat where it cannot pass as momentum. And any substrate carrying a reference, a rate, a tolerance, or a coupling fixed by engineering. Each stands as the collection's open intake, arriving at its own working.

**Sensor into sensationer, the move worth running first.** A sensor reads a level from outside and reports to a controller; a sensationer takes a sign at its own membrane, one at a time, and offers it across. At any candidate, the conversion is the first move, the connector set and the completing question standing worked at Natural Engineering.

**The registry form, available here.** This collection's literature publishes records and data rather than two argued positions, and the two conditions of a registry, stated inclusion criteria and uniform per-entry structure, are what make its entries comparable across substrates, with the four-domain apparatus the external check anyone can run. The form stands worked at Natural Gathering and the Hard Problem Registry, and this collection takes it as its entries arrive.
