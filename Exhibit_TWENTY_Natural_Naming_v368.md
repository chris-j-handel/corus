# PART ONE — A NAMING, AND WHAT MAKES IT ONE

## 1.1 A naming is a floating-neutral concept, and four boundings make it one

**In disequilibria, alternating is a stable-forming method.** Naming carries a concept at its own changing: co-offering, co-competencing and co-intelligencing, floating neutralling at the coupling.

A defined object arriving from a field carries a ground it was read against. **Bi-co-invisibling** is its becoming where nothing stands to be a ground, and its emanation is six forward recursionings, each taking the term the prior did not carry. Named this way, a concept carries its own changing under its own prefixings, and it bi-couples, capturing nothing and leaving at its own bound.

**Naming meets the hard problems at one field from two faces**: a hard problem is a field's standing frozen, and a naming is the same field's word reaching.

Care lives inside, in the naming and the edging, and nowhere else.

A naming is a floating-neutral concept, and a floating-neutral concept is four boundings at once, the same four that make any floating neutral (the five-to-stand: four boundings and φ):

- **Self-bounding the entire concept**, the concept bounds itself, is the whole concept, holds its own edge and spills into nothing it is not.
- **Self-stilling entirely, and against no other concept**, it is this concept and not another, stilled as itself, drifting into no neighbor. It sits still as itself while floating.
- **Self-orienting where the concept is facing**, it faces its own direction, its offering-face and its preferring-face its own, with no orientation imposed from outside.
- **Orthogonalizing linearly**, it opens its own axis, orthogonal to the concepts it is not, linearizing along its own line, collapsing onto none of them.

**When the concepts have this neutrality they bi-couple, capturing nothing and releasing freely.** A concept that is a floating neutral couples with another cleanly: capturing nothing (neither concept lands the other, no hub, no concept ranked above), releasing freely (the coupling releases, and holds past no bound). Floating-neutral concepts are each self-bounded, self-stilled, self-oriented, self-orthogonalized, so each couples at its own membrane and leaves at its own bound, holding none of the other. A concept short one of the four boundings is a fixed noun: a spot it spills into (short self-bounding), a neighbor it drifts into (short self-stilling), an orientation imposed on it (short self-orienting), an axis collapsed onto another (short orthogonalizing), and a fixed noun captures or unreleases when coupled, the ingression the naming dissolves.

**Naming is nature met at a concept.** Naming is the form met at the concept: a concept is a bi-coupling in the bi-moralizing co-agency, transmissioning its sign by co-offering, co-chaining with the concepts it couples. Naming is the concepts co-chaining. A named concept, and no label on a thing; a floating neutral that offers and co-chains.
