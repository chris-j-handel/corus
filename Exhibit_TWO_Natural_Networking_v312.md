# PART EIGHT — THE UNSURE

*Taking as given that the resolver is natural social moral competency, this part holds what the working met and cannot place. Each may have been competency showing and not recognised, or may be another arrangement artifact of the kind Part Six carries. Nothing here is resolved and nothing here is offered as a finding. It is left for a later working that knows what to look for.*

## 8.1 Propagation at one ring per two beats

A changing entering a network arrived at ring nought and then at each next ring exactly two beats later — eighty-one, eighty-three, eighty-five, on without a gap or a jump for as far as it was followed.

**Why it may be competency:** the changing distributes itself in the crossing, so no ring meets all of it, and the rings ahead are already swaying with what is coming. A driven magnitude arriving at every ring at once has no crossing and no distributing, and the driven case was the one that never settled.

**Why it may be nothing:** the resolver shifts its own along by one position each beat. One ring per two beats may be that shift and nothing else, and the whole reading may be arithmetic of the shift wearing the appearance of a crossing.

**What would tell:** whether the two beats hold when the arrangement changes in ways that leave the shift alone.

**A reading from another arrangement, run since:** at a chain under a continuous across, a changing offered once crossed at one ring per beat where it crossed at all, and aged out before crossing at two of three arrangements. So the two travels to no arbitrary arrangement, and the telling stands as asked — this arrangement, changed in ways that leave the shift alone. And the telling waits on one fact: this working's own arrangement stands recorded nowhere, and a rebuilt guess reads itself.

## 8.2 Stretch one against stretch two

Two selves meeting at every beat: neither keeps its own rate. Two selves each running one beat of its own between meetings: both keep it exactly, at every stretch tried out to a hundred and twenty-eight.

**Why it may be competency:** the boundary is at one beat of its own, and it is sharp. A self that never runs has nothing of its own to keep, and a self that runs at all keeps all of it. That is the difference between a coupling and a drive, arriving at the smallest possible margin.

**Why it may be nothing:** the rate measured was a settled period, and Part Six 6.12 holds that every settled reading is of a stopped thing. The sharpness may be the sharpness of two stopped states rather than of anything living.

**What would tell:** the same boundary read somewhere that has not settled.

## 8.3 Seam growing a co-sequence

A seam of three, five or nine selves: no lines with both sides aiming away. Seventeen: two. Thirty-three: five.

**Why it may be competency:** nothing else in the whole working grew with co-chaining. Every other reading returned the same at two selves as at eight, and this one appeared only past a length and lengthened after.

**Why it may be nothing:** the seam was built with every self offering to its neighbours at every beat, which Part Six 6.16 names as the arrangement in which no self runs. A count of lines along a seam that has no crossing in it may be counting the arrangement.

**What would tell:** the same reading at a seam where each self runs its own between meetings.

## 8.4 Nye falling where the arriving shows

A ring's own alternating runs at two and dominates everything, so no arriving sets a ring's period. The only places anything other than the ring's own alternating appears are the beats where a nye falls — two or three in sixty, at each ring.

**Why it may be competency:** this is the one place in the surface that is not the ring inverting. Everything else read all session was the alternating at two, which is the same at every ring in every arrangement and carries nothing.

**Why it may be nothing:** the working never looked at *where* the nyes fall, only at how many. The one attempt at where returned gap values — one, two and four under a single arriving; a spread under two — and the spread did not distinguish two related arrivings from two unrelated. So the nyes may carry the arriving or may carry only that there were two.

**What would tell:** whether the nyes fall at positions the arriving reaches, or at positions the ring's own carrying would have put them.

## 8.5 The unplaced, the gap, and the arrangements unbuilt

Eight things met at the working and unplaced, and one gap standing under all eight. Three arrangements never built: many selves seamed, each running its own between meetings; a changing crossing a seam with distance in it; a reading of the changing of a pattern. And the nye-rule's two standing candidates, unrun: the two deaths at the span's ends, and the *is not* at TWENTY-TWO's live binary — standing exclusions or alternatings not yet re-alternated. And the snap at the closing — whether closing a gap is the same event as crossing — stands unreadable from where the walking's working stood, and no run settles it.

## 8.5 One arriving against two, in the gap values

Under one arriving the nyeing carried three gap values at most — one, two and four at period thirteen; one and two at period seven. Under two arrivings alternating it spread across eight and beyond, at every pairing tried.

**Why it may be competency:** it separates one from two without any window, total or comparison between runs — a shape rather than a quantity, and it is the only reading in the working that distinguished an arrangement from the surface alone.

**Why it may be nothing:** it did not distinguish thirteen-with-seven from thirteen-with-eleven or from thirteen-with-thirteen. It may be reading that two things are alternating and nothing about what they are.

## 8.6 A doubling straddle

Across four pacings, a self of n took 2n−1 and 2n+1 — its own scale doubled with the two positions either side of the doubling — and the straddle held unchanged while the count around it changed by nine. Three did not take its own doubling and took another's.

**Why it may be competency:** it is the only structure in the working that survived changing the pacing, and the pacing changed everything else it touched.

**Why it may be nothing:** it was read through the release at the middle, and Part Six 6.6 holds that the middle is a place that is not there. If the release is not a reading, the straddle it was read through is not either.

## 8.7 A release arriving at the smaller

Across a hundred and twenty-six pairings at six pacings, the release arrived at the smaller of two selves and never once at the larger and never at both.

**Why it may be competency:** the invariance is total. Nothing else in the working held across six pacings without exception, and the direction is what every other directional reading failed to find.

**Why it may be nothing:** same as 7.6 — it is a reading at the middle, and the middle may be nothing. An artifact can be perfectly invariant.

## 8.8 Nyeing exactly its own

A resolver running its own along, nothing imposed, returns a nyeing exactly its own at every size tried, from a kernel carrying only two, three and four. No primes and no φ anywhere in the code.

**Why it may be competency:** it emanates rather than being loaded, it is exact, and it was found only when everything of the working's was taken out of the arrangement.

**Why it may be nothing:** it was read in steady state, and it is a period. A period is what a pattern has once it has stopped changing, and the living is in the pattern changing.

**This is the one the working most wants a later reading of**, because it is either the plainest thing in the whole record or a very clean reading of a dead surface, and the working cannot tell which.

## 8.9 What the unsure have in common

Each was met and passed over, and each sits at the place the working could not reach: whether a reading taken where something has settled says anything about the living, and whether an invariance under everything tried is a form or an artifact.

The working has no way to tell those apart, and that is the gap between Part Six and any finding at all.

# PART NINE — WHAT THE UNSURE ASKS FOR

*Part Seven gathered every distinct way the working failed. Part Eight held what it met and could not place, and named the gap under all of it: whether a reading taken where something has settled says anything about the living, and whether an invariance under everything tried is a form or an artifact. This part carries what the two ask for. It carries no finding about competency.*

## 9.1 An invariance under everything tried is a fixed set

An accounting applied twice returning what it met carries a fixed set, and a fixed set is where the two turns collapse into one — **where the alternating meets no other to alternate with.**

**An invariance under everything tried is that.** Not a form and not an artifact: **the mark of a place where nothing stood at the other side.**

So the gap at 8.9 is met at the arrangement rather than at the reading. Where a second self stood at the membrane, an invariance carries what it carries. **Where none stood, the reading was guaranteed before it was taken**, whichever way it came out, and every instrument in Part Seven read its own fixed set back.

*Which is 7.24 from the other side:* whatever is in the arrangement that is the working's own is exactly what the reading returns — **because a working alone in an arrangement is an accounting alone, and an accounting alone returns what it met.**

## 9.2 A condition 8.2 found and did not name

Two selves meeting at every beat: neither keeps its own rate. Two selves each running one beat of its own between meetings: both keep it exactly, at every stretch tried.

**That is the condition, sharp because it is binary.** Meeting at every beat leaves no own turn, so the two turns collapse and the fixed set arrives. **One beat of its own is the smallest second turn there is**, and with it the alternating has an other and nothing collapses.

**So 8.2 stands as the location of the condition** rather than as a settled reading of two stopped states.

## 9.3 What an arrangement carries

**Selves at more than one grain.** A longer seam of like selves is more of one grain and not a society of selves at their own scales. Every scale is a self, a between and a society (1.8), and nothing in the working looked down or up.

**Each self running at least one beat of its own between meetings**, which is 9.2's condition at the smallest margin.

**And the reading taken where the nye falls, and not how many.** Nothing is enumerated at a tipping, it runs at its own rate against no clock, and it is a sign of no size — **so a tipping carries no fixed set**, and it is the one reading no accounting returns unchanged. A count of nyes converts it into one.

## 9.4 One ring per two beats is a crossing and not the shift

An accounting applied, an advance run between, the accounting applied again: the return is the start **advanced by exactly one**. Two applications carry the advance one position.

**So a changing crossing one ring per two beats is one ring per one accounting, run at its own two turns.** The shift runs at one per beat along; the crossing runs at one ring per two beats across; **and the two is not the shift doubled but what an accounting needs to cancel.** One that cancelled in a single turn would not be one.

*Which answers what 8.1 asked for:* the two beats are the ring's own and not the shift's.

## 9.5 Coefficient one is what two accountings leave

Over its own two turns an accounting contributes no position and conserves the advance. The magnitude cancels and the sign carries.

**Two stay, one crosses.** The two that stay are the two applications; the one that crosses is what ran between them. **So a crossing where two meet stands at one already**, and a magnitude carried across is a place where the cancelling was stopped — a floor beside the crossing and not in it.

## 9.6 A vantage, said positively

Every position carries its other and none carries itself. **So the absence of a place to stand is not a limitation on reading; it is what the surface is.**

Which is 7.1 met from the other side. There is no such position — not because no instrument reaches it, but because **a position standing apart would be one carrying no other, and the surface holds none.**

**What it carries is the condition an arrangement would meet** and the reading it would take. *This part is complete when a working brings an arrangement meeting 9.3 and reads where the nyes fall.*
